\problem{Number track 8--13 with one empty box (between 10 and 12).}

\textbf{Solution:} Read the number just before the empty box: 10. Counting on,
one more than 10 is 11 --- and 11 comes right before 12, so it fits. \ans{11}

\problem{Number track 45--50 with two empty boxes.}

\textbf{Solution:} One more than 46 is \ans{47}

One more than 48 is \ans{49} \quad Check each answer against the number that
follows it: 47 comes before 48, and 49 comes before 50. Both fit.

\problem{Find and fix: Mia counted 18, 19, 21, 22 and left one number out.}

\textbf{Solution:} Whisper-count and match: ``18, 19, \emph{20}, 21, 22.''
Mia jumped from 19 straight to 21, so the missing number is \ans{20}

\textbf{What Mia should remember (instructor-judged):} every number gets said
in order --- after 19 comes 20, then 21. Accept any wording that names the
skipped number or says ``don't skip any numbers.''

\problem{Find and fix: Leo counted 38, 39, 30 --- one number is wrong.}

\textbf{Solution:} The X goes on 30. After a number ending in 9 a \emph{new}
tens group starts: one more than 39 is \ans{40}

\textbf{What went wrong (instructor-judged):} Leo slid back to the start of
the thirties instead of starting the forties. Accept any wording that says the
forties begin after 39 (``after 39 comes 40, not 30'').

\problem{Three mixed-up cards: 54, 52, 53 --- write them in counting order.}

\textbf{Solution:} Whisper-count from 51: ``51, 52, 53, 54.'' The card said
first is least. In counting order the cards read \ans{52,\ 53,\ 54}

\problem{Challenge: hundred-chart row 91--100 with two empty boxes and 69 in
the wrong spot.}

\textbf{Solution:} One more than 92 is \ans{93}

One more than 97 is \ans{98}

The box after 95 must hold one more than 95, which is \ans{96} \quad (69 shows
the same two digits in reversed order --- an easy mix-up to spot.)

\textbf{Why 69 cannot be there (instructor-judged):} counting on from 95 gives
96, not 69 --- and 69 is much smaller than 95, so it belongs far earlier in
the count. Accept either the counting-on reason or the size reason.
