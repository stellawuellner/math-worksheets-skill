Three PDFs are ready for an AP Calculus AB/BC student on evaluating algebraic
limits by factoring and rationalizing:

- **Worksheet** — 8 problems ramping 1→5. Problem 1 pairs a numerical table with
  the algebra so the limit is predicted before it is proved; 2–3 are factoring
  (including a difference of cubes), 4–6 are conjugate/rationalizing with one
  sign trap, 7 patches a removable discontinuity, 8 asks for the reasoning.
- **Answer key** — full worked steps, with problem 8 marked for manual review
  and an explicit note on what to accept and what not to.
- **Study guide** — three skills, each a rule, a worked example and a try-it.

7 of the 8 answers were machine-verified with SymPy. Problem 8 is a written
explanation and is labelled `manual` rather than claimed as verified — the build
exits 2 to make that visible instead of silent.
