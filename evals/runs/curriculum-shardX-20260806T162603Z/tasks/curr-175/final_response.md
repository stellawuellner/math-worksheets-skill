# Equivalent Expressions: Expanded & Factored Forms (Grades 6-7)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_forms_curr175.pdf`) — 12 problems. A short blocked warm-up
  (two expansions, one factorization) is followed by an interleaved mix of
  expanding, factoring, and combining like terms, so your child must read each
  expression and choose the rewrite rather than repeat one move. It includes an
  equivalence test by substitution, a banner-area application answered in
  centimeters, and finishes with a synthesis challenge: expand and simplify an
  expression, then factor the result.
- **Answer key** (`ak_forms_curr175.pdf`) — step-by-step reasoning for every
  problem (which rewrite was chosen and why), a quick-answers column, and the
  generated curriculum section with grade band and standard (7.EE.A). The
  banner-width answer prints its verified cm unit.
- **Study guide** (`ss_forms_curr175.pdf`) — a two-page card with one section
  per subskill: expanding, combining like terms, factoring out the GCF, and
  mixed rewrites (expand first, then combine). Each has a rule box, a worked
  example opening with its strategy, and a try-it with the answer upside down.

**What is verified:** 13 of the 14 responses the worksheet asks for are
machine-checked with a computer algebra system (every expansion, factorization,
simplification, and both substitution values in problem 9, plus all 8
study-guide examples/try-its). One response is instructor-judged: 9(c), the
sentence explaining why the two expressions are equivalent for every x — the
answer key states that credit requires pointing at the expansion, not just the
matching test value. The key's "What is verified" note reflects the same split.
