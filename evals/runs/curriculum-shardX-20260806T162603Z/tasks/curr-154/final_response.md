Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 ratio error-analysis set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_ratio_errors_curr154.pdf`) — 8 problems, each presenting
  work with a possible ratio mistake to diagnose: a reversed milk-to-oats
  ratio, an equivalence claim to test (that one is actually correct — students
  must notice), a reversed speed calculation, two broken ratio tables to find
  and fix, a flipped proportion, a false equivalence built by adding instead
  of multiplying, and an unfair rate comparison. Four are find-and-fix items.
- **Answer key** (`ak_ratio_errors_curr154.pdf`) — quick-answers table,
  generated curriculum section (6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3, difficulty 1–4), worked
  solutions, and a "Common wrong answers" note for every planted mistake
  (each planted wrong result is declared as a machine-checked misconception
  trap, so the sheet is proven to distinguish the error from the right
  answer).
- **Study guide** (`ss_ratio_errors_curr154.pdf`) — three sections: catching a
  reversed ratio, testing equivalence (multiply, never add), and fixing a
  broken ratio table, each with rule, worked example, and try-it.

**What is verified:** every numeric correction on the sheet — 9 of the 15
responses — is machine-checked with SymPy, and all 7 declared traps are
machine-proven to give a visibly different (wrong) value. The other 6
responses are the written diagnoses ("name the mistake", "explain the test"),
which only an instructor can judge; each is flagged for manual review in the
key, with a rubric stating what a full-credit answer must contain. All six
study-guide answers are machine-checked.
