## What the worksheet covers

Difficulty ramps 1 → 4. The four interleaved skills are: reading zeros straight
from factored form; factoring first and then reading the zeros; using
multiplicity to decide whether the curve crosses or turns; and building a
polynomial from stated zeros. Five problems are set in a real context with the
units stated (break-even prices in dollars, a tray volume in cubic centimetres,
a start-up cost in dollars, a berm height in metres); the rest are direct
symbolic work. Problem 9 includes a grid for sketching the graph.
