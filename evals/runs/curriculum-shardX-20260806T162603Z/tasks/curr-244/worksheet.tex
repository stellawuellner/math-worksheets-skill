\vspace{0.05cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item The theorem is $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, where $c$ is always the \textbf{hypotenuse} --- the side opposite the right angle, and the longest side.
  \item To find the hypotenuse, \textbf{add} the squares of the legs. To find a leg, \textbf{subtract} the known leg's square from the hypotenuse's square. Decide which one you need before you write anything.
  \item Where a student's work is shown, correct it \textit{and} say what went wrong.
  \item Round any answer that is not exact to two decimal places.
\end{itemize}
