# Unlike Fractions — Grades 4–5 practice set

Three PDFs are ready:

- **`ss_fractions_curr127.pdf` — Skills Summary (2 pages).** The reference card to
  read first. Three sections, each with a rule box, a worked example, and a "try it"
  whose answer is printed upside down inside the box: (1) rewriting a fraction with
  a new denominator (multiply top and bottom by the same factor), (2) adding and
  subtracting unlike fractions over a common denominator, and (3) mixed numbers with
  unlike denominators, including when to regroup. A watch-out box names the classic
  error — *adding* to the denominator instead of multiplying.
- **`ws_fractions_curr127.pdf` — Student worksheet (5 pages), 12 problems.** Every
  problem on the sheet turns on finding a common denominator. It starts with two
  missing-numerator warm-ups (`1/2 = ?/8`), moves to rewriting a pair of fractions
  over a given common denominator, then interleaves adding, subtracting, and finding
  the least common denominator so the student has to decide what the situation needs
  rather than repeat one recipe. It finishes with two mixed-number problems (one
  requiring regrouping) and a recipe word problem. Difficulty ramps 1 → 4; each
  problem has 4–6 cm of work space and its own answer line.
- **`ak_fractions_curr127.pdf` — Answer key (3 pages).** Each problem is restated
  with the reasoning written out: how the common denominator was chosen, both
  rewritten fractions, and the simplification. It opens with a quick-answer bank for
  fast grading and a generated Curriculum section listing the standards (4.NF.A for
  the equivalence items, 5.NF.A.1 for the addition and subtraction) and the
  difficulty range.

**Verification.** All 14 machine checks were recomputed independently with SymPy and
