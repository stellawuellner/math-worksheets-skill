# How Many? Counting Practice (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **ws_howmany_curr002.pdf** — a 10-problem fluency worksheet on one-to-one
  counting and stating how many objects are in a set. The ramp starts with
  short dot rows, moves through ten-frames and arrays, and finishes with a
  20-dot challenge array. No two problems share an identical skeleton: rows,
  arrays, and ten-frames rotate so the child keeps re-choosing how to count.
- **ak_howmany_curr002.pdf** — the step-by-step answer key. Each solution
  models the counting reasoning (one touch per dot, "the top row is 5, count
  on"), with a quick-answers bank for fast grading and a generated Curriculum
  section (K.CC.B.5, difficulty 1–4).
- **ss_howmany_curr002.pdf** — a one-page study guide with three sections:
  counting a row one-to-one, counting an array row by row, and counting on a
  ten-frame. Each has a rule box, a worked example, and a try-it with the
  answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** 10 of the 11 responses are machine-checked — every
"How many?" count on the sheet has its own SymPy-verified entry (dot rows are
checked as an object count from the same data that draws them; arrays and
ten-frames as the row sums the child counts on through).
**One item is instructor-judged:** the challenge asks the child to tell how
they counted, and the key gives the rubric (any genuine one-to-one strategy
counts; a bare guess does not). The study guide's examples and try-its are
machine-verified the same way.
