\textit{Zeros from factored form, factoring to find zeros, multiplicity and graph behavior, and building a polynomial from its zeros}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Show the work that produces each answer, then write the final answer on the blank provided.
  \item ``Multiplicity'' means the exponent on a factor: in $(x-r)^m$ the zero $x=r$ has multiplicity $m$.
  \item A zero of odd multiplicity is a place where the curve passes through the $x$-axis; a zero of even multiplicity is a place where the curve meets the axis and turns back.
  \item Every model in this set states its units. Answers to modelling questions should be given in those units.
\end{itemize}
