# Synthesis Review: Rates, Extrema, and the Shape of a Graph — three PDFs

**Course:** AP Calculus AB/BC · **Topic:** derivative applications and
modelling, focused on solving related-rates, optimization, and curve-analysis
syntheses · **10 problems**

## What you get

- **`ws_synthesis_curr470.pdf` — the student worksheet (6 pages).**
  Problems 1–3 are a short warm-up, one problem per method: a related rate, an
  optimization, a curve analysis. From problem 4 on the three are shuffled so
  no two consecutive problems use the same method — the student has to *choose*
  the tool before writing anything, which is the whole point of an interleaved
  review. Problem 10 is the synthesis finish: an inflating balloon where the
  answer to (c) requires combining both rates into a single relationship.
- **`ak_synthesis_curr470.pdf` — the full answer key (5 pages).**
  Every problem restated and worked, with the setup shown before the
  arithmetic. It opens with a generated Quick Answers bank, a "What is
  verified" note, and a Curriculum block listing the AP codes against the
  problems that carry them.
- **`ss_synthesis_curr470.pdf` — the study guide (2 pages).**
  One section per method, and each formula box opens with the **tell** — the
  phrase in a problem that signals this is the method to reach for. That is the
  skill an interleaved sheet actually tests. Each section then has a worked
  example and a try-it with its answer printed upside down.

## Verification, stated honestly

The ten problems ask for **22 responses**.

- **19 of 22 are machine-checked** with SymPy: every derivative recomputed,
  every equation re-solved, every numeric rate recomputed, and the two
  "rewrite in one variable" steps (problems 4a and 6a) checked as algebraic
  identities rather than by eye.
- **3 of 22 are instructor-judged**: problems 5(c), 7(b) and 10(c), the three
  parts that ask for a justification rather than a value. They print as `---`
  in the Quick Answers bank, and each has a matching Solution paragraph in the
  key stating what a correct argument must contain — including what does *not*
  earn credit (a graph sketch with no sign reasoning; naming the larger number
  without the comparison).
- **1 misconception trap** is declared and machine-checked on problem 8: a
  student who adds the two speeds gets 140 instead of 100, and the key's
  "Common wrong answers" line says so.

The study guide's six results are all machine-checked.

## Interleaving

The verifier reports the facet mix directly: four related-rates problems, three
optimization, three curve-analysis, with a **maximum same-method run of 1**
after the warm-up. Nothing on this sheet lets a student coast on the method
they used a moment ago.

## Suggested order

Study guide first, then the worksheet, then the answer key.
