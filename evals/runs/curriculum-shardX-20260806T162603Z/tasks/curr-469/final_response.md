# Related Rates and Optimization: Finding the Setup Error — three PDFs

**Course:** AP Calculus AB/BC · **Topic:** derivative applications and
modelling, focused on diagnosing related-rates and optimization *setup* errors ·
**8 problems**

## What you get

- **`ws_rr_optim_errors_curr469.pdf` — the student worksheet (4 pages).**
  One correct warm-up (the spherical balloon), then seven problems that each
  show a student's written work and ask what went wrong and what the right
  answer is. Six are full find-and-fix items — the task asked for at least
  two. The planted errors are the classic ones: a missing chain-rule factor,
  setting the area rather than its derivative to zero, a dropped minus sign on
  a falling ladder, cutting $x$ instead of $2x$ off a box edge, ignoring the
  stated domain, the wrong similar-triangle proportion, and a halved lateral
  surface area.
- **`ak_rr_optim_errors_curr469.pdf` — the full answer key (4 pages).**
  Every problem is worked, not just answered, and each judged part says
  explicitly what earns credit and what does not. The generated Quick Answers
  bank also carries a **"Common wrong answers"** line per planted error, so
  when you see 48, or 314.16, or 1.6, or 4.30 on a page you know immediately
  which misconception produced it.
- **`ss_rr_optim_errors_curr469.pdf` — the study guide (2 pages).**
  Three sections — setting up a related-rates problem, building an
  optimization model, and justifying that you actually found the extremum —
  each with a rule box, a worked example that opens by naming the strategy,
  and a try-it with its answer printed upside down.

## Verification, stated honestly

The eight problems ask for **18 responses**, and the split is deliberate on an
error-analysis sheet:

- **10 of 18 are machine-checked** with SymPy: every corrected value, every
  critical-value list, and the corrected surface-area function (checked as an
  algebraic identity, not just numerically).
- **8 of 18 are instructor-judged** — the "name the error / describe what went
  wrong" halves of problems 2 through 8. These print as `---` in the Quick
  Answers bank, and the key's matching Solution paragraph is the rubric: it
  states what a correct diagnosis must say and, where it matters, what does
  *not* earn credit.
- **6 misconception traps are declared and machine-checked.** The verifier
  confirms each planted wrong answer is genuinely rejected by that problem's
  own check, so no problem is asking a student to distinguish two answers that
  are actually the same number.

The study guide's six results are all machine-checked.

## Note on the diagnosis halves

The written diagnosis is the point of this sheet, so it is not something to
skip when grading. A student who produces the corrected number without naming
the error has done the arithmetic and missed the lesson.

## Suggested order

Study guide first, then the worksheet, then the answer key.
