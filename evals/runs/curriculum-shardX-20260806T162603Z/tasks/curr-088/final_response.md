# Mass and Liquid Volume: One-Step Problems (Grade 3) — what you received

Three PDFs:

- **Student worksheet** (`ws_massvol_curr088.pdf`) — 10 one-step word problems
  on mass (g, kg) and liquid volume (mL, L), per 3.MD. Every stem states its
  givens and units explicitly, with no extra story detail. The four operations
  are interleaved after the warm-up so your child must decide each time:
  put together, take away, equal groups, or equal shares. Two problems use
  realistic representations (a level pan balance, a measuring jug), and every
  answer line names the unit the answer must carry.
- **Step-by-step answer key** (`ak_massvol_curr088.pdf`) — for each problem, a
  sentence naming which operation the situation calls for and why, the
  arithmetic, and the boxed answer with its unit, plus a quick-answers bank
  for fast grading.
- **Study guide** (`ss_massvol_curr088.pdf`) — four short sections (add,
  subtract, multiply, divide), each with the decision rule, one worked
  example, and a fresh try-it problem with its answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** all 10 worksheet answers are machine-checked — SymPy
recomputed every sum, difference, product, and quotient from the printed
givens, and the unit gate confirmed each answer line and each boxed answer
carries the declared unit. Nothing on this sheet needs manual review: there
are no open-response items, and the key's own "What is verified" note is
silent because every problem is covered. The 8 study-guide examples and
try-its are machine-verified the same way.
