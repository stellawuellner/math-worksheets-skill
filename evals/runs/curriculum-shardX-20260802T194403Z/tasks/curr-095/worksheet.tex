\wstitleblock{Composite Rectilinear Figures: Area and Perimeter}{Grade 3 --- Measurement and Data}{}

\noindent\small\textbf{Skills practised:} Adding rectangles, subtracting a corner, going around the
outside, and finding a missing side. \emph{Every shape here is made only of rectangles, and every
corner is a square corner.} Read each question carefully --- some ask for \textbf{area} (how much
space is covered, in square units), some ask for \textbf{perimeter} (how far it is all the way
around), and some ask for a \textbf{missing side length}. Show your work in the space beside each
figure.\normalsize

one straight line, find the area of each rectangle, then add. What is the \textbf{area} of the
whole sticker?

  \draw[thick] (0,0) -- (8,0) -- (8,3) -- (4,3) -- (4,6) -- (0,6) -- cycle;
  \node[below] at (4,0) {$8$};
  \node[right] at (8,1.5) {$3$};
