# Numerals, Number Words, and Quantities (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **ws_matching_curr003.pdf** — an 8-problem representations workshop on
  matching numerals, number words, and represented quantities. Every problem
  states its givens as an explicit representation — dot rows, ten-frames,
  numeral cards, word cards — and asks the child to translate between them,
  ending with a challenge that puts all three forms side by side and orders
  them.
- **ak_matching_curr003.pdf** — the step-by-step answer key, with the counting
  or matching reasoning written out for each item, a quick-answers bank, and a
  generated Curriculum section (K.CC.A.3, K.CC.B.5, K.CC.C.7).
- **ss_matching_curr003.pdf** — a one-page study guide: dots-to-numeral,
  number words (with a word-numeral chart for one through ten), and
  numeral-to-quantity on a ten-frame, each with a rule, worked example, and
  try-it.

**What is verified:** 10 of the 12 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every written numeral, the comparison symbol, and the least-to-greatest
ordering each has its own labeled entry (word-card matches are checked against
the word-to-numeral data that defines them). **Two items are
instructor-judged**, and the key says exactly what to accept: drawing eight
dots for the numeral card (count the drawn dots), and writing the number word
"seven" (invented spelling that clearly reads as seven is fine). The study
guide's examples and try-its are machine-verified the same way.
