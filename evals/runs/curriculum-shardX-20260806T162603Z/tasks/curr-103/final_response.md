# Rounding to Named Places — Grades 4–5

Here are the three PDFs for this representations-and-applications set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_rounding_curr103.pdf`) — 10 problems on rounding
  multi-digit numbers to named places. The first two problems use labeled
  number-line models (with the midpoint marked); every later problem carries a
  blank number line your child may label and use if it helps. The set covers
  the digit rule at the ten, hundred, and thousand, two realistic applications
  with units stated (stadium tickets, a fundraiser total in dollars), the
  boundary case 49,850 → 50,000 where the carry changes several digits, and a
  final Kai-vs-Jo item where the canonical wrong answer (3,900) is planted and
  must be diagnosed.
- **Answer key** (`ak_rounding_curr103.pdf`) — quick-answers bank, then
  step-by-step solutions naming the underlined digit and the look-right digit
  for every problem. The Kai/Jo item's planted error is declared as a
  machine-checked misconception trap, so the key also prints a "common wrong
  answers" note (if they got 3,900: rounded down despite the tens digit 5). A
  generated Curriculum section lists 4.NBT.A.3 and the difficulty range.
- **Study guide** (`ss_rounding_curr103.pdf`) — three sections: rounding on a
  number line, the digit rule, and rounding in real contexts, each with a rule
  box, a worked example, and a try-it, plus a watch-out box on carrying
  (3,997 → 4,000).

**What is verified:** 13 of the 14 answers are machine-checked — every rounded
value on the sheet is recomputed independently, including all three blanks of
problem 9 and the trap's planted wrong value. One response is
instructor-judged: problem 10's written explanation of who is correct and why,
flagged for manual review with a full-credit rubric in the key. The key's
"What is verified" note reflects the same split.
