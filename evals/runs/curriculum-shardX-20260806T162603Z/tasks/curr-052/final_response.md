# Numbers Three Ways — Grade 2 place value

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_numforms_curr052.pdf`) — 10 fluency problems on standard, word,
  and expanded form. The directions show one worked pattern (234 all three ways),
  then the sheet ramps from two-digit expanded form to zero-placeholder numbers
  (705, nine hundred seven), scrambled expanded form (9 + 800 + 30), and a
  missing-part challenge (800 + __ + 2 = 862) with an explanation. No two problems
  share an identical skeleton, and the three forms are interleaved after the warm-up.
- **Answer key** (`ak_numforms_curr052.pdf`) — reasoning for every problem, a
  quick-answers bank, "common wrong answers" notes for the three declared
  misconception traps (750 for 705's expanded form, 983 for the scrambled parts,
  97 for the dropped zero), and the curriculum section (2.NBT.A).
- **Study guide** (`ss_numforms_curr052.pdf`) — one section per form with a rule
  box, worked example, try-it, and a watch-out on the zero placeholder.

**Verification split:** 9 of 12 responses are machine-verified with SymPy — every
standard-form and expanded-form answer (expanded forms are checked as identities,
e.g. 500 + 70 + 3 = 573) plus the missing-part challenge; the three traps are
proven distinguishable. **3 responses are instructor-judged**: the two word-form
answers (problems 4 and 7) and the challenge explanation — the key prints the model
wording and what a correct explanation must contain, and its "What is verified"
note marks the same three. All 6 study-guide examples/try-its are machine-verified.
