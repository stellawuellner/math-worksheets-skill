\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $x + 4 < 9$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} The variable has $4$ added to it, so undo the addition:
subtract $4$ from both sides. Subtraction never moves the inequality sign.

\textbf{Step 2.} $x + 4 - 4 < 9 - 4$, which leaves $x < 5$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: the sign is strict, so an \emph{open} circle at $5$,
shaded to the left. Solution: $\ans{x < 5}$

\textit{Check:} $x = 4$ gives $8 < 9$ (true); $x = 5$ gives $9 < 9$ (false), so
$5$ itself is correctly excluded.}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $n - 6 \ge 2$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} Six is subtracted from $n$, so add $6$ to both sides.

\textbf{Step 2.} $n - 6 + 6 \ge 2 + 6$, which leaves $n \ge 8$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: $\ge$ includes the boundary, so a \emph{solid} dot at
$8$, shaded to the right. Solution: $\ans{n \ge 8}$

\textit{Common wrong answer:} $n \ge -4$ --- subtracting $6$ instead of adding
it. Undo the operation you see, do not repeat it.}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $x + 7 \le 2$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} Subtract $7$ from both sides to isolate $x$.

\textbf{Step 2.} $x \le 2 - 7$, and $2 - 7 = -5$, so $x \le -5$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: solid dot at $-5$, shaded to the left. Solution:
$\ans{x \le -5}$

\textit{Listen for:} a negative boundary is not a reason to flip anything. The
sign only moves when you multiply or divide by a negative.}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $4x > 20$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} The variable is multiplied by $4$, so divide both sides by
$4$. Four is positive, so the sign stays as it is.

\textbf{Step 2.} $\dfrac{4x}{4} > \dfrac{20}{4}$, which leaves $x > 5$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: open circle at $5$, shaded to the right. Solution:
$\ans{x > 5}$}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $\dfrac{m}{3} \le 2$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} The variable is divided by $3$, so multiply both sides by $3$
--- again a positive number, so no flip.

\textbf{Step 2.} $3 \cdot \dfrac{m}{3} \le 3 \cdot 2$, which leaves $m \le 6$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: solid dot at $6$, shaded to the left. Solution:
$\ans{m \le 6}$

\textit{Common wrong answer:} $m \le \frac{2}{3}$ --- dividing by $3$ again
instead of undoing the division.}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $-2x > 8$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} The coefficient is $-2$, so isolating $x$ means dividing both
sides by a negative number --- and that reverses the inequality sign.

\textbf{Step 2.} $\dfrac{-2x}{-2} \;\text{and}\; \dfrac{8}{-2}$ give $x$ and
$-4$, and the $>$ becomes $<$: $x < -4$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: open circle at $-4$, shaded to the left. Solution:
$\ans{x < -4}$

\textit{Common wrong answer:} $x > -4$ --- the sign was not flipped. Test
$x = 0$: $-2(0) = 0$, and $0 > 8$ is false, so $0$ must \emph{not} be in the
solution --- which rules the un-flipped answer out immediately.}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $y + 9 > 4$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} Subtract $9$ from both sides.

\textbf{Step 2.} $y > 4 - 9$, and $4 - 9 = -5$, so $y > -5$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: open circle at $-5$, shaded to the right. Solution:
$\ans{y > -5}$}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $6w \le -18$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} The coefficient is $6$, a positive number, so divide both
sides by $6$ and leave the sign alone. The $-18$ on the right is not what
decides this.

\textbf{Step 2.} $w \le \dfrac{-18}{6} = -3$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: solid dot at $-3$, shaded to the left. Solution:
$\ans{w \le -3}$

\textit{Common wrong answer:} $w \ge -3$ --- flipped because a negative number
appeared somewhere. The rule keys on what you divide \emph{by}, not on the sign
of the answer.}

\problem{\textbf{Solve and graph $-\dfrac{x}{3} < 2$.}

\textbf{Step 1.} The left side is $x$ divided by $-3$, so undo it by
multiplying both sides by $-3$ --- a negative multiplier, so the sign reverses.

\textbf{Step 2.} $x$ on the left, $2 \cdot (-3) = -6$ on the right, and $<$
becomes $>$: $x > -6$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: open circle at $-6$, shaded to the right. Solution:
$\ans{x > -6}$

\textit{Check:} $x = 0$ gives $0 < 2$ (true), and $0$ does lie to the right of
$-6$, so the flipped direction is the right one.}

\problem{\textbf{Chess club: at least $25$ members.}

\textbf{Step 1.} ``At least $25$'' means the total must be $25$ or more, so the
relation is $\ge$. The total is the $18$ current members plus the $m$ new ones:
$m + 18 \ge 25$.

\textbf{Step 2.} Subtract $18$ from both sides: $m \ge 25 - 18$, so $m \ge 7$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: solid dot at $7$, shaded to the right. Solution set:
$\ans{m \ge 7}$

\textbf{Step 4.} Part (b) asks for the smallest value that works. The boundary
is included, and a club signs up whole people, so the answer is
$\ans{m = 7}$

\textit{Listen for:} ``more than $25$'' --- ``at least'' includes $25$, which is
exactly why the dot is solid.}

\problem{\textbf{Concert tickets: at most $72$ dollars.}

\textbf{Step 1.} ``At most'' means the cost may reach the budget but not pass
it, so the relation is $\le$. The cost of $t$ tickets is $8t$: $8t \le 72$.

\textbf{Step 2.} Divide both sides by $8$ (positive, so no flip):
$t \le \dfrac{72}{8} = 9$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: solid dot at $9$, shaded to the left. Solution:
$\ans{t \le 9}$

\textit{Note for the reader:} in context $t$ also has to be a whole number that
is $0$ or more, so the practical answer is $0$ through $9$ tickets. The
inequality itself is what the problem asked for.}

\problem{\textbf{Jordan's error on $-5x \ge 20$.} \emph{Part of this problem is
an open explanation, flagged for manual review; the model wording below is what
a full-credit sentence contains.}

\textbf{Step 1.} Divide both sides by $-5$. Because $-5$ is negative, the
inequality sign must reverse at the same moment.

\textbf{Step 2.} $\dfrac{-5x}{-5} = x$ and $\dfrac{20}{-5} = -4$, and $\ge$
becomes $\le$: $x \le -4$.

\textbf{Step 3.} Graph: solid dot at $-4$, shaded to the \emph{left} --- the
opposite ray from the one Jordan drew. Correct solution:
$\ans{x \le -4}$

\textbf{Step 4 (the explanation).} Jordan divided by a negative number and kept
the sign. Multiplying or dividing by a negative reflects every number to the
other side of $0$, so the number that used to be larger is now smaller and the
order of the two sides is reversed. Concretely: $3 > 1$, but $-3 < -1$.

\textit{Full credit:} the correct solution $x \le -4$, a correctly shaded
graph, \emph{and} a sentence that says dividing by a negative reverses order.
``You forgot to flip'' names the rule but not the reason --- push for the
second half.}
