\begin{center}\small\textit{Sales tax, tips, discounts, markup, and working
backwards to the original price}\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{The percent bar model.} Every problem on this sheet is one of
these two pictures. The whole bar is the \emph{base} --- the price the percent is
taken \emph{of}. Adding tax, a tip, or a markup builds a bar longer than the
base; a discount cuts the bar shorter.

\noindent\textbf{Directions.}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Name the base first --- the price the percent is taken of --- then decide
        whether the percent is added on or taken off.
  \item Show the multiplier you use (for example $1.08$ or $0.85$) or the two
        steps you take.
  \item All prices are in dollars. Round money answers to the nearest cent and
        write the answer on the answer line.
\end{itemize}

\problem[5cm]{A jacket is priced at \$$40$. The sales tax rate is $6\%$.
How much sales tax is charged, in dollars? (Find the tax only, not the total.)}

\problem[5cm]{A family's dinner bill is \$$36$ before the tip. They want to
leave a $15\%$ tip. How much is the tip, in dollars?}

\problem[7cm]{A backpack is priced at \$$45$ and is on sale for $20\%$ off.
Fill in the ratio table, then write the sale price in dollars.

\problem[5cm]{A bike shop buys a bike from the maker for \$$80$ and marks the
price up by $35\%$ before putting it on the floor. What is the shop's selling
price, in dollars?}

\problem[6cm]{A calculator is priced at \$$24.50$ and the sales tax rate is
$7.25\%$. What is the total amount paid, in dollars, rounded to the nearest
cent?}

\problem[6cm]{A restaurant bill is \$$58.40$ before the tip. A customer leaves
an $18\%$ tip. What is the total amount the customer pays, in dollars, rounded
to the nearest cent?}

\problem[7cm]{A coat is priced at \$$72$. It is marked $25\%$ off, and then
$6\%$ sales tax is charged on the sale price. What is the total amount paid, in
dollars? Take the two steps in order --- the tax is charged on the sale price,
not on the original price.}

\problem[6cm]{A store pays \$$18.60$ for a board game and marks it up $45\%$.
What is the shelf price, in dollars, rounded to the nearest cent?}

\problem[7cm]{A sweater is on sale for \$$34$ after a $15\%$ discount. Let $x$
be the original price in dollars. Write an equation for $x$ using the sale
price, then solve it to find the original price in dollars.}

\problem[7cm]{A receipt shows a total of \$$77.04$, which already includes
$7\%$ sales tax. Let $x$ be the price before tax, in dollars. Write an equation
for $x$, then solve it to find the price before tax in dollars.}
