\problem{Warm-up: equation from the graph.

\textbf{Solution:} The line passes through the origin and the marked point
$(2, 8)$, so $k = \dfrac{8}{2} = 4$. The equation is \ans{y = 4x}}

\problem{Warm-up: unit rate for apples.

\textbf{Solution:} The line passes through $(0, 0)$ and the marked point
$(5, 10)$, so the rate is $\dfrac{10}{5}$ --- \ans{2} dollars per pound.}

\problem{Table $(2, 9)$, $(4, 18)$, $(6, 27)$: equation, then predict.

\textbf{Solution (a):} Every row gives the same ratio:
$\dfrac{9}{2} = \dfrac{18}{4} = \dfrac{27}{6} = 4.5$, so $k = 4.5$ and the
equation is \ans{y = 4.5x}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Substitute $x = 10$: $y = 4.5 \cdot 10$, so
\ans{45}}

\problem{Tour bus at $40$ km per hour: table and graph.

\textbf{Solution (a):} Distance $= 40 \times$ hours: at $1$ hour \ans{40} km;
at $2$ hours $40 \cdot 2$, giving \ans{80} km; at $3$ hours $40 \cdot 3$,
giving \ans{120} km.

\textbf{Solution (b):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} The points $(1, 40)$,
$(2, 80)$, $(3, 120)$ lie on one straight line through the origin --- the graph
should show that line.}

\problem{Rice at $y = 3.5x$ dollars for $x$ kg.

\textbf{Solution (a):} $y = 3.5 \cdot 6$, so $6$ kg costs \ans{21} dollars.

\textbf{Solution (b):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} The point $(1, 3.5)$ says
that $1$ kilogram costs $\$3.50$ --- it is the unit price, the value of $k$
read straight off the graph.}

\problem{Table $(3, 7.5)$, $(5, 12.5)$: equation, then solve.

\textbf{Solution (a):} $k = \dfrac{12.5 - 7.5}{5 - 3} = \dfrac{5}{2} = 2.5$
(or $\dfrac{7.5}{3} = 2.5$ from one row). The equation is \ans{y = 2.5x}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Solve $2.5x = 20$: $x = \dfrac{20}{2.5}$, so \ans{8}}

\problem{Hiker's graph through $(4, 6)$: equation, then distance for $10$ hours.

\textbf{Solution (a):} $k = \dfrac{6}{4} = 1.5$, so the equation is
\ans{y = 1.5x}

\textbf{Solution (b):} $y = 1.5 \cdot 10$, so the hiker walks \ans{15} km.}

\problem{Printer at $12$ pages per minute: sketch, then read off $2.5$ minutes.

\textbf{Solution (a):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} The graph is the straight
line through the origin with slope $12$ --- through $(1, 12)$, $(2, 24)$,
$(3, 36)$.

\textbf{Solution (b):} Pages $= 12 \cdot 2.5$, so \ans{30} pages print.}

\problem{Train at $y = 60x$: distance, then interpret $k$.

\textbf{Solution (a):} $y = 60 \cdot 1.5$, so the train covers \ans{90} km.

\textbf{Solution (b):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} The $60$ is the unit rate:
the train travels $60$ kilometers in each hour --- its speed.}

\problem{Soup table $(4, 3)$, $(8, 6)$ servings-to-cups: equation, then invert.

\textbf{Solution (a):} $k = \dfrac{3}{4} = 0.75$ cups per serving, so the
equation is \ans{y = 0.75x}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Solve $0.75x = 15$: $x = \dfrac{15}{0.75}$, so
\ans{20} servings.}

\problem{Plant A through $(2, 9)$ vs.\ Plant B at $y = 4x$: rate difference.

\textbf{Solution:} Plant A's rate: $\dfrac{9}{2} = 4.5$ cm per week. Plant B's
rate: $4$ cm per week. Difference: $4.5 - 4$, so Plant A grows \ans{0.5} cm
more per week.}

\problem{Challenge --- Tank A (graph through $(4, 18)$) vs.\ Tank B (table).

\textbf{Solution (a):} $k = \dfrac{18}{4} = 4.5$ liters per minute, so Tank A's
equation is \ans{y = 4.5x}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Tank B's rate from the table: $\dfrac{10}{2} = 5$
liters per minute (check: $\dfrac{30}{6} = 5$). Comparing rates:
\ans{4.5 < 5} --- Tank B fills faster.

\textbf{Solution (c):} After $8$ minutes: Tank B holds $5 \cdot 8 = 40$ liters,
Tank A holds $4.5 \cdot 8 = 36$ liters. The faster tank holds $40 - 36$, that
is \ans{4} more liters.}
