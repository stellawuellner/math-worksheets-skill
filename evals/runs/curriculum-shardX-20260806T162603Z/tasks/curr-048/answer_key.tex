\noindent The shelf, for reference --- top: circle, triangle, square,
rectangle; bottom: hexagon, small circle, tilted square.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem{(a) How many shapes on the TOP shelf? (b) Farthest LEFT on top?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Count only the top row: circle, triangle, square,
rectangle --- four shapes. \ans{4}

(b) \emph{Instructor judges:} the first shape on the left is the
\textbf{circle}.

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{(a) How many shapes on the BOTTOM shelf? (b) Below the triangle?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Count only the bottom row: hexagon, small circle,
tilted square --- three shapes. \ans{3}

(b) \emph{Instructor judges:} straight down from the triangle sits the
\textbf{small circle}.

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{(a) How many shapes BETWEEN the circle and the rectangle?
(b) NEXT TO the rectangle?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Between the two end shapes sit the triangle and the
square --- two shapes. \ans{2}

(b) \emph{Instructor judges:} the shape touching distance from the rectangle
is the \textbf{square}.

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Top shelf 4 shapes, bottom shelf 3. Write $>$, $<$, or $=$:
\ 4 \_\_ 3.}

\textbf{Solution:} Four is more than three, so \ans{4 > 3}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{(a) How many shapes NEXT TO the small circle? (b) To its RIGHT?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) The small circle has a neighbor on each side: the
hexagon on its left and the tilted square on its right --- two shapes.
\ans{2}

(b) \emph{Instructor judges:} the shape to the right is the \textbf{tilted
square} (accept ``square'').

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{The square moves from the top shelf to the bottom shelf. (a) Bottom
count then? (b) Top count then?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) The bottom shelf had 3 shapes and gains one:
$3 + 1 = 4$. \ans{4}

(b) The top shelf had 4 shapes and loses one: $4 - 1 = 3$. \ans{3}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Finish: the hexagon is \_\_ the circle.}

\textbf{Solution:} \emph{Instructor judges:} the hexagon sits on the bottom
shelf, straight down from the circle, so the sentence is completed with
\textbf{below} (accept ``under'' or ``beneath'').

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Challenge: (a) how many shapes in all? (b) Tell where the tilted
square is, with two position words.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Add the shelves: $4 + 3 = 7$ shapes. \ans{7}

(b) \emph{Instructor judges:} accept any accurate description using two
position words, such as ``it is on the BOTTOM shelf, to the RIGHT of the
small circle'' or ``it is BELOW the square and NEXT TO the small circle.''
