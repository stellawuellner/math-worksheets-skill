# Signed Rational Numbers: Subtraction & Sign Errors (Grades 6-7)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_signedsub_curr169.pdf`) — 8 problems on subtracting signed
  rational numbers, built around the sign errors students actually make:
  subtraction turned into addition, dropped double negatives, and a minus sign
  distributed to only the first term in a parenthesis. Two find-and-fix items
  (problems 4 and 7) show a fictional student's wrong work; your child names the
  error and computes the correct value. The sheet ramps from integer warm-ups to
  a fraction challenge.
- **Answer key** (`ak_signedsub_curr169.pdf`) — full step-by-step reasoning for
  every problem, a quick-answers column for fast grading, and a "common wrong
  answers" note for each planted misconception (e.g. "if they got -10: they
  treated subtracting negative 6 as subtracting 6"), so a wrong answer tells you
  which error to reteach. The grade band and standards (7.NS.A.1) print here,
  not on the student sheet.
- **Study guide** (`ss_signedsub_curr169.pdf`) — a two-page reference card with
  one section per skill tested: adding the opposite, subtracting a negative, and
  distributing a minus across parentheses. Each section has the rule, a short
  worked example, and a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** 8 of the 10 responses on the sheet are machine-checked
with a computer algebra system (every numeric/fraction answer, the comparison in
problem 5, and all 6 declared misconception traps were proven to give visibly
wrong values). The 2 explanation parts — 4(a) and 7(a), where your child writes
one sentence naming the error — are open responses, so they are flagged for
instructor judgment; the answer key states exactly what a correct sentence must
contain. The key's own "What is verified" note reflects the same split.
