\problem{Expand: \quad $3(x + 7)$}

\textbf{Solution:} Distribute the 3 to both terms: $3 \cdot x + 3 \cdot 7$. \ans{3x + 21}

\problem{Expand: \quad $5(2x + 3)$}

\textbf{Solution:} $5 \cdot 2x = 10x$ and $5 \cdot 3 = 15$. \ans{10x + 15}

\problem{Factor out the greatest common factor: \quad $4x + 12$}

\textbf{Solution:} The GCF of 4 and 12 is 4: $4x = 4 \cdot x$ and $12 = 4 \cdot 3$.
\ans{4(x + 3)}

\problem{Combine like terms: \quad $8x + 2 - 3x + 5$}

\textbf{Solution:} $x$-terms: $8x - 3x = 5x$. Constants: $2 + 5 = 7$. \ans{5x + 7}

\problem{Factor out the greatest common factor: \quad $14x + 21$}

\textbf{Solution:} The GCF of 14 and 21 is 7: $14x = 7 \cdot 2x$ and $21 = 7 \cdot 3$.
\ans{7(2x + 3)}

\problem{Expand: \quad $-4(x - 6)$}

\textbf{Solution:} Distribute $-4$, watching signs: $-4 \cdot x = -4x$ and
$-4 \cdot (-6) = +24$. \ans{-4x + 24}

\problem{Combine like terms: \quad $2x + 9 + 4x - 9$}

\textbf{Solution:} $x$-terms: $2x + 4x = 6x$. Constants: $9 - 9 = 0$, so no constant
remains. \ans{6x}

\problem{Factor out the greatest common factor: \quad $18x + 24$}

\textbf{Solution:} The GCF of 18 and 24 is 6: $18x = 6 \cdot 3x$ and $24 = 6 \cdot 4$.
\ans{6(3x + 4)}

\problem{Consider $2(3x + 1)$ and $6x + 2$. (a) evaluate the first at $x = 4$;
(b) evaluate the second at $x = 4$; (c) equivalent for every $x$?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) $2(3 \cdot 4 + 1) = 2 \cdot 13 = 26$. \ans{26}

(b) $6 \cdot 4 + 2 = 24 + 2 = 26$. \ans{26}

(c) Model answer: yes --- expanding gives $2(3x + 1) = 6x + 2$ exactly, so the two
expressions agree for \emph{every} $x$, not just at $x = 4$. (Instructor judges the
sentence; a matching test value alone is evidence, and the expansion is the proof.)

\problem{A banner has area $(12x + 30)$ square centimeters and height 6 cm. Write and
simplify an expression for its width.}

\textbf{Solution:} Width is area divided by height: $\dfrac{12x + 30}{6}$. Split the
division over both terms: $12x \div 6 = 2x$ and $30 \div 6 = 5$.
\ans{2x + 5\ \text{cm}}

\problem{Simplify completely: \quad $4(2x + 3) - 5x$}

\textbf{Solution:} Expand first: $4(2x + 3) = 8x + 12$. Then combine like terms:
$8x + 12 - 5x = (8x - 5x) + 12$. \ans{3x + 12}

\problem{\textbf{Challenge.} Consider $6(x + 2) + 3x + 6$. (a) simplest expanded
form; (b) factor the result.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Expand: $6(x + 2) = 6x + 12$. Combine like terms:
$6x + 12 + 3x + 6 = 9x + 18$. \ans{9x + 18}

(b) The GCF of 9 and 18 is 9: $9x = 9 \cdot x$ and $18 = 9 \cdot 2$. \ans{9(x + 2)}
