Three PDFs are ready for a kindergarten/grade-1 learner on counting forward from
varied starting numbers within 100:

- **Worksheet** — 8 problems. Number strips, a hundred-chart row, and paired
  ten-frames anchor the counting; problems 6-8 move to counting by tens and to
  comparing two counted results.
- **Answer key** — step-by-step, with the count said out loud for each item.
- **Study guide** — three skills (by ones, by tens, comparing), each with a rule,
  a worked example and an upside-down try-it.

All 8 worksheet answers and all 6 study-guide results were machine-verified with
SymPy; the full gate chain passed, including the per-problem binding of every
printed answer back to its verified value.
