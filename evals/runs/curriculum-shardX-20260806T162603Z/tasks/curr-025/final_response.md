# Strategy Pickers: Addition Facts (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_strategies_curr025.pdf`) — 10 mixed addition facts
  within 10 built around choosing an efficient strategy. A short blocked
  warm-up drills counting on; after that the three strategies — counting on,
  doubles/near doubles, and tens partners — are interleaved so your child
  must look at the numbers and pick a method each time. The sheet ends with
  a synthesis challenge (4 + 5): solve it with any strategy and explain the
  choice.
- **Answer key** (`ak_strategies_curr025.pdf`) — each solution names the
  efficient strategy and shows it (hops, double-plus-one, partner pair), a
  quick-answers bank, and a curriculum section (1.OA.C.6 and K.OA.A,
  difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_strategies_curr025.pdf`) — one section per strategy
  with a when-to-use-it rule, a worked example, and a fresh try-it, plus a
  watch-out about over-using slow counting.

**What is verified:** 10 of the 11 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every sum on the sheet, including the challenge. The one open response
("tell which strategy you picked and why") is flagged for instructor review;
the answer key states that any named strategy counts as long as the reason
matches what the student actually did. The key's "What is verified" note
reflects the same split.
