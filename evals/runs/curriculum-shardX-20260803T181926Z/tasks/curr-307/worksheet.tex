problem, and do not read anything off the drawing. (Not to scale.)}\normalsize

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent\textbf{How to work these problems.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
\item Decide first whether the two angles named are \textbf{congruent}
(corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior, or vertical) or
\textbf{supplementary} (a linear pair, or same-side interior). That decision is
the whole problem.
\item Congruent means \emph{set the expressions equal}. Supplementary means
\emph{add them and set the sum to 180}.
\item When a problem gives expressions in $x$, finding $x$ is only half the
job --- substitute it back to get the angle the question asked for.
\item Name the relationship you used beside each answer.
\end{itemize}
