\begin{center}
\small Right angles, parallel sides, equal sides, and the groups shapes share
\end{center}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions:} The six shapes below are the \textbf{Shape Set}. Every problem except
$5$, $8$ and $9$ uses it. Three attributes matter on this page:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a \textbf{square corner} (a right angle) --- the corner of an index card fits it exactly;
  \item a \textbf{pair of parallel sides} --- two sides that stay the same distance apart;
  \item \textbf{equal sides} --- sides of the same length.
\end{itemize}
Use a ruler or the corner of a card to check. Answer in whole numbers.

\problem[3cm]{Look at shape B in the Shape Set. How many square corners does it have?}

\problem[3cm]{Look at shape E. How many pairs of parallel sides does it have?}

\problem[3.6cm]{Sort the whole Shape Set. How many of the six shapes have four equal sides?}

\problem[3.6cm]{Shape A and shape E are both quadrilaterals, but they do not share every attribute.
How many more pairs of parallel sides does shape A have than shape E?}

\problem{A tile shop sold quadrilateral floor tiles last week: $9$ square tiles, $12$ rectangle
tiles, $4$ rhombus tiles and $5$ trapezoid tiles. All four kinds are quadrilaterals --- they all have
four straight sides. How many tiles did the shop sell in all?
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{tiles}}

\problem[3.8cm]{Count the pairs of parallel sides on each of the six shapes in the Shape Set. Most of
the shapes share the same count. What is that shared number?}

\problem{At the tile shop, the tiles with four square corners are the square tiles and the rectangle
tiles. Using last week's sales ($9$ square tiles, $12$ rectangle tiles, $4$ rhombus tiles,
$5$ trapezoid tiles), how many tiles sold had four square corners?
\par\vspace*{4.2cm}\answerline{tiles}}

\problem[4.4cm]{Still at the tile shop.
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] A trapezoid has exactly one pair of parallel sides. How many trapezoid tiles were sold?
        \quad \ansblank
  \item[(b)] Explain why the trapezoid tiles cannot join the group of tiles with four square corners.
\end{itemize}}

\problem[5cm]{Go back to the Shape Set.
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] How many of the six shapes have at least one pair of parallel sides? \quad \ansblank
