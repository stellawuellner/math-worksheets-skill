# Rounding and Ordering on the Number Line — Grade 3

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_roundorder_curr055.pdf`) — 12 problems in an interleaved
  synthesis format. Every problem carries its own number line: labeled lines
  (with a taller halfway mark) for the rounding items, blank lines for the
  ordering items and the challenge. A four-problem warm-up blocks the basic
  rounding moves, then rounding to ten, rounding to hundred, and ordering are
  interleaved so the student must choose the method; hard cases include 455
  (halfway rounds up), 604 (big numbers can round down), 695 (crossing into the
  next hundred), and 649 (the ones digit doesn't vote). It ends with a
  mystery-number synthesis challenge combining both rounding clues.
- **Answer key** (`ak_roundorder_curr055.pdf`) — number-line reasoning per problem,
  a quick-answers bank, a "common wrong answers" block from the eight declared
  rounding traps (e.g. 340 for 347, 400 for 455, 690 for 695), and the curriculum
  section (3.NBT.A.1 rounding, 2.NBT.A ordering).
- **Study guide** (`ss_roundorder_curr055.pdf`) — three sections (nearest ten,
  nearest hundred, ordering on a line), each with rule, worked example, and try-it,
  plus a watch-out on the tens-digit rule.
