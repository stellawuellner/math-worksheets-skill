Here are the three PDFs for the Grades 6–7 interleaved review of proportional
relationships across representations.

**Student worksheet (12 problems).** After a two-problem graph warm-up, the
representations interleave so the student must choose a method each time:
equations from graphs (plants, hikers, tanks), equations from tables (recipes,
soup servings), completing and plotting a table from a story rate (a tour bus,
a school printer), and interpreting equations back into context (what the point
(1, 3.5) means for rice prices; what k = 60 says about a train). Problem 12 is
the synthesis challenge: one tank given as a graph, one as a table — write the
equation, compare rates, and find the difference after 8 minutes. Every problem
carries its own graph or plotting grid.

**Answer key.** Step-by-step reasoning for all 12 problems, a quick-answers
bank, and a generated Curriculum section (standard 7.RP.A.2, difficulty range
1–5).

**Study guide (2 pages).** Four sections, one per representation move —
graph-to-equation, table-to-equation, equation-to-context, and
context-to-graph — each with a rule box, a worked example, and a try-it with
the upside-down answer.

**What is verified.** 20 answers are machine-checked with SymPy (every unit
rate, equation constant, table entry, prediction, the rate comparison, and the
challenge's three parts), plus all 8 study-guide examples/try-its. Four
responses are open and flagged for instructor review: the two drawn graphs
(problems 4b and 8a) and the two written interpretations (problems 5b and 9b).
Grading guidance for each is printed in the key.
