# Bounds, Signs, and the Variable of Integration — three PDFs

**Course:** AP Calculus AB/BC · **Topic:** definite integrals and the
Fundamental Theorem, focused on diagnosing bounds, sign, and
variable-of-integration errors · **8 problems**

## What you get

- **`ws_int_errors_curr474.pdf` — the student worksheet.**
  One correct warm-up, then seven problems that each show a student's written
  work and ask what went wrong and what the right answer is — well past the
  two find-and-fix items the task asked for. The planted errors cover all
  three named categories: reversed limits, un-converted substitution limits
  (twice, in different disguises), a dummy variable leaking into the answer, a
  dropped factor from `du`, a signed integral used where area was asked for,
  and displacement reported as total distance.
- **`ak_int_errors_curr474.pdf` — the full answer key.**
  Every problem restated and worked, with each judged part stating what earns
  credit. The generated Quick Answers bank carries a **"Common wrong answers"**
  line per planted error, so seeing 21, or 4, or 2, or 6, or 0 on a page tells
  you immediately which misconception produced it.
- **`ss_int_errors_curr474.pdf` — the study guide (2 pages).**
  Three sections matching the three error families: limit order and
  conversion; signed integral versus geometric area; substitution bookkeeping
  (integrand, differential and limits change together). Each has a rule box, a
  worked example that opens by naming the strategy, and a try-it with its
  answer upside down.

## Verification, stated honestly

The eight problems ask for **18 responses**.

- **10 of 18 are machine-checked** with SymPy: every corrected integral is
  recomputed exactly, including the two absolute-value integrals and the two
  substitution problems, and problem 5's total distance is checked as the exact
  sum of its two verified pieces rather than by eye.
- **8 of 18 are instructor-judged** — the "name the error / describe what went
  wrong" halves, plus problem 8(c), which asks for the habit that prevents the
  error. They print as `---` in the Quick Answers bank, and each has a matching
  Solution paragraph in the key that is the rubric.
- **8 misconception traps are declared and machine-checked.** The verifier
  confirms each planted wrong answer is genuinely rejected by that problem's own
  check, so no problem asks the student to distinguish two numbers that are
  actually equal.

The study guide's six results are all machine-checked.

## Suggested order

Study guide first, then the worksheet, then the answer key.
