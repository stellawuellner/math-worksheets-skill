\wstitleblock{Number Bonds to 10 --- Fixing a Family That Is Missing a Fact or Says One Twice}{Kindergarten--Grade 1}{}

\noindent\small A \textbf{number bond} shows a \textbf{whole} on top and its two
\textbf{parts} underneath. Every bond makes a \textbf{family of four facts}:
two adding facts and two take-away facts.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Some friends below wrote a family with a fact \textbf{missing}.
  \item Some friends wrote the \textbf{same fact twice} instead of the last one.
  \item Read the bond, find what went wrong, and write the fact that is really missing.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize

\problem[4cm]{\textbf{Find and fix.} Mia's bond shows the whole $9$ and one part $3$.
Mia writes $12$ in the empty circle.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (w) at (0,1.5) {$9$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (p) at (-1.4,0) {$3$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (q) at (1.4,0) {$?$};
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (p);
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (q);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
Tell a friend why $12$ cannot be a part of $9$. Then write the part that really goes
in the circle.}

\problem[4cm]{\textbf{Find and fix.} Ben's bond has parts $2$ and $7$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (w) at (0,1.5) {$9$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (p) at (-1.4,0) {$2$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (q) at (1.4,0) {$7$};
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (p);
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (q);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
Ben writes his family like this:\par
\begin{center}\large
$2 + 7 = 9$ \qquad $7 + 2 = 9$ \qquad $9 - 7 = 2$ \qquad $9 - 7 = 2$
\end{center}
Cross out the fact Ben wrote twice. Then write the take-away fact that is missing:
$9 - 2 = $ \underline{\hspace{1.2cm}}}

\problem[4cm]{\textbf{Find and fix.} Ana's bond has parts $6$ and $4$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (w) at (0,1.5) {$10$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (p) at (-1.4,0) {$6$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (q) at (1.4,0) {$4$};
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (p);
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (q);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
Ana writes her family like this:\par
\begin{center}\large
$6 + 4 = 10$ \qquad $4 + 6 = 10$ \qquad $10 - 4 = 6$ \qquad $6 + 4 = 10$
\end{center}
Ana used an adding fact where a take-away fact belongs. Cross out the repeat, then
write the missing take-away fact: $10 - 6 = $ \underline{\hspace{1.2cm}}}

\problem[4cm]{Jo's bond has parts $7$ and $3$. Jo starts the family but leaves one number blank.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (w) at (0,1.5) {$10$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (p) at (-1.4,0) {$7$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (q) at (1.4,0) {$3$};
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (p);
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (q);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
\begin{center}\large
$7 + 3 = 10$ \qquad $3 + 7 = 10$ \qquad $10 - 7 = 3$ \qquad $10 - \underline{\hspace{1.2cm}} = 7$
\end{center}
Write the number that belongs on the blank line.}

\problem[4.5cm]{\textbf{Challenge.} Sam's bond has parts $5$ and $3$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (w) at (0,1.5) {$8$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (p) at (-1.4,0) {$5$};
  \node[draw, thick, circle, minimum size=1cm, font=\large] (q) at (1.4,0) {$3$};
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (p);
  \draw[thick] (w) -- (q);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
Sam writes only two facts: $5 + 3 = 8$ and $3 + 5 = 8$. Sam says his family is
finished. Sam's family is \emph{not} finished --- both take-away facts are missing.
Write them both.\par
\vspace{0.2cm}
\quad\large $8 - 5 = $~\ansblank \qquad $8 - 3 = $~\ansblank}
