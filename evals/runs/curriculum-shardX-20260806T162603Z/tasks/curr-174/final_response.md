# Expressions: Find the Distribution & Like-Term Errors (Grades 6-7)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_exprerr_curr174.pdf`) — 8 problems built around the two
  errors this unit is about: distributing to only the first term, and combining
  unlike terms (or dropping the variable). It includes three find-and-fix items
  showing a fictional student's wrong work (Deshawn's 4(x+3) = 11, Ava's
  "the x's cancel", and Jaylen's 2(3x+4) = 6x+4); your child names the error
  and computes the correct value. Two problems teach the diagnostic move
  itself: substituting a number into both expressions to expose a fake
  equivalence.
- **Answer key** (`ak_exprerr_curr174.pdf`) — full reasoning for every problem,
  a quick-answers column, and "common wrong answers" notes generated from the
  three machine-checked misconception traps (e.g. "if they got 11: distributed
  the 4 to only the first term"). Grade band and standard (7.EE.A) print here
  only.
- **Study guide** (`ss_exprerr_curr174.pdf`) — a two-page card with three
  sections: distribute to every term, combine only like terms (the variable
  never disappears), and testing equivalence with a number — each with rule,
  worked example, try-it, and watch-out boxes naming the classic error.

**What is verified:** 9 of the 13 responses on the sheet are machine-checked
with a computer algebra system (every numeric value and symbolic
simplification, plus all 3 planted wrong results, which were proven visibly
different from the correct answers). The 4 written parts — 4(c), 5(a), 6(a),
and 8(c), where your child names or explains an error — are open responses
flagged for instructor judgment; the answer key states for each what a correct
sentence must contain. The key's "What is verified" note reflects this split.
