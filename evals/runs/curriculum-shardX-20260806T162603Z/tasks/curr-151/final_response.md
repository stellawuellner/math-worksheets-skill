Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 ratios practice set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_ratios_curr151.pdf`) — 10 problems on writing and
  interpreting ratios in multiple forms: writing one ratio in word, colon, and
  fraction form (with counter pictures to count from), part-to-part vs.
  part-to-whole ratios, checking and building equivalent ratios, ordering
  ratios, and a final reasoning problem about what a ratio does and does not
  say about actual counts.
- **Answer key** (`ak_ratios_curr151.pdf`) — a quick-answers table for fast
  grading, a curriculum summary (standards 6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3, difficulty
  1–4), and a full worked solution for every problem, including the two most
  common wrong answers on the marble problem (reading the ratio as counts, and
  using the part-to-part fraction on the total) so you can diagnose mistakes.
- **Study guide** (`ss_ratios_curr151.pdf`) — three short sections (writing a
  ratio three ways, part-to-part vs. part-to-whole, equivalent ratios), each
  with a rule box, a worked example, and a try-it with the answer printed
  upside down.

**What is verified:** 16 of the 17 answers on the sheet are machine-checked
with SymPy (every ratio value, every missing-value and ordering answer, plus
both declared misconception traps). One response is instructor-judged: the
written explanation on problem 10 of why a 2:3 ratio does not mean exactly 2
students — the key states what a full-credit explanation must contain. All six
study-guide example and try-it answers are machine-checked as well.
