# Measure It, Graph It (Grade 1, measurement + picture graphs)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_measuredata_curr045.pdf`) — 10 one-step problems that
  all lean on evidence: three length-comparison warm-ups (difference,
  compare symbols, ordering), then a sticker picture chart (each dot = one
  sticker) whose questions are interleaved with more measurement so your
  child must pick the right move each time (read a row, find a total, find a
  difference, compare lengths). The challenge combines chart evidence with a
  missing addend: how many more stickers does Kim need to reach 12, and how
  does the chart tell you?
- **Answer key** (`ak_measuredata_curr045.pdf`) — reasoning for every answer
  (including reading rows off the chart), a quick-answers column, and a
  common-wrong-answer note for the "how many more" problem designed to catch
  adding-instead-of-subtracting.
- **Study guide** (`ss_measuredata_curr045.pdf`) — three skills: compare two
  lengths, read a picture graph, and "how many more" as a difference, each
  with a rule, a worked example on a mini pets chart, and a try-it, plus a
  watch-out on the add-the-rows error.

**What is verified:** 12 of the 13 responses are machine-checked — the length
differences, comparisons and ordering with SymPy, and every chart answer
(including "who has the most") checked directly against the same data table
the chart is drawn from. The one open response ("say how the chart helps") is
flagged for instructor judgement with a model answer in the key. The planted
add-instead-of-subtract trap is machine-checked to be distinguishably wrong.
