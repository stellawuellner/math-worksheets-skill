# Subtracting on a Number Path — Kindergarten / Grade 1 (10 problems)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_numpath_curr027.pdf`, 4 pages) — 10 subtraction problems within
  10, each with its **own large number path** printed underneath it to hop on, and
  plenty of blank space to draw hops.
- **Answer key** (`ak_numpath_curr027.pdf`, 2 pages) — the hop-by-hop reasoning for
  every problem, written so an adult can say it out loud, plus a quick-answer bank.
- **Study guide** (`ss_numpath_curr027.pdf`, 2 pages) — three short sections, each
  with a how-to-do-it box, a worked example, and a try-it whose answer is printed
  upside down.

## What the worksheet covers

The focus is the *number path*, not mental arithmetic, so every problem tells the
child where to put a finger and which way to hop:

- **Problems 1–3, 5, 8** — count back: start on the bigger number, hop left.
  Difficulty builds from $6-2$ to a frog story problem.
- **Problems 4, 7, 10** — count up: when the two numbers are close ($9-7$,
  $10-6$, $10-8$), starting at the smaller number and counting hops upward is
  fewer moves and fewer miscounts. Problem 10 makes that choice the point of the
  problem.
- **Problems 6 and 9** — missing number: $9 - \underline{\ \ } = 4$ and
  $\underline{\ \ } - 3 = 5$. These are the two hardest and sit at the end.

Answers stay between 2 and 8, so no hop runs off either end of the path.

## Verification

All 10 answers were checked independently by the computer algebra system: 8 direct
subtractions and 2 missing-number equations. Nothing on this sheet is flagged for
manual review — every problem has one right answer that was recomputed rather than
trusted.

## Notes for the adult

The most common error at this age is counting the starting square as the first
hop (getting 5 for $6-2$). The answer key flags it under problem 1 and the study
guide's watch-out box repeats it — say the starting number *on* the square, then
count only the hops. No grade level is printed anywhere the child sees; it appears
on the answer key only.
