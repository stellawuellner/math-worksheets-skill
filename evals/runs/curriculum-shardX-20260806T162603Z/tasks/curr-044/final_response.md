# Measuring Fix-It: Gaps and Overlaps (Grade 1 measurement)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_measurefix_curr044.pdf`) — 6 problems about measuring
  with units laid end to end. Three are find-and-fix problems: Leo overlapped
  his sticks (count too big), Zoe left gaps between her paper clips (count
  too small), and Kim's overlapped count gets fixed and checked against a
  careful second measurement. The child repairs each count and explains the
  error. Measurement answers carry their units (cubes, sticks, paper clips)
  on the answer line.
- **Answer key** (`ak_measurefix_curr044.pdf`) — worked reasoning for every
  fix, a quick-answers column, and a "Common wrong answers" note for each
  planted count, so a child who answers 10 sticks or 4 clips is diagnosed
  instantly (they kept the bad count).
- **Study guide** (`ss_measurefix_curr044.pdf`) — two skills: fixing a count
  with gaps or overlaps (gaps make counts too small, overlaps too big) and
  comparing two measurements in the same unit, each with a rule, worked
  example, and try-it, plus a watch-out box.

**What is verified:** 6 of the 10 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every fixed count, difference, and the comparison. The 4 open responses (the
"which count is true", "tell what went wrong", "tell how you know", and "do
they agree" explanations) are flagged for instructor judgement, with model
answers and accept-criteria in the key. All 4 planted wrong counts are
declared as misconception traps and machine-checked to be distinguishably
wrong.
