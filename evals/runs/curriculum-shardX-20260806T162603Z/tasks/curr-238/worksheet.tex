\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every dilation on this sheet is centred at the origin. To dilate a point by scale factor $k$, multiply \textbf{both} coordinates by $k$: $(x, y) \mapsto (kx, ky)$.
  \item Lengths taken from the coordinate grid are in grid units. Real-world lengths carry the unit named in the question --- write it on the answer line.
  \item Show the calculation you used, not just the result.
\end{itemize}
