# Solving for Angle Measures on Parallel Lines — high-school geometry (10 problems)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_parallel_angles_curr307.pdf`, 7 pages) — 10 problems built as a clean
  fluency ramp. It opens with the two basic patterns one at a time (a congruent pair, then
  a supplementary pair), then mixes them so the student has to name the pair before
  choosing between "set equal" and "add to 180". No two problems repeat a skeleton: the
  set moves through corresponding, same-side interior, alternate interior, alternate
  exterior, a two-link chain, a three-parallel-lines figure, and finally a chain the
  student has to name for themselves. Every problem carries its own diagram.
- **Answer key** (`ak_parallel_angles_curr307.pdf`, 3 pages) — the pair named, the equation
  it produces, the solving worked line by line, and a substitution check on every value.
- **Study guide** (`ss_parallel_angles_curr307.pdf`, 2 pages) — three sections: congruent
  pairs (set the expressions equal), supplementary pairs (add to 180), and two-step chains
  (count the supplementary links; an odd number means subtract from 180). Rule box, worked
  example, and try-it in each.

## What is verified, and what is not

**16 of the 17 responses are machine-checked with SymPy** — every value of $x$ and every
angle measure on the sheet: $x = 20$, $x = 30$, $x = 15$ and $63^\circ$, $133^\circ$,
$x = 17$ and $63^\circ$, $x = 20$ and $115^\circ$, $x = 15$ and $100^\circ$, $x = 28$ and
$116^\circ$, $62^\circ$, $x = 17$ and $92^\circ$. All six study-guide answers are verified
the same way. Nine of the ten problems are checked end to end, which is what the key's
"What is verified" note reports.

**1 response is flagged for your judgement** — part (a) of the final problem, where the
student has to name the two relationships being chained and explain why a congruent link
followed by a supplementary link leaves the two end angles supplementary rather than
congruent. That is written reasoning, so it needs a reader; the answer key states both link
names and the conclusion a correct answer must reach, and the Quick Answers bank marks it
`---`. No `[unchecked]` marks appear.

## Notes

Standard: HSG-CO.C throughout. Difficulty ramps 1 to 5. Problem 9 uses three parallel lines
rather than two — same reasoning, one more link — and problem 10 is the synthesis. The
worksheet runs 7 pages because each problem carries a figure and a real working space;
that is the honest page cost of not shrinking the room to write.
