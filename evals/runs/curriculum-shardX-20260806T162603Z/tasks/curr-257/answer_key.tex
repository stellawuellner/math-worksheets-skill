\problem{Solve $\dfrac{x}{4} + 3 = 10$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Subtract $3$ from both sides: $\dfrac{x}{4} = 7$. Multiply both sides by
$4$: $x = 28$. Check: $28 \div 4 + 3 = 10$. \ans{x = 28}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{2}{3}(x - 9) = 4$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Multiply both sides by $\dfrac{3}{2}$ (or by $3$ then divide by $2$), which
undoes the fraction in front of the bracket: $x - 9 = 6$. Add $9$ to both sides. Check:
$\frac{2}{3}(15 - 9) = \frac{2}{3}\cdot 6 = 4$. \ans{x = 15}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{3x}{4} = \dfrac{x}{2} + 5$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The denominators are $4$ and $2$, so multiply every term on both sides by
$4$: $3x = 2x + 20$. Subtract $2x$ from both sides: $x = 20$. Check:
$\frac{3(20)}{4} = 15$ and $\frac{20}{2} + 5 = 15$. \ans{x = 20}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{x}{6} = 4$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Multiply both sides by the common denominator $6$:
$3x - x = 24$, so $2x = 24$ and $x = 12$. (Combining first also works:
$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{x}{6} = \frac{3x - x}{6} = \frac{x}{3}$.) Check: $6 - 2 = 4$. \ans{x = 12}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $0.4(x + 5) = 6$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Divide both sides by $0.4$: $x + 5 = 15$. Subtract $5$ from both sides.
Distributing first gives the same answer: $0.4x + 2 = 6$, so $0.4x = 4$ and $x = 10$. Check:
$0.4 \times 15 = 6$. \ans{x = 10}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{2x - 1}{5} = 3$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The whole numerator is divided by $5$, so multiply both sides by $5$
before touching anything else: $2x - 1 = 15$. Add $1$ to both sides for $2x = 16$, then divide by
$2$. Check: $(16 - 1) \div 5 = 3$. \ans{x = 8}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{3x}{4} - 2 = \dfrac{x}{6} + 12$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The denominators are $4$ and $6$, so the common denominator is $12$.
Multiply every term on both sides by $12$:
\begin{align*}
  9x - 24 &= 2x + 144\\
  7x &= 168\\
  x &= 24.
\end{align*}
The $-2$ and the $+12$ must be multiplied by $12$ as well --- that is the step most often missed.
Check: $\frac{3(24)}{4} - 2 = 18 - 2 = 16$ and $\frac{24}{6} + 12 = 4 + 12 = 16$. \ans{x = 24}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{8x + 12}{4} = x + 9$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Divide the whole numerator by $4$ first: $2x + 3 = x + 9$. Then subtract
$x$ from both sides for $x + 3 = 9$, and subtract $3$. Check: $(48 + 12)\div 4 = 15$ and
$6 + 9 = 15$. \ans{x = 6}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{x + 2}{3} - \dfrac{x - 4}{6} = 2$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Multiply every term by the common denominator $6$. The second fraction is
subtracted, so its whole numerator changes sign:
\begin{align*}
  2(x + 2) - (x - 4) &= 12\\
  2x + 4 - x + 4 &= 12\\
  x + 8 &= 12 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 4.
\end{align*}
Check: $\frac{6}{3} - \frac{0}{6} = 2 - 0 = 2$. \ans{x = 4}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $0.25(x - 8) = 0.5x - 7$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Distribute on the left: $0.25x - 2 = 0.5x - 7$. Subtract $0.25x$ from both
sides: $-2 = 0.25x - 7$. Add $7$ to both sides: $5 = 0.25x$. Divide both sides by $0.25$, which is
the same as multiplying by $4$. Check: $0.25(20 - 8) = 3$ and $0.5(20) - 7 = 3$. \ans{x = 20}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve $\dfrac{2}{5}(x + 10) - \dfrac{x - 6}{2} = 4$.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad Common denominator $10$; multiply every term on both sides by it:
\begin{align*}
  4(x + 10) - 5(x - 6) &= 40\\
  4x + 40 - 5x + 30 &= 40\\
  -x + 70 &= 40 \;\Rightarrow\; -x = -30 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 30.
\end{align*}
Check: $\frac{2}{5}(40) - \frac{24}{2} = 16 - 12 = 4$. \ans{x = 30}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{$\dfrac{3x - 1}{2} + \dfrac{x}{4} = \dfrac{7x}{4} + 1$: (a) solve; (b) what does the last line mean?}

\textbf{Solution (a):} \quad Multiply every term by the common denominator $4$:
\begin{align*}
  2(3x - 1) + x &= 7x + 4\\
  6x - 2 + x &= 7x + 4\\
  7x - 2 &= 7x + 4\\
  -2 &= 4.
\end{align*}
The variable terms are identical on both sides and cancel completely, leaving a statement with no
$x$ in it at all --- and a false one. \ans{\text{no solution}}

\noindent\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} Because $-2 = 4$ is false, there is no number
that can be substituted for $x$ to make the original equation true: the two sides differ by a fixed
amount of $6$ whatever $x$ is. The solution set is empty. Full credit says the false numerical
statement means no value of $x$ works; writing $x = 0$, or treating $-2 = 4$ as something to solve,
does not.
