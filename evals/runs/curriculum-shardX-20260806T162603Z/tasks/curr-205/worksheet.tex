\wstitleblock{Ordering Mixed Rational and Irrational Quantities}{Grade 8 / Pre-Algebra}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Converting each quantity to decimal form, comparing a pair, and ordering a mixed
list}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Fractions, roots and decimals cannot be compared as they are written. Put every
        quantity into decimal form first --- two places is enough for everything here.
  \item An irrational value never converts exactly, so use $\approx$ for it and $=$ for
        the exact ones.
  \item Show the decimal you used for each quantity, not just the final answer.
\end{itemize}

``$3.9$, then $\dfrac{15}{4}$, then $\sqrt{16}$''.
