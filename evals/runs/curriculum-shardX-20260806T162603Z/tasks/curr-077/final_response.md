# Fact Strategies: Turn It Around, Break It Apart (Grades 2–3)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_factstrategies_curr077.pdf`) — 10 procedural-fluency problems on commutative (turn-around) and distributive (break-apart) fact strategies. The ramp starts from fully worked patterns (problem 3 shows 7 × 6 = 7 × 5 + 7 × 1 = 35 + 7 with only the final blank; problem 4 opens up all three part-blanks), moves through independent turn-arounds, add-a-group and take-away-a-group splits, a strategy-choice problem, a two-ways problem (6 × 7 split both directions, plus *why* the answers must agree), and a free-choice challenge on 9 × 7. No two problems share an identical skeleton.
- **Answer key** (`ak_factstrategies_curr077.pdf`) — worked reasoning for each strategy step, acceptable alternate strategies for the choice problems, a labeled quick-answers bank, and a Curriculum section (3.OA.C.7, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_factstrategies_curr077.pdf`) — 2 pages: turn-around facts, break-apart facts, and choosing between them, each with rule box, worked example, and try-it, plus a watch-out on the classic forgot-one-piece slip.

**What is verified:** 12 of the 16 graded responses are machine-checked with SymPy — every numeric blank, with break-apart answers verified in the exact split the sheet shows (e.g. 8 × 7 checked as 8 × (5 + 2), parts and total separately). The 4 open responses — the written turn-around fact, the circled skip-count choice, the why-both-ways-agree sentence, and the challenge strategy — are instructor-judged with rubrics, and the quick-answers bank marks each `---`.
