# Find the Missing Number (Grade 1, missing addends within 20)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_missingaddend_curr038.pdf`) — 8 missing-addend problems
  within 20. The representations do real work: a ten frame, part--whole
  boxes, and short real-life stories (grapes, the bus, shells) with the units
  and givens stated plainly and no extra story clutter. The set ramps from a
  warm-up ten-frame fact to a start-unknown story problem.
- **Answer key** (`ak_missingaddend_curr038.pdf`) — every solution shows the
  reasoning (count on, make ten, or subtract to find the part), plus a
  quick-answers column and "Common wrong answers" notes for the two problems
  designed to catch the classic add-the-two-numbers-you-see mistake.
- **Study guide** (`ss_missingaddend_curr038.pdf`) — three skills (see the
  parts and the whole, count on to the total, use subtraction to find and
  check), each with a rule, a worked example, and a try-it with the answer
  upside down, plus a watch-out box on the add-everything error.

**What is verified:** all 9 responses on the sheet (one per blank, including
both parts of the fact-family problem) are machine-checked with SymPy —
nothing on this sheet needs manual grading, and the key's "What is verified"
note says the same. The two planted wrong-method traps (adding the visible
numbers) are machine-checked to be distinguishably wrong.
