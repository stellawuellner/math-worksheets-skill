\problem{Round 362 to the nearest hundred (number line 300--400).}

\textbf{Solution:} The midpoint between 300 and 400 is 350. On the line, 362
sits to the \emph{right} of 350, so it is closer to 400. \ans{400}

\problem{Round $4{,}238$ to the nearest ten (number line $4{,}230$--$4{,}240$).}

\textbf{Solution:} The midpoint is $4{,}235$, and $4{,}238$ sits past it —
closer to $4{,}240$. \ans{4240}

\problem{Round $5{,}671$ to the nearest ten.}

\textbf{Solution:} Underline the tens digit (7); look one place right at the
ones digit: $1 < 5$, so keep the 7 tens and zero the ones. \ans{5670}

\problem{Round $5{,}671$ to the nearest hundred.}

\textbf{Solution:} Underline the hundreds digit (6); the tens digit is
$7 \geq 5$, so round the 6 hundreds up to 7 hundreds. \ans{5700}

\problem{Round $5{,}671$ to the nearest thousand.}

\textbf{Solution:} Underline the thousands digit (5); the hundreds digit is
$6 \geq 5$, so round the 5 thousands up to 6 thousands. \ans{6000}

\problem{$18{,}437$ tickets, reported to the nearest thousand.}

\textbf{Solution:} The thousands digit is 8; the hundreds digit is $4 < 5$, so
the thousands digit stays and everything to the right becomes zero. The paper
prints $18{,}000$ tickets. \ans{18000}

\problem{Round $49{,}850$ to the nearest thousand.}

\textbf{Solution:} The thousands digit is 9; the hundreds digit is $8 \geq 5$,
so round up. But 49 thousands rounding up becomes 50 thousands — the carry
changes the ten-thousands digit too: $49{,}850 \rightarrow 50{,}000$.
\ans{50000}

\problem{$7{,}352$ dollars, rounded to the nearest hundred dollars.}

\textbf{Solution:} The hundreds digit is 3; the tens digit is $5 \geq 5$, so
round the 3 hundreds up to 4 hundreds: $7{,}400$ dollars. \ans{7400}

\problem{Round the population $26{,}489$ to the nearest ten, hundred, and
thousand.}

\textbf{Solution:} Nearest ten: ones digit $9 \geq 5$, so $26{,}490$.
\ans{26490}

Nearest hundred: tens digit $8 \geq 5$, so 4 hundreds become 5: $26{,}500$.
\ans{26500}

Nearest thousand: hundreds digit $4 < 5$, so the 6 thousands stay: $26{,}000$.
\ans{26000}

\problem{Kai says $3{,}952$ rounds to $4{,}000$ (nearest hundred); Jo says
$3{,}900$. Who is correct?}

\textbf{Solution:} The hundreds digit is 9 and the tens digit is $5 \geq 5$, so
the 9 hundreds round \emph{up}: 39 hundreds become 40 hundreds, and the carry
gives $4{,}000$. Kai is correct. \ans{4000}

Full credit for the explanation: the tens digit 5 means round up; Jo rounded
down; and rounding 39 hundreds up to 40 hundreds carries into the thousands
place, which is why the answer looks like a thousand.
