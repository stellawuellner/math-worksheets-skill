\begin{center}
{\large Moving among tables, graphs, equations, and contexts}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item In a proportional relationship $y = kx$, the number $k$ is the \emph{constant of proportionality} --- the value of $y$ when $x$ is $1$.
  \item Every problem asks you to move between representations. Show the equation you are using, even when the question starts from a table or a graph.
  \item Work in the space on the left of each problem; use the grid on the right to plot or sketch the relationship.
\end{itemize}
