# Comparing Three-Digit Numbers — Grade 2

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_compare3_curr053.pdf`) — 10 problems in a representations-and-
  applications format on comparing three-digit numbers by place value: symbol
  comparisons, an expanded-form comparison, ordering sets of three and four
  numbers, and real-world comparisons with explicit givens and units (pages read,
  hill heights in meters, cans collected, points scored). Problem 9 is an
  error-analysis item (Sam compares tens before hundreds); the challenge orders
  907/790/970/709, where the same digits sit in different places.
- **Answer key** (`ak_compare3_curr053.pdf`) — place-by-place reasoning for every
  problem, a quick-answers bank with per-blank labels, a "common wrong answers"
  note for the declared trap (building 258 instead of 852 from the digits 2, 5, 8),
  and the curriculum section (2.NBT.A).
- **Study guide** (`ss_compare3_curr053.pdf`) — three sections (symbol comparison,
  ordering, comparing in a story), each with a rule box, worked example, and try-it.

**Verification split:** 10 of 12 responses are machine-verified with SymPy — every
comparison symbol, every ordering, and the digit-building challenge (its trap is
proven distinguishable). **2 responses are instructor-judged**: "who read more?" in
problem 4 and the explanation of Sam's error in problem 9 — the key states exactly
what each correct response must contain, and its "What is verified" note marks the
same two. All 6 study-guide examples/try-its are machine-verified.
