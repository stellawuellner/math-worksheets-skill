# Second and Higher Derivatives — three PDFs

**Course:** AP Calculus AB/BC · **Topic:** implicit, inverse and higher-order
differentiation, focused on finding second and higher derivatives ·
**8 problems**

## What you get

- **`ws_higher_derivs_curr463.pdf` — the student worksheet (4 pages).**
  Eight applied problems, every one of which asks for a second or higher
  derivative. Each model states its units (metres, seconds, litres, minutes,
  thousand cells) and every given is in the stem — there is no decorative
  story detail to wade through. The ramp runs from a single polynomial second
  derivative (problem 1) to a synthesis problem that uses the second
  derivative to locate the minimum of a growth *rate* (problem 8).
  The grade/course level is deliberately not printed on the worksheet.
- **`ak_higher_derivs_curr463.pdf` — the full answer key (3 pages).**
  Every problem is restated and worked line by line, not answered. It opens
  with a generated Quick Answers bank, a "What is verified" note, and a
  Curriculum block listing the AP codes with the problems that carry them.
- **`ss_higher_derivs_curr463.pdf` — the study guide (2 pages).**
  Three skill sections — repeated differentiation, acceleration from a
  position model, second derivative of an implicit curve — each with a
  rule box, a worked example whose first step says *why* that tool applies,
  and a try-it whose answer is printed upside down inside the box.

## Verification, stated honestly

There are **14 responses** across the 8 problems (several problems have parts
(a)/(b)/(c)).

- **13 of 14 are machine-checked** with SymPy: every derivative is
  recomputed from the model in the stem, every root is re-solved, and every
  numeric evaluation is recomputed. The answer key's boxed values are bound
  back to those verified results by a separate gate, so a printed answer
  cannot drift from the checked one.
- **1 of 14 is instructor-judged**: problem 8(c), "explain how the sign of
  $P''$ shows the growth rate is smallest there". It appears in the Quick
  Answers bank as `---`, and the key's Solution (c) spells out exactly what a
  correct response must contain — the sign-change argument *and* the
  conclusion about the growth rate rather than the population. Nothing else on
  this sheet needs your judgement.

The study guide's six worked results (three examples, three try-its) are all
machine-checked too.

## A note on standards tags

`references/standards-map.md` has no row of its own for higher-order
derivatives (AP's FUN-3.F). Rather than tag off-topic, each problem carries the
cluster it actually exercises from inside the map's own ranges: FUN-3.A for
repeated power-rule work, FUN-3.C for the chain-rule cases (the sinusoid and
the exponential), and FUN-3.D for the two implicit curves. The task's
`standard_refs` (FUN-3.D–FUN-3.E) is a range, so the specific cluster is what
gets printed on the key.

## Suggested order

Study guide first, then the worksheet, then the answer key.
