\begin{center}
\textit{Reading a parabola from vertex form, from standard form, and from its
intercepts}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions.} Every grid runs from $-6$ to $6$ across and $-10$
to $10$ up and down. For each function, find the features asked for, then
sketch the parabola on its grid using the vertex, the axis of symmetry, and at
least two more points.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Vertex form} $y = a(x-h)^2 + k$: the vertex is $(h, k)$. Read the
sign carefully --- $(x+3)^2$ means $h = -3$.
\item \textbf{Standard form} $y = ax^2 + bx + c$: the axis of symmetry is
$x = -\frac{b}{2a}$; substitute it back to get the vertex $y$-value. The
$y$-intercept is $c$.
\item $a > 0$ opens up, $a < 0$ opens down; $|a| > 1$ is narrower and
$|a| < 1$ is wider than $y = x^2$.
\end{itemize}
