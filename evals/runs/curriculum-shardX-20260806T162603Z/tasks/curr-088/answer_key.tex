% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 1
\problem{A pear has a mass of 154 g. An apple has a mass of 138 g. What is the
total mass of the two fruits together?}

\textbf{Solution:} ``Together'' means put the masses in one group, so add:
$154 + 138 = 292$. The total mass is \ans{292\ \text{g}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 2
\problem{A bottle holds 500 mL of juice. Amir pours out 175 mL. How many
milliliters of juice are left in the bottle?}

\textbf{Solution:} Juice is taken away, so subtract what was poured from what
the bottle held: $500 - 175 = 325$. There are \ans{325\ \text{mL}} of juice
left.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 3
\problem{One brick has a mass of 2 kg. What is the mass of a stack of 9 bricks?}

\textbf{Solution:} There are 9 equal groups of 2 kg, so multiply:
$9 \times 2 = 18$. The stack has a mass of \ans{18\ \text{kg}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 4
\problem{A cooler holds 24 L of lemonade. It is poured equally into 4 jugs. How
many liters of lemonade go into each jug?}

\textbf{Solution:} One total is shared into equal groups, so divide:
$24 \div 4 = 6$. Each jug gets \ans{6\ \text{L}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 5
\problem{A bag of rice has a mass of 750 g. A bag of beans has a mass of 425 g.
How much more mass does the bag of rice have than the bag of beans?}

\textbf{Solution:} ``How much more'' compares the two masses, so subtract the
smaller from the larger: $750 - 425 = 325$. The rice has \ans{325\ \text{g}}
more mass.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 6
\problem{A fish tank holds 38 L of water. Keiko adds 17 L more. How many liters
of water are in the tank now?}

\textbf{Solution:} Water is added to what is already there, so add:
$38 + 17 = 55$. The tank now holds \ans{55\ \text{L}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 7
\problem{One spoonful of cough medicine is 5 mL. Dev takes 7 spoonfuls during
the whole week. How many milliliters of medicine is that in all?}

\textbf{Solution:} There are 7 equal spoonfuls of 5 mL, so multiply:
$7 \times 5 = 35$. Dev takes \ans{35\ \text{mL}} in all.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 8
\problem{A farmer divides 56 kg of potatoes equally into 8 sacks. How many
kilograms of potatoes go into each sack?}

\textbf{Solution:} One total is split into 8 equal shares, so divide:
$56 \div 8 = 7$. Each sack holds \ans{7\ \text{kg}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 9
\problem{The pan balance is level. The left pan holds a bag of flour; the right
pan holds a 400 g weight and a 250 g weight together. What is the mass of the
bag of flour?}

\textbf{Solution:} A level balance means both pans hold the \emph{same} mass.
The right pan holds $400 + 250 = 650$ grams, so the flour must match it. The
bag of flour has a mass of \ans{650\ \text{g}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 10
\problem{A measuring jug holds 900 mL of water. Lena pours all of the water
equally into 3 glasses. How many milliliters of water go into each glass?}

\textbf{Solution:} The whole 900 mL is shared into 3 equal glasses, so divide:
$900 \div 3 = 300$. Each glass gets \ans{300\ \text{mL}}
