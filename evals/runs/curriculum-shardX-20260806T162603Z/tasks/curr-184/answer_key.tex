\problem{Solve the inequality: $6x > 42$

\textbf{Solution:} Divide both sides by $6$ (a \emph{positive} number, so the
symbol stays the same): $x > \frac{42}{6}$, so the solution is \ans{x > 7}}

\problem{Solve the inequality: $-5x \le 30$

\textbf{Solution:} Divide both sides by $-5$. Dividing by a \emph{negative}
number reverses the symbol, so $\le$ becomes $\ge$: $x \ge \frac{30}{-5}$,
giving \ans{x \ge -6}}

\problem{Solve the inequality $-4x < 18$; then give the least integer solution.

\textbf{Solution (a):} Divide both sides by $-4$ and reverse the symbol:
$x > \frac{18}{-4}$, so \ans{x > -\tfrac{9}{2}}

\textbf{Solution (b):} $-\tfrac{9}{2} = -4.5$, and the least integer greater
than $-4.5$ is \ans{-4}

\emph{Common wrong answer:} a student who forgets to reverse the symbol gets
$x < -4.5$ and picks $-5$, which is not a solution: $-4(-5) = 20$ and $20 < 18$
is false.}

\problem{Solve the inequality: $3x - 7 > 5$

\textbf{Solution:} Add $7$ to both sides: $3x > 12$. Divide by $3$ (positive,
no reversal): \ans{x > 4}}

\problem{Solve the inequality: $-2x + 9 \le 1$

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $9$ from both sides: $-2x \le -8$. Divide by $-2$
and reverse the symbol: $x \ge \frac{-8}{-2}$, so \ans{x \ge 4}}

\problem{Priya's work on $-3x < 12$: she wrote $x < -4$.

\textbf{Solution (a):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} Priya divided both sides by
$-3$ but did \textbf{not} reverse the inequality symbol. Full credit for any
sentence naming the missing reversal when dividing by a negative.

\textbf{Solution (b):} Divide both sides of $-3x < 12$ by $-3$ and reverse the
symbol: \ans{x > -4}}

\problem{Deshaun's work on $-2x + 3 > 11$: he wrote $-2x > 14$, then $x < -7$.

\textbf{Solution (a):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} The error is in step 1:
Deshaun \emph{added} $3$ to the right side ($11 + 3 = 14$) instead of
\emph{subtracting} $3$ from both sides. The correct step is $-2x > 11 - 3$,
that is $-2x > 8$. (His step 2 reversal was actually correct.)

\textbf{Solution (b):} From $-2x > 8$, divide by $-2$ and reverse the symbol:
$x < \frac{8}{-2}$, so \ans{x < -4}

The boundary value of the correct solution comes from $-2x + 3 = 11$, whose
solution is $x = -4$: machine-checked as \ans{-4}}

\problem{Freezer experiment: temperature $14 - 3h$ after $h$ hours; when is it
below $-7^\circ$F?

\textbf{Solution (a):} Require $14 - 3h < -7$. Subtract $14$: $-3h < -21$.
Divide by $-3$ and reverse the symbol: $h > \frac{-21}{-3}$, so \ans{h > 7}
The temperature is below $-7^\circ$F after more than $7$ hours.

\textbf{Solution (b):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} The symbol reversed because
both sides were divided by $-3$, a negative number; dividing by a negative
reverses the order of every pair of numbers. Any clear statement of this rule
earns full credit.}
