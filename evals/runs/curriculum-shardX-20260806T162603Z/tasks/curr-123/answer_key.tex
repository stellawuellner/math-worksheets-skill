\problem{The strip is cut into 8 equal parts and half is shaded. Fill in:
$\tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{\square}{8}$}

\textbf{Solution:} Half of the 8 parts are shaded, and half of 8 is
$8 \div 2 = 4$. So $\tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{4}{8}$; the missing numerator is
\ans{4}

\problem{Write the correct symbol: $\tfrac{3}{8}$ \_\_ $\tfrac{1}{2}$}

\textbf{Solution:} The benchmark $\tfrac{1}{2}$ written in eighths is
$\tfrac{4}{8}$. Since 3 eighths is less than 4 eighths, we get
$\tfrac{3}{8}$ $\ans{<}$ $\tfrac{1}{2}$

\problem{Compare $\tfrac{2}{3}$ and $\tfrac{3}{4}$ by rewriting both in
twelfths.}

\textbf{Solution:} $\tfrac{2}{3}$: multiply top and bottom by 4 to get
twelfths --- numerator \ans{8}
$\tfrac{3}{4}$: multiply top and bottom by 3 --- numerator \ans{9}
Now compare 8 twelfths with 9 twelfths: 8 is smaller, so
$\tfrac{2}{3}$ $\ans{<}$ $\tfrac{3}{4}$

\problem{Lena's ribbon is $\tfrac{2}{5}$ m; her ruler is marked in tenths.
Write the same length in tenths of a meter.}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiply top and bottom by 2:
$\tfrac{2}{5} = \tfrac{2 \times 2}{5 \times 2}$, which is
$\ans{\tfrac{4}{10}}$

\problem{Jug A is $\tfrac{5}{8}$ full, Jug B is $\tfrac{3}{8}$ full. Compare
each to $\tfrac{1}{2}$.}

\textbf{Solution:} One half in eighths is $\tfrac{4}{8}$.
Jug A: 5 eighths is more than 4 eighths, so
$\tfrac{5}{8}$ $\ans{>}$ $\tfrac{1}{2}$
Jug B: 3 eighths is less than 4 eighths, so
$\tfrac{3}{8}$ $\ans{<}$ $\tfrac{1}{2}$
Only Jug A is more than half full.

\problem{Pedro ran $\tfrac{5}{6}$ mi, Amara ran $\tfrac{7}{9}$ mi. Rewrite
both in eighteenths and compare.}

\textbf{Solution:} $\tfrac{5}{6}$: multiply top and bottom by 3 --- numerator
\ans{15}
$\tfrac{7}{9}$: multiply top and bottom by 2 --- numerator \ans{14}
15 eighteenths beats 14 eighteenths, so
$\tfrac{5}{6}$ $\ans{>}$ $\tfrac{7}{9}$ --- Pedro ran farther.

\problem{Compare $\tfrac{4}{9}$ and $\tfrac{6}{11}$ using the benchmark
$\tfrac{1}{2}$, and explain.}

\textbf{Solution:} Half of 9 is $4.5$, and $4 < 4.5$, so $\tfrac{4}{9}$ is
\emph{less} than $\tfrac{1}{2}$. Half of 11 is $5.5$, and $6 > 5.5$, so
$\tfrac{6}{11}$ is \emph{more} than $\tfrac{1}{2}$. A fraction below the
benchmark is smaller than one above it:
$\tfrac{4}{9}$ $\ans{<}$ $\tfrac{6}{11}$

\emph{Explanation (instructor judges):} full credit for any reasoning that
places $\tfrac{4}{9}$ below one half and $\tfrac{6}{11}$ above one half and
concludes from the benchmark.

\problem{Marco shades $\tfrac{2}{3}$ of one strip and $\tfrac{8}{12}$ of an
identical strip. (a) Fill in $\tfrac{2}{3} = \tfrac{\square}{12}$.
(b) Compare.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Multiply top and bottom by 4: the numerator is
\ans{8}
(b) Both strips show 8 twelfths, so the fractions are equivalent:
$\tfrac{2}{3}$ $\ans{=}$ $\tfrac{8}{12}$

\problem{Jon has $\tfrac{7}{12}$ of a pan, Kayla has $\tfrac{5}{8}$. Rewrite
both in twenty-fourths and compare.}

\textbf{Solution:} $\tfrac{7}{12}$: multiply top and bottom by 2 ---
numerator \ans{14}
$\tfrac{5}{8}$: multiply top and bottom by 3 --- numerator \ans{15}
14 twenty-fourths is less than 15 twenty-fourths, so
$\tfrac{7}{12}$ $\ans{<}$ $\tfrac{5}{8}$ --- Kayla has more.

\problem{Challenge. (a) Compare $\tfrac{3}{8}$ and $\tfrac{1}{2}$.
(b) Find a fraction between them and explain.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) One half is $\tfrac{4}{8}$, and 3 eighths is less:
$\tfrac{3}{8}$ $\ans{<}$ $\tfrac{1}{2}$

(b) \emph{(instructor judges)} Any fraction strictly between works. Example:
in sixteenths, $\tfrac{3}{8} = \tfrac{6}{16}$ and
$\tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{8}{16}$, so $\tfrac{7}{16}$ fits between them. Another
correct choice is $\tfrac{2}{5}$. Credit any valid fraction with sound
reasoning.
