\begin{center}
\small Partial quotients, long division, and estimating before you divide
\end{center}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Show the division, not just the answer --- long division steps, or partial quotients written
        underneath each other and added at the end.
  \item Check every answer by multiplying back: (quotient) $\times$ (divisor) $+$ (remainder) should
        rebuild the number you started with.
  \item Where a problem has no remainder, say so by writing the quotient alone.
\end{itemize}
