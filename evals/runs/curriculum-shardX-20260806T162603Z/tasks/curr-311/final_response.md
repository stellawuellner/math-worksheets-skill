# Reading the Evidence for Triangle Congruence — high-school geometry (8 problems)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_congruence_evidence_curr311.pdf`, 7 pages) — 8 guided-concept problems,
  each with its own marked figure. The sheet works from the picture to the notation: tick
  marks and arcs on a pair of triangles, and the student names the criterion and writes the
  congruence statements it rests on. It covers all five criteria — SSS, SAS, ASA, HL, AAS —
  and spends two problems on the evidence nobody marks (a shared side by the reflexive
  property, vertical angles at a crossing). The final problem asks why side-side-angle is
  not on the list, using the figure in front of the student.
- **Answer key** (`ak_congruence_evidence_curr311.pdf`, 3 pages) — the criterion named with
  the marks that produced it, the congruence statements in correct correspondence, every
  equation worked with a substitution check, and a grading note on each open item pointing
  at the error to look for (naming an angle without checking it is *included*, or listing
  pairs out of correspondence).
- **Study guide** (`ss_congruence_evidence_curr311.pdf`, 2 pages) — three sections: naming
  the criterion from the marks (with the SSA warning and why HL escapes it), reading
  correspondence off a congruence statement to get an equation, and finding the evidence
  nobody marked. Rule box, worked example, and try-it in each.

## What is verified, and what is not

**6 of the 14 responses are machine-checked with SymPy** — every numeric answer: $x = 7$,
$x = 10$ and $m\angle A = 46^\circ$, the missing leg $BC = 12$ cm from the Pythagorean
theorem, and $x = 8$ with $EF = 20$ cm. All six study-guide answers are verified the same
way. Three problems (2, 4 and 7) are checked end to end, which is what the
key's "What is verified" note reports.

**8 responses are flagged for your judgement** — every naming and reasoning item: problems
1, 3(a), 3(b), 5(a), 6(a), 6(b), 8(a) and 8(b). Naming a criterion and justifying it is
prose, and it is the whole point of a concept sheet on congruence evidence, so it cannot be
machine-checked and should not be replaced by something that can. The answer key gives a
model answer for each plus the specific element that must be present. The Quick Answers
bank marks them `---`; no `[unchecked]` marks appear.

## Notes

Standards: HSG-CO.B (the congruence criteria) on problems 1–7, HSG-CO.C on problem 8, which
leans on the vertical angles theorem. Difficulty ramps 1 to 5. Every problem carries its own
figure, and the three figures that show numbers show only numbers the verification uses.
