\vspace{0.05cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, with $c$ the hypotenuse. On a grid, the horizontal and vertical gaps between two points are the legs and the segment joining them is the hypotenuse.
  \item Inside a box, work in two stages: first the diagonal across the base, then the diagonal of the box using that answer as a leg.
  \item The problems mix the four methods on purpose. Decide which one a question needs before you calculate.
  \item Round to two decimal places when the answer is not exact, and write the unit on the answer line.
\end{itemize}
