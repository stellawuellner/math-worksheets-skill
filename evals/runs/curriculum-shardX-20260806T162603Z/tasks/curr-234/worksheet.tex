\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item In $f(a) = b$ the number $a$ is the \textbf{input} and $b$ is the \textbf{output}. Decide which one a question is asking for before you start.
  \item Several problems show work that a student got wrong. Correct it \textit{and} say what went wrong --- both are part of the answer.
  \item Write the substitution you used, not just the final number.
\end{itemize}
