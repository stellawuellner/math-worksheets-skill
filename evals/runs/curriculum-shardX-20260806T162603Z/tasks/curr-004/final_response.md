# Find It and Fix It: Counting Detective (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **ws_fixcount_curr004.pdf** — a 6-problem error-analysis worksheet on
  finding missing or misplaced numbers in a counting sequence. It includes
  three find-and-fix items (Mia skips 20; Leo counts "38, 39, 30"; a
  hundred-chart row where 69 sits where 96 belongs) plus number tracks with
  missing numbers and mixed-up number cards to reorder.
- **ak_fixcount_curr004.pdf** — the step-by-step answer key. Each solution
  shows the detective reasoning (whisper-count and match, check both
  neighbors), and the key prints a "Common wrong answers" note for each
  planted misconception so a wrong response diagnoses itself.
- **ss_fixcount_curr004.pdf** — a one-page study guide: filling a missing
  number using its neighbors, spotting a counting mistake by whisper-counting,
  and putting numbers back in counting order — each with a rule, worked
  example, and try-it.

**What is verified:** 9 of the 12 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every missing number, every correction, and the card ordering has its own
labeled entry. The three planted wrong results are declared as misconception
traps, and the checker proved each trap value (copying the neighbor, writing
21 for the skipped 20, sliding back to 30 after 39, reversing 96 to 69) is
distinguishably wrong, so the problems really do catch the errors they were
designed around. **Three items are instructor-judged** — the child's spoken or
written explanations ("what should Mia remember", "what went wrong", "why
can't 69 be there") — and the key gives an explicit accept rubric for each.
The study guide's examples and try-its are machine-verified.
