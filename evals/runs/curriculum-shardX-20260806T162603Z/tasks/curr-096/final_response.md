# Reading Scaled Picture and Bar Graphs (Grade 3) — what you received

Three PDFs:

- **Student worksheet** (`ws_graphs_curr096.pdf`) — 8 guided problems on 3.MD
  built around two data displays printed on the sheet: Graph A, a scaled
  picture graph (each book symbol = 5 books), and Graph B, a scaled bar graph
  whose number line counts by twos. Problems move from reading one row with
  the scale, to combining rows, to building a row (draw Dev's 25 books), to
  reading, totaling, comparing, and ordering the bar-graph data — each model
  tied to the multiplication, division, or subtraction sentence it stands for.
- **Step-by-step answer key** (`ak_graphs_curr096.pdf`) — worked reasoning
  showing how the scale is used in every problem, a quick-answers bank, a
  "Common wrong answers" note for the declared trap (adding the bars instead
  of subtracting in problem 6), and rubrics for the two open responses.
- **Study guide** (`ss_graphs_curr096.pdf`) — three sections (read the picture
  graph scale, count bar-graph scale steps, compare two bars), each with a
  rule box, worked example, and a try-it with the answer upside down, plus a
  watch-out box about counting symbols as ones.

**What is verified:** the worksheet's 10 responses split 8 machine-checked and
2 instructor-judged. The bar-graph checks read from the same data array that
draws the chart, so the picture and the answers cannot disagree; SymPy
recomputed the scaled readings, the total, the comparison (and confirmed the
declared add-instead-of-subtract trap is distinguishably wrong), the smallest
group, and the least-to-greatest ordering. The two instructor-judged items are
the drawn pictograph row in problem 3(b) and the "which display and why"
explanation in 8(b) — the key prints a rubric for each, matching its own
"What is verified" note. All 6 study-guide examples and try-its are
machine-verified.
