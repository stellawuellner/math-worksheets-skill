\begin{center}
{\large Two-step stories about time, money, mass, and liquid volume}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{How to work these problems}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every problem takes \emph{two} steps. Write both steps, not just the answer.
  \item Watch the units. If one number is in litres and another is in millilitres,
        change one of them first: $1$ L $= 1000$ mL and $1$ kg $= 1000$ g.
  \item Write the unit on your answer line: minutes, cents, grams or millilitres.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.15cm}

\problem{Maya's swim lesson lasts 45 minutes. After the lesson she waits 10 minutes
for her ride, and then the ride home takes 20 minutes. How many minutes pass from
the start of the lesson until Maya gets home?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{minutes}}

\problem{A movie is 95 minutes long. Ben watches 40 minutes of it before dinner and
25 minutes of it after dinner. How many minutes of the movie does he still have
left to watch?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{minutes}}

\problem[5cm]{Ella has \$20. She buys a book for \$8.75 and a marker for \$3.50.
How much money does Ella have left? Give your answer in dollars.}

\problem{Sam has 3 quarters and 4 dimes in his pocket. He spends 55 cents on a
sticker. How many cents does Sam have left?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{cents}}

\problem{A bag of apples has a mass of 250 g and a bag of pears has a mass of 180 g.
Ravi puts both bags into a basket with a mass of 400 g. What is the mass of the
basket with the fruit inside?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{g}}

\problem{A jug holds 2 L of juice. Nina fills 2 glasses from the jug, and each glass
holds 350 mL. How many millilitres of juice are left in the jug?
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{mL}}

\problem{Leo practises the piano for 25 minutes on each of the 5 school days. On
Saturday he practises for 40 minutes. How many minutes does Leo practise in the
whole week?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{minutes}}

\problem[5cm]{A museum ticket costs \$6. Mr Diaz buys 4 tickets and pays with a
\$50 bill. How much change does he get? Give your answer in dollars.}

\problem{A box of books has a mass of 3 kg. Ana takes 2 books out of the box, and
each book has a mass of 450 g. What is the mass of the box now?
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{g}}

\problem{A water cooler holds 5 L of water when it is full. In the morning the class
drinks 1200 mL and in the afternoon the class drinks 1500 mL. How many millilitres
of water are left in the cooler?
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{mL}}

\problem{Sara rides the bus to school and back home again, so she makes 2 bus rides
on each of the 5 school days. Each ride takes 35 minutes. How many minutes does
Sara spend on the bus in one school week?
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{minutes}}

\problem[6cm]{Ms Park is buying art supplies for her class. Each of the 6 groups
needs a pack of paper that costs \$3 and 2 glue sticks that cost \$2 each. Ms Park
starts with \$50. How much money will she have left after she pays for all of the
groups? Give your answer in dollars.}
