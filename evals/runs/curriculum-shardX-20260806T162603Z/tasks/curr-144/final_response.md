Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 5 measurement-units worksheet:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_sqcb_curr144.pdf`) — 8 problems built around the
  squared-versus-cubed unit error: area and cube-counting warm-ups, a
  pick-the-right-unit item (cm / m² / mL / cm³), volume with labeled cm³
  answer lines, and three find-and-fix items — Tomas labels a volume in cm²,
  Lena converts 2 m² to cm² by multiplying by 100 only once, and Roy claims a
  doubled edge doubles a cube's volume. Every answer line that needs a unit
  prints it.
- **Answer key** (`ak_sqcb_curr144.pdf`) — full reasoning for every problem
  (squares cover, cubes fill, squared conversions use the factor twice), a
  quick-answers bank with sub-part slots labeled, and "common wrong answers"
  notes for the six declared misconception traps (perimeter-for-area, one
  layer only, base area only, added dimensions, factor-of-100 once, edge times
  three).
- **Study guide** (`ss_sqcb_curr144.pdf`) — three skills: square units for
  flat space, cubic units for solid space, and converting squared units, each
  with rule box, worked example, try-it, plus a unit-sense watch-out box.

**What is verified:** 9 of the 15 checks are machine-verified with SymPy —
every numeric answer (with its unit bound to the printed answer line and the
key's boxed answer) plus all 6 study-guide examples/try-its, and all six traps
are proven distinguishable from the correct answers. The 6 open items are
judged by the instructor: the three pick-the-unit choices in problem 3 and
the three written "name the error" explanations (5a, 7a, 8d). Each has a
rubric in the key stating what a correct response must contain; the key's
"What is verified" note shows the same split.
