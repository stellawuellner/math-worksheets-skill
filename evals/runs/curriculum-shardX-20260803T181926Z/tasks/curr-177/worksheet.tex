\small\itshape Solving two-step equations with positive and negative coefficients, division form, and checking solutions by substitution
\end{center}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Undo the addition or subtraction first, then undo the multiplication or division.
  \item Show every step, and keep both sides of the equation balanced.
  \item When a problem says \textit{check}, substitute your answer back into the original equation and show that the two sides are equal.
  \item Write your final answer on the answer line.
\end{itemize}
