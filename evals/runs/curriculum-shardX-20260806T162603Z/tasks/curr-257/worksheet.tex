\begin{center}
\textit{Clearing fractions, distributing a fractional factor, and collecting the variable on one side}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{The pattern to copy}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Multiplying \emph{both} sides by a common denominator clears every fraction at once ---
        do it before anything else, and multiply every term, including the ones with no fraction.
  \item A fraction in front of a bracket still has to reach both terms inside.
  \item Once the fractions are gone, collect the variable on one side and the numbers on the other,
        then divide.
  \item Check every answer by substituting it back into the \emph{original} equation.
\end{itemize}
