# Teen Numbers: Tens and Ones Fix-It (Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_teennums_curr034.pdf`) — 6 problems on teen numbers and
  place value, all aimed at the classic mix-up of swapping the tens and the
  ones. Two are find-and-fix problems (Ben builds 13 with 3 tens and 1 one;
  Mia writes 61 for 1 ten and 6 ones), and the child both fixes the number and
  tells how they know. Difficulty ramps from a warm-up to a challenge.
- **Answer key** (`ak_teennums_curr034.pdf`) — full reasoning for every
  problem, a quick-answers column for fast grading, and a "Common wrong
  answers" note for each planted mistake, so a wrong answer of 91 or 61 tells
  you exactly which swap happened.
- **Study guide** (`ss_teennums_curr034.pdf`) — a 2-page card with three
  skills (making a number from tens and ones, finding the tens and ones, and
  comparing swapped-digit numbers), each with a rule box, a worked example,
  and a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** 8 of the 10 responses on the sheet are machine-checked
with SymPy (every number blank and the comparison). The 2 "tell how you know"
explanations on the find-and-fix problems are genuinely open responses — they
are flagged for instructor judgement, and the key prints a model explanation
and what to accept. All 4 planted wrong results (the swapped-digit answers)
are declared as misconception traps and machine-checked to be distinguishably
wrong, so the sheet really can catch the error it teaches against.
