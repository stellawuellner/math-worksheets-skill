# Take Away and Write It (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_takeaway_curr026.pdf`) — 8 problems on taking away
  objects and writing the matching subtraction equation. The models do the
  teaching: crossed-out counter pictures, a ten-frame to mark up, a
  draw-it-yourself problem, and fill-in equation frames
  (start − taken away = left) that connect each picture or story to
  notation. It ends with a challenge where the picture shows the start (10
  balloons) and the result (7 left), and the child crosses out balloons to
  find the missing change.
- **Answer key** (`ak_takeaway_curr026.pdf`) — reasoning for every problem
  (count-back sequences, partner-of-ten thinking), a quick-answers bank,
  and a curriculum section (K.OA.A and 1.OA.C.6, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_takeaway_curr026.pdf`) — three sections: cross out to
  subtract, write the subtraction equation, and take-away facts with ten,
  each with a rule box, worked example, and fresh try-it, plus a watch-out
  ("count what is left, not what you crossed out").

**What is verified:** 8 of the 11 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every difference and the challenge's missing change. The equation frames'
first two blanks transcribe the story numbers and are printed as working
space, which the answer key discloses. The 3 drawing/marking responses
(draw 7 and cross out 3; cross out 4 dots; cross out the popped balloons)
are flagged for instructor review, with the key stating exactly what a
correct picture shows. The key's "What is verified" note reflects the same
split.
