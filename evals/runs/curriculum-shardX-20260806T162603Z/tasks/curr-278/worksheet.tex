\wstitleblock{Writing Growth and Decay Models from Data}{Algebra 1}{}

\begin{center}
\small Finding the multiplier from data, writing growth and decay models, and solving with them
\end{center}
\vspace{0.15cm}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every model on this sheet has the shape $y = a \cdot r^{\,t}$: $a$ is the amount at $t = 0$ and $r$ is the number you multiply by in each period.
  \item From a table, $r$ is the \emph{ratio} of consecutive values. From a percent, $r = 1 + \frac{p}{100}$ for growth and $r = 1 - \frac{p}{100}$ for decay.
  \item Round money, mass and population answers to two decimal places when a decimal appears; keep exact values otherwise.
  \item State the units of $t$ (hours, days, years) before you use a model, and keep them with your answer.
\end{itemize}
