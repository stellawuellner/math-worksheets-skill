# Powers of Ten and Place Value — Grades 4–5

Here are the three PDFs for this fluency set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_powersten_curr102.pdf`) — 12 problems of procedural
  fluency practice on powers-of-ten place-value relationships. It opens with a
  worked pattern (7 × 10 = 70 shown, the next two lines to complete), then
  ramps through multiplying and dividing by 10/100/1,000, exponent form
  (10³, 45 × 10², 8,000 ÷ 10³), and place-value shift comparisons, ending with
  a two-step markers-and-cases word problem. No two problems repeat an
  identical skeleton, and the four subskills are interleaved after the warm-up
  so your child must choose the method each time.
- **Answer key** (`ak_powersten_curr102.pdf`) — a quick-answers bank for fast
  grading, then step-by-step reasoning for each problem (shift-the-digits
  language throughout, not just answers), plus a generated Curriculum section
  (5.NBT.A.2, 4.NBT.A.1) with the difficulty range.
- **Study guide** (`ss_powersten_curr102.pdf`) — four short sections matching
  the four subskills: multiplying by powers of ten, dividing by powers of ten,
  exponent form, and place-value shifts. Each has the rule, one worked example,
  and a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** 15 of the 16 answers are machine-checked with a computer
algebra system — every product, quotient, power, and place-value ratio,
including all three blanks of problem 10. One response is instructor-judged:
problem 9's "explain how you know" sentence, flagged for manual review, with
the answer key stating what a full-credit explanation must contain. The key's
"What is verified" note reflects the same split.
