# See It Fast! Quantities to Five (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **ws_subitize_curr006.pdf** — an 8-problem guided concept worksheet on
  recognizing quantities to 5 without counting one by one. The models are the
  classic subitizing arrangements — dice patterns, five-frames, dominoes, and
  part-part-total layouts — and every model is connected to notation ("__ and
  __ make __", comparison symbols).
- **ak_subitize_curr006.pdf** — the step-by-step answer key. Each solution
  names the pattern being recognized (the square of four, the die's X, the
  almost-full frame) so a parent can coach the seeing, not just mark the
  number. Includes a quick-answers bank and a generated Curriculum section
  (K.CC.B.4, K.CC.B.5, K.CC.C.6).
- **ss_subitize_curr006.pdf** — a one-page study guide with three sections:
  knowing the dot patterns, seeing the parts in a pattern, and the five-frame
  flash — each with a rule, worked example, and try-it.

**What is verified:** 18 of the 19 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every "how many" is checked against the dot data that lays out its pattern,
every part-part-total triple is verified as the addition it shows, empty-box
counts are verified as 5 minus the dots, and the card comparison is verified
as 4 < 5. **One item is instructor-judged:** the challenge asks the child to
tell how they saw the quantity, and the key's rubric says what counts (any
parts-based seeing; counting all five one by one does not show subitizing).
The study guide's examples and try-its are machine-verified the same way.
