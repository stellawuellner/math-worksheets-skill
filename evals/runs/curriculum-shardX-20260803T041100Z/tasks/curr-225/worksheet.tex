\noindent\textbf{Read a table or graph, find the rate of change, and build the
equation that matches the story.}

\smallskip
\noindent\small The same linear relationship can arrive as a story, a table, an
equation, or a graph. Every problem below asks you to move between two of those
forms, so decide first \emph{which} form you have and \emph{which} you need.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Rate of change} (slope) $=\dfrac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}$
        --- from any two points of the relationship, however they are given.
  \item \textbf{Initial value} (the $y$-intercept) is the value when $x$ is
        zero: where the graph crosses the vertical axis, or the row of the
        table with a zero input.
  \item \textbf{Equation:} $y = (\text{rate}) \cdot x + (\text{initial value})$.
        Use it forwards to predict a $y$, and solve it backwards to find an $x$.
\end{itemize}
Show the two points or the substitution you used, then write the answer on the
answer line.\normalsize

\problem{A taxi company posts this fare table. The distance is in miles and the
fare is in dollars.
\par\smallskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Miles} & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\ \hline
\textbf{Fare}  & 9 & 14 & 19 & 24 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=2.8cm, leftmargin=1.5cm, topsep=4pt]
  \item What is the fare for a ride of 3 miles? \ansline
  \item What is the rate of change of the fare, in dollars per mile? \ansline
\end{enumerate}

\par\smallskip
Write an equation for the total cost, then use it to find the total cost after

\problem[5.5cm]{The line in \textbf{Graph C} has the equation $y = 4x$.

\problem{A tank is draining. The table shows the volume of water left, in
litres, after each minute.
\par\smallskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Minutes} & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\ \hline
\textbf{Litres}  & 60 & 48 & 36 & 24 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=2.8cm, leftmargin=1.5cm, topsep=4pt]
  \item How much water is left after 2 minutes? \ansline
  \item What is the rate of change, in litres per minute? \ansline
\end{enumerate}

\problem[6cm]{One of the three reference graphs is the graph of the equation
$y = 12 - 2x$.
\par\smallskip
Substitute $x = 5$ into that equation and write the resulting $y$-value on the
answer line. Then name the graph the equation matches, and explain how the
point you found proves it.}

\problem[5.5cm]{A different tank is filling at a steady rate. After $3$ minutes
it holds $26$ litres; after $8$ minutes it holds $51$ litres.
\par\smallskip
Find the rate at which the tank is filling, in litres per minute.}

\problem{A seedling is $12$ centimetres tall and grows $3$ centimetres each
week. Let $w$ be the number of weeks from now.
\par\smallskip
Write an equation for the seedling's height, then use it to predict the height
after $9$ weeks.
\par\vspace*{5.5cm}
\answerline{cm}}

\problem{The table below shows a linear relationship.
\par\smallskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$ & 0 & 2 & 4 & 6 \\ \hline
$y$ & 7 & 13 & 19 & 25 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=2.8cm, leftmargin=1.5cm, topsep=4pt]
  \item Read the value of $y$ when $x$ is 4. \ansline
  \item Find the rate of change of this relationship. \ansline
\end{enumerate}

\problem[6cm]{\textbf{Compare two representations.} \textbf{Graph A} and
\textbf{Graph C} are drawn on identical grids.
\par\smallskip
Find the rate of change of \textbf{Graph C}, then state which of the two lines
climbs faster and how you can see that on the grids without computing
anything.}

\problem{\textbf{Challenge.} A phone plan charges a fixed monthly fee plus a
charge for each gigabyte of data used. Two months of bills are shown, with data
in gigabytes and the bill in dollars.
\par\smallskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Gigabytes} & 2 & 5 \\ \hline
\textbf{Bill}      & 31 & 46 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=2.8cm, leftmargin=1.5cm, topsep=4pt]
  \item Find the charge per gigabyte. \ansline
  \item Build the equation for the bill, then find how many gigabytes give a
        bill of $61$ dollars. \ansline
\end{enumerate}
