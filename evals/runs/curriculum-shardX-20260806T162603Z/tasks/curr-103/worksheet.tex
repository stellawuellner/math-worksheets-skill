\begin{center}\textit{Rounding on a number line, by the digit rule, and in real
contexts}\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions:} Problems 1--2 give you a labeled number line.
Every other problem has a blank number line you may label and use if it helps.
Write each final answer on its answer blank.

\problem[2.5cm]{The number line shows 362 between 300 and 400.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1.0]
  \draw[thick, <->] (-0.4,0) -- (8.4,0);
  \foreach \x/\lab in {0/300, 4/350, 8/400}{
    \draw[thick] (\x,-0.12) -- (\x,0.12);
    \node[below=3pt] at (\x,0) {\lab};
  }
  \fill (4.96,0) circle (2.5pt);
  \node[above=3pt] at (4.96,0) {362};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Which hundred is 362 closer to? Round 362 to the nearest hundred.}
