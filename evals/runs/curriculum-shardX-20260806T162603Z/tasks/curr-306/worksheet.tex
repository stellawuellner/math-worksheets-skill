\wstitleblock{Naming the Angle Pairs a Transversal Makes}{High-school geometry}{}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item In every figure below, the double arrowheads on two lines mean those lines are \textbf{parallel}. Where the marks are missing, you may not assume it.
  \item \textbf{Interior} angles lie between the two lines; \textbf{exterior} angles lie outside them. ``Alternate'' means opposite sides of the transversal; ``same-side'' means the same side.
  \item Name the pair first, then write what the name gives you --- congruent (equal measures) or supplementary (measures adding to $180$) --- and only then compute.
\end{itemize}
