\wstitleblock{Sorting Four-Sided Shapes by What They Share}{Grades 2--3 Mathematics}{}

\noindent\small
Sorting four-sided shapes by square corners, by side lengths, and by parallel
sides, and reading a shape-sort graph. Ms.\ Diaz's class made a set of shape
cards. Every card has four straight sides.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item A \textbf{square corner} is a corner shaped like the corner of a piece
        of paper. Use the corner of your paper to test one.
  \item Two sides are \textbf{parallel} when they run the same way and would
        never bump into each other, like two rails of a train track.
  \item Shapes are drawn the true size they were cut, so you can compare
        side lengths by looking and by measuring.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize
