\wstitleblock{Where Limit Work Goes Wrong}{AP Calculus AB/BC}{}

\noindent\textbf{Directions:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Two errors account for most wrong limits: substituting when substitution is not allowed, and deciding an infinite limit from the numerator's sign alone.
  \item Before you compute anything, run the two checks: is the denominator zero at the target? and if so, what is the sign of the denominator on the side you are approaching from?
  \item In the find-and-fix problems, the printed work is a real student's. Give the correct answer \emph{and} say what the mistake was --- both count.
\end{itemize}
