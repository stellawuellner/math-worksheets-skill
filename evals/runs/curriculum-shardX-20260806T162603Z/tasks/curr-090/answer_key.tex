% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 1
\problem{Sam's recipe should take 40 minutes in all. He stirred for 5 minutes
and baked for 30 minutes. How many minutes are left for cooling?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- time already used: $5 + 30 = 35$ minutes.
Step 2 --- take that from the whole recipe time: $40 - 35 = 5$.
Cooling gets \ans{5\ \text{minutes}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 2
\problem{Rosa buys a pencil for 35 cents and an eraser for 25 cents. She pays
with a one-dollar bill (100 cents). How many cents does she get back?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- what the two items cost together:
$35 + 25 = 60$ cents. Step 2 --- change from the dollar: $100 - 60 = 40$.
Rosa gets back \ans{40} cents.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 3
\problem{Mrs.\ Diaz buys 12 kg of apples and 8 kg of pears. Her cart can hold
30 kg. How many more kilograms can she add?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- mass already in the cart: $12 + 8 = 20$ kg.
Step 2 --- room left: $30 - 20 = 10$. She can add \ans{10\ \text{kg}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 4
\problem{A soup pot holds 6 L. The chef pours in 2 L of broth and 3 L of water.
How many more liters can the pot hold?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- liquid already in the pot: $2 + 3 = 5$ L.
Step 2 --- space left: $6 - 5 = 1$. The pot can hold \ans{1\ \text{L}} more.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 5
\problem{Gym class is 45 minutes. The class plays 3 rounds of 10 minutes each.
How many minutes are not spent on the game?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- game time as equal groups:
$3 \times 10 = 30$ minutes. Step 2 --- the rest of class:
$45 - 30 = 15$. That leaves \ans{15\ \text{minutes}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 6
\problem{Sticker packs cost 4 dollars each. Jon buys 3 packs and pays with a
20-dollar bill. How many dollars does he get back?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- cost of the packs: $3 \times 4 = 12$ dollars.
Step 2 --- change: $20 - 12 = 8$. Jon gets back \ans{8} dollars.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 7
\problem{One can of beans is 400 g. Talia buys 2 cans and one 350 g jar of
sauce. What is the total mass, in grams?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- mass of the cans: $2 \times 400 = 800$ g.
Step 2 --- add the jar: $800 + 350 = 1150$. The groceries have a mass of
\ans{1150\ \text{g}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 8
\problem{A 24 L aquarium is filled using 4 equal buckets. After 3 buckets, how
many liters are still needed?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- size of one bucket: $24 \div 4 = 6$ L.
Step 2 --- three buckets poured means $3 \times 6 = 18$ L are in, so
$24 - 18 = 6$ liters remain to pour. Still needed: \ans{6\ \text{L}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 9
\problem{Train A's ride takes 48 minutes. Train B takes 29 minutes plus a
12-minute wait. How many minutes longer is Train A than Train B's whole trip?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- Train B's whole trip: $29 + 12 = 41$ minutes.
Step 2 --- compare: $48 - 41 = 7$. Train A is longer by \ans{7\ \text{minutes}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 10
\problem{Kane saves 5 dollars each week for 4 weeks. The game costs 32 dollars.
How many more dollars does he need?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- saved so far: $4 \times 5 = 20$ dollars.
Step 2 --- still missing: $32 - 20 = 12$. Kane needs \ans{12} more dollars.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 11
\problem{One brick is 3 kg and one board is 7 kg. The wagon carries 4 bricks
and one board. What is the total mass of the load?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- mass of the bricks: $4 \times 3 = 12$ kg.
Step 2 --- add the board: $12 + 7 = 19$. The load has a mass of
\ans{19\ \text{kg}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 12
\problem{Challenge. Papa makes 18 L of lemonade. He fills 5 jugs of 2 L each,
then 4 bottles of 1 L each. How many liters are left over?}

\textbf{Solution:} Step 1 --- lemonade poured into jugs: $5 \times 2 = 10$ L.
Step 2 --- poured into bottles: $4 \times 1 = 4$ L, so $10 + 4 = 14$ L are
poured in all, and $18 - 14 = 4$ liters remain. Left over: \ans{4\ \text{L}}
