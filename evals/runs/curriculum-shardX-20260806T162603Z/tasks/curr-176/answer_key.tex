\problem{Solve (balance model): \quad $x + 7 = 12$}

\textbf{Solution:} Undo the $+\,7$ by subtracting 7 from both sides (removing 7 unit
weights from both pans): $x = 12 - 7$. Check: $5 + 7 = 12$. \checkmark \ans{x = 5}

\problem{Solve (tape model): \quad $\dfrac{x}{5} = 4$}

\textbf{Solution:} The strip $x$ is 5 equal parts of 4 each, so undo the division by
multiplying both sides by 5: $x = 4 \cdot 5$. Check: $20 \div 5 = 4$. \checkmark \ans{x = 20}

\problem{Solve: \quad $x - \dfrac{3}{4} = 2$}

\textbf{Solution:} Undo the subtraction by adding $\tfrac{3}{4}$ to both sides:
$x = 2 + \tfrac{3}{4}$. Check: $\tfrac{11}{4} - \tfrac{3}{4} = \tfrac{8}{4} = 2$.
\checkmark \ans{x = \tfrac{11}{4}}

\problem{Solve, writing the answer as a fraction: \quad $6x = 21$}

\textbf{Solution:} Undo the multiplication by dividing both sides by 6:
$x = \tfrac{21}{6}$, which simplifies by 3 to $\tfrac{7}{2}$. Check:
$6 \cdot \tfrac{7}{2} = 21$. \checkmark \ans{x = \tfrac{7}{2}}

\problem{Solve: \quad $0.4x = 10$}

\textbf{Solution:} Divide both sides by $0.4$: $x = 10 \div 0.4 = 25$. Check:
$0.4 \cdot 25 = 10$. \checkmark \ans{x = 25}

\problem{Solve (bar model): \quad $\dfrac{2}{3}x = 8$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiplying by the reciprocal of $\tfrac{2}{3}$ undoes it in one
step: $x = 8 \cdot \tfrac{3}{2} = 12$. (Model view: 2 parts total 8, so each part is
4, and the 3 parts of the whole bar total 12.) Check: $\tfrac{2}{3} \cdot 12 = 8$.
\checkmark \ans{x = 12}

\problem{Solve: \quad $\dfrac{x}{2.5} = 6$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiply both sides by $2.5$: $x = 6 \cdot 2.5 = 15$. Check:
$15 \div 2.5 = 6$. \checkmark \ans{x = 15}

\problem{Solve, writing the answer as a fraction in simplest form:
\quad $\dfrac{3}{4}x = \dfrac{9}{10}$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiply both sides by the reciprocal $\tfrac{4}{3}$:
\[ x = \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{36}{30} = \frac{6}{5}. \]
Check: $\tfrac{3}{4} \cdot \tfrac{6}{5} = \tfrac{18}{20} = \tfrac{9}{10}$.
\checkmark \ans{x = \tfrac{6}{5}}

\problem{Solve: \quad $x + 2.6 = 1.4$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $2.6$ from both sides: $x = 1.4 - 2.6$. Subtracting a
larger number from a smaller one gives a negative result. Check:
$-1.2 + 2.6 = 1.4$. \checkmark \ans{x = -1.2}

\problem{\textbf{Challenge.} Two-fifths of a number $x$ is 18. (a) write the
equation; (b) solve it.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) ``Two-fifths \emph{of} the number'' means multiplication, so
the sentence in symbols is $\tfrac{2}{5}x = 18$ (also fine: $2x/5 = 18$ or
$0.4x = 18$ --- instructor judges the equation).

(b) Multiply both sides by the reciprocal $\tfrac{5}{2}$:
$x = 18 \cdot \tfrac{5}{2} = 45$. Check: $\tfrac{2}{5} \cdot 45 = 18$.
\checkmark \ans{x = 45}
