# Make Ten Practice (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_maketen_curr020.pdf`) — 10 problems on using composition
  and decomposition to make 10. It opens with a short blocked warm-up
  (ten-frame partners to ten), then interleaves the three subskills —
  missing partner to ten, decomposing ten, and making ten to add — so your
  child has to choose a method on each problem, and it finishes with a
  synthesis challenge: use the partner 6 + 4 to add three numbers quickly.
- **Answer key** (`ak_maketen_curr020.pdf`) — worked reasoning for every
  problem (the make-ten moves written out step by step), a quick-answers
  bank, and a curriculum section (K.OA.A and 1.OA.C.6, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_maketen_curr020.pdf`) — one section per subskill:
  partner pairs to ten, decomposing ten, and making ten to add, each with a
  rule box, a worked example, and a fresh try-it.

**What is verified:** 11 of the 12 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every partner, every decomposition, every sum, including both blanks of the
challenge problem. The one open response ("tell how making ten helped you
add") is flagged for instructor review, and the answer key states what a
good explanation contains. The key's "What is verified" note reflects this
same split.
