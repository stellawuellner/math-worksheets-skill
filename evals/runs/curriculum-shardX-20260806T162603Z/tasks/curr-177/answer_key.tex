\problem{Solve and check: \quad $2x + 3 = 11$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract 3 from both sides: $2x = 8$. Divide by 2: $x = 4$.
Check: $2(4) + 3 = 11$. \checkmark \ans{x = 4}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $3x - 5 = 16$}

\textbf{Solution:} Add 5 to both sides: $3x = 21$. Divide by 3: $x = 7$.
Check: $3(7) - 5 = 16$. \checkmark \ans{x = 7}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $\dfrac{x}{4} + 2 = 7$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract 2 from both sides: $\tfrac{x}{4} = 5$. Multiply by 4:
$x = 20$. Check: $20 \div 4 + 2 = 7$. \checkmark \ans{x = 20}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $5x + 9 = -6$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract 9 from both sides: $5x = -15$. Divide by 5: $x = -3$.
Check: $5(-3) + 9 = -6$. \checkmark \ans{x = -3}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $\dfrac{x}{3} - 8 = -2$}

\textbf{Solution:} Add 8 to both sides: $\tfrac{x}{3} = 6$. Multiply by 3: $x = 18$.
Check: $18 \div 3 - 8 = -2$. \checkmark \ans{x = 18}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $10 - 2x = 4$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract 10 from both sides: $-2x = -6$. Divide by $-2$
(dividing by a negative keeps the equation balanced): $x = 3$.
Check: $10 - 2(3) = 4$. \checkmark \ans{x = 3}

\problem{Nina says $x = 6$ solves $4x - 7 = 17$. (a) evaluate $4x - 7$ at $x = 6$;
(b) is she correct?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) $4(6) - 7 = 24 - 7 = 17$. \ans{17}

(b) Model answer: yes --- the left side comes out to 17, exactly the right side of
the equation, so $x = 6$ makes the equation true and is the solution. (Instructor
judges the sentence; it must connect the matching value to ``is a solution.'')

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $7 = 2x - 9$}

\textbf{Solution:} The variable is on the right --- the steps are the same. Add 9 to
both sides: $16 = 2x$. Divide by 2: $x = 8$. Check: $2(8) - 9 = 7$. \checkmark \ans{x = 8}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $\dfrac{x + 5}{3} = 4$}

\textbf{Solution:} The \emph{whole} sum $x + 5$ is divided by 3, so multiply both
sides by 3 first: $x + 5 = 12$. Subtract 5: $x = 7$.
Check: $(7 + 5) \div 3 = 4$. \checkmark \ans{x = 7}

\problem{Solve and check: \quad $-4x + 3 = 19$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract 3 from both sides: $-4x = 16$. Divide by $-4$:
$x = -4$. Check: $-4(-4) + 3 = 16 + 3 = 19$. \checkmark \ans{x = -4}

\problem{Leo solves $4x - 6 = 18$ and gets $x = 3$. (a) check his answer;
(b) solve correctly; (c) name his error.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) $4(3) - 6 = 12 - 6 = 6$, not 18 --- so $x = 3$ fails the
check. \ans{6}

(b) Add 6 to both sides: $4x = 24$. Divide by 4: $x = 6$.
Check: $4(6) - 6 = 18$. \checkmark \ans{x = 6}

(c) Model answer: Leo \emph{subtracted} 6 from both sides instead of adding it ---
to undo $-\,6$ you add 6, so he computed $(18 - 6) \div 4 = 3$ instead of
$(18 + 6) \div 4$. (Instructor judges the sentence.)

\problem{\textbf{Challenge.} Bike rental: flat fee 4 dollars plus 2.50 dollars per
hour, total 16.50 dollars: solve $2.5h + 4 = 16.5$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract 4 from both sides: $2.5h = 12.5$. Divide by $2.5$:
$h = 5$. Check: $2.5(5) + 4 = 12.5 + 4 = 16.5$. \checkmark So Amara rented for 5
hours. \ans{h = 5}
