\begin{center}\small\textit{Finding the third angle first, then solving AAS and ASA triangles with the law of sines}\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{The tool.} \ $\dfrac{a}{\sin A} = \dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{c}{\sin C}$ \quad
(each side is named by the angle \emph{opposite} it; $A + B + C = 180^\circ$). Every figure
is drawn to scale from the given data, with the requested quantity marked $?$.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{AAS} --- the given side is opposite a given angle: one side--angle pair is already complete, so use the law of sines at once.
  \item \textbf{ASA} --- the given side lies \emph{between} the given angles: find the third angle first, then use the law of sines.
  \item Round side lengths to two decimal places; keep full precision until the last step.
\end{itemize}
