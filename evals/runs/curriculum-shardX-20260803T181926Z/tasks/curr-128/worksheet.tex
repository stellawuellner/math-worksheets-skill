\wstitleblock{Adding and Subtracting Mixed Numbers}{Grades 4--5 \quad Number and Operations---Fractions}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Adding mixed numbers with like denominators, subtracting mixed numbers
by regrouping, and rewriting with a common denominator before adding or
subtracting}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Before you start.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
\item Add or subtract the whole numbers and the fraction parts separately, then
put the two results back together.
\item Fractions can only be combined when the denominators match. If they do
not, rewrite both fractions with a common denominator first.
\item If the fraction part of a sum is bigger than one whole, trade it for a
whole and keep the rest.
\item If the fraction you are subtracting is bigger than the one you have,
regroup: take one whole from the whole-number part and add it to the fraction.
\item Write your answer in simplest form, with the unit the story uses.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem{Rosa walked $2\tfrac{3}{8}$ miles before lunch and $1\tfrac{1}{8}$
miles after lunch. How far did she walk in all?
\par\vspace*{3.5cm}\answerline{miles}}

\problem{A recipe uses $1\tfrac{5}{12}$ cups of orange juice and
$3\tfrac{1}{12}$ cups of apple juice. How much juice does the recipe use
altogether?
\par\vspace*{3.5cm}\answerline{cups}}

\problem{Devon practised piano for $2\tfrac{5}{6}$ hours on Saturday and
$1\tfrac{4}{6}$ hours on Sunday. How many hours did he practise over the two
days? Give your answer in simplest form.
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{hours}}

\problem{A bag of birdseed held $5\tfrac{1}{4}$ pounds. A gardener poured out
$2\tfrac{3}{4}$ pounds. How many pounds of birdseed are left in the bag?
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{pounds}}

\problem{A pitcher held some water. After $3\tfrac{2}{5}$ cups were poured
out, $4\tfrac{3}{5}$ cups were still in the pitcher. Write an equation with a
letter for the starting amount, then find how many cups the pitcher held at the
start.
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{cups}}

\problem{A soup pot already holds $1\tfrac{1}{2}$ cups of broth. A cook adds
$2\tfrac{1}{3}$ cups more. How much broth is in the pot now?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{cups}}

\problem{A bike path is $4\tfrac{3}{4}$ miles long. Priya has ridden
$1\tfrac{2}{3}$ miles of it. How much of the path does she still have to ride?
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{miles}}

\problem{A rain barrel held $7\tfrac{1}{6}$ gallons. After a gardener used
some of the water, $3\tfrac{5}{6}$ gallons were left. Write an equation with a
letter for the amount used, then find how many gallons were used.
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{gallons}}

\problem{Sam had to find $6\tfrac{1}{8} - 2\tfrac{5}{8}$ for the length of a
piece of trim, in inches. He wrote $4\tfrac{4}{8}$ inches. Explain in one
sentence what Sam did wrong in the fraction column, then find the correct
length in simplest form.
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{inches}}

\problem{A bread recipe calls for $3\tfrac{1}{4}$ cups of flour. Ana has only
$1\tfrac{2}{3}$ cups in the jar. How many more cups of flour does she need?
Show the common denominator you used.
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{cups}}
