\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Law of cosines: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos C$, and the same shape with $a$ or $b$ in front. Side $a$ is opposite angle $A$, and so on.
  \item Rearranged for an angle: $\cos C = \dfrac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{2ab}$.
  \item Every triangle is drawn to scale from its own given values; the unknown is marked with a question mark.
  \item Round sides and angles to two decimal places. Keep full precision inside the calculation and round once, at the end.
\end{itemize}
