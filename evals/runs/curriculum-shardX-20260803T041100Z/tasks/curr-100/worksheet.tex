\noindent\small
Reading a bar graph, reading a line plot, comparing and measuring data, and
sorting shapes by their sides and corners. Use the three displays below.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Look first, then decide.} Some questions ask you to read one
        bar or one stack of X marks. Others ask you to add, to compare, or to
        sort shapes. Decide which one the question wants before you write.
  \item \textbf{Show how you got it.} Write the numbers you used, not just the
        answer.
  \item \textbf{Sides and corners.} A shape with 4 sides always has 4 corners.
        A square needs 4 equal sides \emph{and} 4 square corners.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize
