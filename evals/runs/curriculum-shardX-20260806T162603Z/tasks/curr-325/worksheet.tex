\begin{center}
{\small\textit{choosing between the Pythagorean theorem, special right triangles and trigonometry}}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Decide first \emph{which tool the givens allow}, then compute.
        Three sides and no angle is Pythagoras; a $45^\circ$ or a
        $30^\circ$/$60^\circ$ angle gives an exact special-triangle ratio; any
        other angle needs sine, cosine or tangent.
  \item Round lengths to two decimal places and angles to two decimal places.
        Write the unit on every answer.
  \item Each diagram is drawn to scale from that problem's own givens.
\end{itemize}
