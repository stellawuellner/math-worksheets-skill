# Evaluating Functions from Rules, Tables, and Graphs — Grade 8 / Pre-Algebra

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_functions_curr232.pdf`, 5 pages) — 12 problems in two parts.
  Part A (problems 1–7) works from rules and tables; Part B (problems 8–12) works
  from graphs, and each of those problems carries its own coordinate grid so no
  graph can be mistaken for a neighbour's. The ramp runs from a one-step
  substitution (`f(x) = 4x - 3`, find `f(5)`) up to a graph-in-context synthesis
  problem about a growing seedling.
- **Answer key** (`ak_functions_curr232.pdf`, 3 pages) — a Quick Answers bank, a
  "What is verified" note, a curriculum block with the standards codes, and a
  worked solution for every problem that shows the substitution or the point
  actually read, not just the number.
- **Study guide** (`ss_functions_curr232.pdf`, 2 pages) — four sections, one per
  skill the worksheet tests: putting an input into a rule, reading a table,
  reading a graph, and comparing two functions at the same input. Each section has
  a rule box, a worked example with a strategy step, and a try-it whose answer is
  printed upside down inside the box.

## What is verified

**10 of the 12 problems are fully machine-checked** by SymPy — 15 individual
checks in all, since several problems ask for more than one response and every
printed blank has its own check. The two remaining problems are **partly
instructor-judged**:

- **Problem 7** — the value `f(-4) = -10` is machine-checked; the written reason
  for why `f(-4)` is not `-f(4)` is marked `---` in the Quick Answers bank and
  carries a grading rubric in the key.
- **Problem 12** — the height `w(3) = 24 cm` is machine-checked; the sentence
  interpreting what that value means about the seedling is instructor-judged, with
  a rubric in the key.

That is the same split the key's own "What is verified" note prints. Nothing else
on the sheet is unchecked: the answer-slot gate confirmed all 18 printed blanks
have a corresponding verification entry.

## Notes for you

- The grade level prints on the answer key only — it is deliberately absent from
  the worksheet and the study guide.
- Problem 7 is the classic `f(-4)` vs `-f(4)` confusion and problem 6 makes the
  student compare a rule against a table at the same input; both are worth
  discussing even if the arithmetic is right.
- Standards covered: 8.F.A (problems 1–11) and 8.F.B (problem 12).
