\problem{Write the number the model shows. (1 flat, 3 rods, 5 cubes)}

\textbf{Solution:} The model shows 1 flat (1 hundred), 3 rods (3 tens), and
5 cubes (5 ones). That is $100 + 30 + 5$. \ans{135}

\problem{A model shows 2 hundreds, 4 tens, and 6 ones. Write the number.}

\textbf{Solution:} 2 hundreds is $200$, 4 tens is $40$, 6 ones is $6$.
Together: $200 + 40 + 6$. \ans{246}

\problem{Write the number the model shows. (3 flats, no rods, 8 cubes)}

\textbf{Solution:} The model has 3 flats (3 hundreds) and 8 cubes (8 ones)
--- and \emph{no} rods at all, so the tens place holds a zero. The number is
$300 + 0 + 8$. Watch for the zero: the answer is not 38. \ans{308}

\problem{Fill in the blanks for the number 573.}

\textbf{Solution:} Read each digit by its place: the 5 sits in the hundreds
place, the 7 in the tens place, and the 3 in the ones place.
So 573 has \ans{5} hundreds, \ans{7} tens, and \ans{3} ones.

\problem{What is the value of the tens digit in 482?}

\textbf{Solution:} The tens digit of 482 is 8, and a digit in the tens place
is worth ten times itself: $8 \times 10 = 80$. The digit is 8, but its
\emph{value} is \ans{80}

\problem{Model A and Model B: write $<$, $>$, or $=$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Model A shows 2 flats and 5 rods: $200 + 50 = 250$.
Model B shows 2 flats, 3 rods, and 9 cubes: $200 + 30 + 9 = 239$. The
hundreds match (2 and 2), so compare the tens: 5 tens beats 3 tens, and the
9 extra ones cannot make up a missing ten. \ans{250 > 239}

\problem{Fill in the blanks for the number 407.}

\textbf{Solution:} The 4 is in the hundreds place, the 0 is in the tens
place (a placeholder --- there are no loose tens), and the 7 is in the ones
place. So $407 = \ans{4}$ hundreds $+ \ans{0}$ tens $+ \ans{7}$ ones.

\problem{Challenge: a model shows 12 tens and 5 ones. What number is it?
Explain the regrouping.}

\textbf{Solution:} 12 tens is $120$, and 5 ones is $5$, so the model shows
$120 + 5 = 125$. \ans{125}

\emph{What a good explanation contains:} ten of the twelve tens are traded
for one hundred flat, which leaves 2 rods. After the trade the model shows
1 hundred, 2 tens, and 5 ones --- the same amount, regrouped.
