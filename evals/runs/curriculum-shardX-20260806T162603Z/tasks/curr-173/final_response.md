# Equivalent Expressions: Distribute, Combine, Factor (Grades 6-7)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_expr_curr173.pdf`) — 10 problems on the distributive
  property and combining like terms, taught through representations and
  applications: a garden-bed area model that shows why 3(x + 5) = 3x + 15, a
  triangle-perimeter problem answered in centimeters on a labeled unit line,
  and a deli-cost problem written both as a product and as a sum. Givens and
  units are stated explicitly in each story, with no decorative numbers. The
  sheet ramps from a one-step expansion to two challenge problems that mix
  distribution with combining like terms.
- **Answer key** (`ak_expr_curr173.pdf`) — full reasoning for every problem
  (which property was applied at each step), a quick-answers column, and a
  generated curriculum section with the grade band and standard (7.EE.A). The
  perimeter answer prints its verified cm unit.
- **Study guide** (`ss_expr_curr173.pdf`) — a two-page card with one section
  per skill: distributing (with the rectangle-area picture), combining like
  terms, and factoring out the GCF, each with a rule box, a worked example that
  names its strategy, a try-it with the answer upside down, and watch-outs for
  sign errors.

**What is verified:** 11 of the 12 responses the worksheet asks for are
machine-checked with a computer algebra system (every expansion, factorization,
and simplification, including both forms in the two model problems, plus all 6
study-guide examples/try-its). One response is instructor-judged: problem 2(c),
the one-sentence explanation of why the area model proves the two expressions
equal — the answer key states what a correct sentence must contain. The key's
"What is verified" note reflects this same split.
