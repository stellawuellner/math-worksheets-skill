Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 percent-of-a-quantity concept practice.

- **Worksheet** (`ws_pct_of_quantity_curr161.pdf`) — 10 problems, every one of
  them asking "how much is this percent of this amount?" through a named model.
  Three models run through the sheet and are deliberately interleaved after the
  warm-up: a **hundredths grid** (the whole cut into 100 equal parts, so one
  part is one percent), a **ten-percent tape strip** (build 15% or 60% out of
  strips and half-strips, no calculator), and the **decimal multiplier** (divide
  the percent by 100, then multiply). The last problem is a reasoning item: a
  claim that 20% of $150 must beat 25% of $100 "because 150 is bigger."
- **Answer key** (`ak_pct_of_quantity_curr161.pdf`) — a Quick Answers bank with
  every sub-part labelled by name, a generated Curriculum section (6.RP.A.3.c,
  difficulty 1–5), and worked solutions that show the model first and the
  notation second, so a child who used the grid and a child who multiplied both
  see their own route.
- **Study guide** (`ss_pct_of_quantity_curr161.pdf`) — one section per model:
  rule box, a two-step worked example, and a try-it with the answer printed
  upside down inside the box. Plus one watch-out on the mistake that costs the
  most marks: writing 8% as 0.8 instead of 0.08.

**What is verified.** The sheet asks for 19 separate responses. **18 are
machine-checked** with SymPy — every percent amount, every decimal conversion,
every ten-percent strip value, recomputed from the givens rather than compared
against a typed answer. **1 is instructor-judged**: part (c) of problem 10, the
written explanation of whether the reasoning holds. The answer key marks that
one with `---` in the Quick Answers bank and says in the worked solution exactly
what a correct response must contain (the comparison of the two computed
amounts, *and* the point that comparing only the two starting amounts is not
enough). All ten study-guide checks — three worked examples and three try-its —
are machine-verified.
