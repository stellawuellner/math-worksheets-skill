\begin{center}
\textit{Simplifying and finding the domain, intercepts, holes and vertical asymptotes, and horizontal asymptotes from the degrees}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Problems 1--4 warm up one skill at a time. From problem 5 on they are mixed, so decide for yourself which tool each one needs.
  \item Always factor the numerator and the denominator first. A cancelled factor gives a \textbf{hole}; a surviving denominator factor gives a \textbf{vertical asymptote}.
  \item An $x$-intercept is a zero of the \emph{simplified} function; the $y$-intercept is the value at $x = 0$.
  \item A horizontal asymptote is decided by the degrees: equal degrees give the ratio of the leading coefficients.
\end{itemize}
