# Adding Story Problems (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_addstories_curr023.pdf`) — 8 add-to result-unknown story
  problems within 10. Every story states its givens and its unit plainly
  (crayons, frogs, shells, buttons, pages, ants, stamps) with no distracting
  extra detail. Three representations rotate through the sheet: acting the
  story out with a drawing (one problem includes a ten-frame to draw in),
  writing the equation from the story numbers into fill-in blanks, and
  counting on — including a count-on-from-the-bigger-number problem and a
  three-addend challenge.
- **Answer key** (`ak_addstories_curr023.pdf`) — reasoning for every problem
  (the count-on sequences written out), a quick-answers bank, and a
  curriculum section (K.OA.A and 1.OA.C.6, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_addstories_curr023.pdf`) — three sections: act out the
  story, write the equation, and count on to solve, each with a rule box, a
  worked example, and a fresh try-it, plus a watch-out on the count-on
  slip-up.

**What is verified:** 8 of the 11 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every total, on every answer line, including the challenge's three-addend
sum; the units on the answer lines are also bound to the verification data.
The 3 open responses (the two instructed drawings on problems 1 and 5, and
"tell how you found it" on problem 8) are flagged for instructor review with
the answer key stating what a good response shows. The key's "What is
verified" note reflects the same split.
