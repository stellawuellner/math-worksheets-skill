Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 5 decimal workshop:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_decops_curr138.pdf`) — 10 problems on multiplying and
  dividing decimals through place-value reasoning: powers-of-ten warm-ups,
  tenths-times-tenths reasoning, then realistic applications (notebook prices
  with an estimate-then-exact pair, liters in a watering can, cutting ribbon,
  a 100-box pallet, sharing money, and a buy-milk-and-make-change challenge).
  Units and givens are stated explicitly, and unit answers get labeled answer
  lines (L, kg).
- **Answer key** (`ak_decops_curr138.pdf`) — worked place-value reasoning for
  every problem (why the decimal point moves, why the product gets two decimal
  places), a quick-answers bank, and "common wrong answers" notes for the four
  declared misconception traps (decimal moved the wrong way, one decimal place
  instead of two, shifting only one number in a division).
- **Study guide** (`ss_decops_curr138.pdf`) — three skills matching the sheet
  (powers of ten move the point; multiply then count decimal places; make the
  divisor whole), each with a rule box, worked example, and try-it, plus an
  estimate-first watch-out box.

**What is verified:** all 13 checks on the worksheet (every answer slot,
including both halves of each two-part problem) and all 6 study-guide
examples/try-its are machine-verified with SymPy — nothing on this sheet is
flagged for manual review. The four misconception traps are also
machine-checked to give visibly different numbers from the correct answers.
