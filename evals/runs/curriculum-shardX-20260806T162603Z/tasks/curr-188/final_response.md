Here are the three PDFs for the Grades 6–7 workshop on graphing proportional
relationships through the origin.

**Student worksheet (10 problems).** Every problem carries its own graph or
grid: students read unit rates from real-context graphs (a filling tank, grape
prices, two runners), write equations in the form y = kx from graphed lines (a
remote-control car, custom shirts), and graph from tables (a muffin recipe, a
raffle-ticket table that turns out *not* to be proportional, and a
fill-in-the-missing-entries table). The finale compares a graphed store price
against a competitor's equation. Units and givens are stated in every question,
and each problem has work space beside its graph.

**Answer key.** Step-by-step reasoning for all 10 problems (rise-over-run
computations, ratio tests, substitution checks), a quick-answers bank, and a
generated Curriculum section (standard 7.RP.A.2, difficulty range 1–4).

**Study guide (2 pages).** Three sections matching the worksheet's skills —
reading the unit rate from a graph, writing the equation from a graph, and
graphing from a table — each with a rule box, a worked example, and a try-it
with the answer printed upside-down.

**What is verified.** 15 answers are machine-checked with SymPy (all unit
rates, equations' constants, ratio computations, missing table entries, the
rate comparison, and both application answers), and all 6 study-guide
examples/try-its are machine-checked. Three responses are drawings the machine
cannot grade — the plotted graphs in problems 4, 7, and 9 (problem 7 also
includes the "is it proportional?" judgment) — and are flagged for instructor
review, with the expected graph described in the key.
