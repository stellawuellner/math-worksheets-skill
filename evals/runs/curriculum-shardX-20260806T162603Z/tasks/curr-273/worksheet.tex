\begin{center}
\small Finding the first term and the common difference, writing recursive rules, and extending sequences
\end{center}
\vspace{0.15cm}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item A recursive rule for an arithmetic sequence has two parts: a \textbf{starting value} $a_1$ and a \textbf{step} $a_n = a_{n-1} + d$ for $n \ge 2$. You need both.
  \item None of these problems hands you $a_1$ outright --- find the step first, then walk back to term 1.
  \item The step between term $j$ and term $k$ is spread over $k - j$ jumps, so $d = \dfrac{a_k - a_j}{k - j}$.
  \item Show the arithmetic that gets you each value; a bare number earns no credit.
\end{itemize}
