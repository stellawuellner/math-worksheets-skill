# Naming the Angle Pairs a Transversal Makes — high-school geometry (8 problems)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_transversals_curr306.pdf`, 6 pages) — 8 guided-concept problems, each
  with its own labelled transversal diagram. The sheet works the model and the notation
  together: the first figure carries all eight numbered angles and asks for the *names* of
  two pairs before any arithmetic; later figures carry the algebraic expressions directly
  on the angles they describe. Problem 5 deliberately removes the parallel marks and asks
  which relationships survive — the distinction students most often lose.
- **Answer key** (`ak_transversals_curr306.pdf`, 3 pages) — each pair named with the
  interior/exterior and alternate/same-side reasoning that produced the name, then the
  equation, then the arithmetic with a substitution check.
- **Study guide** (`ss_transversals_curr306.pdf`, 2 pages) — three sections: reading the
  picture and naming the pair, turning the name into an equation (equal versus adds to
  $180$), and chaining two relationships to reach an angle that is not directly paired.
  A watch-out box flags the no-parallel-marks case.
