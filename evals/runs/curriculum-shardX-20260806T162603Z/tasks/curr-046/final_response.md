# Shape Detective: Any Way It Turns (K/Grade 1 geometry)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_shapesid_curr046.pdf`) — 8 guided problems on naming
  2-D shapes no matter how they are turned. Each problem pairs a drawn model
  (a tilted square standing on its corner, a tipped rectangle, an upside-down
  triangle, a rotated hexagon) with the counting that justifies the name:
  count the sides and corners, then name the shape. It builds to Ben's
  "that's a diamond, not a square!" claim and a corners challenge.
- **Answer key** (`ak_shapesid_curr046.pdf`) — reasoning for every count, the
  accepted name for every shape, model explanations for the "say why" parts,
  and a quick-answers column.
- **Study guide** (`ss_shapesid_curr046.pdf`) — three skills: count sides
  and corners, a turned shape keeps its name, and compare shapes by
  counting, each with a rule box, worked example, and try-it, plus a
  watch-out on the "diamond" trap.

**What is verified:** 9 of the 16 responses are machine-checked — every
side/corner count and both arithmetic comparisons. The 7 shape-name and
"say why" responses are word answers, genuinely open at this level, so they
are flagged for instructor judgement; the key states the accepted name or
explanation for each. The key's "What is verified" note reflects the same
split.
