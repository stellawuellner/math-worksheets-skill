\begin{center}
\small\textit{Combining translations, symmetry of regular polygons, reflections in pairs, and naming the single equivalent motion}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Apply the transformations in the order they are written --- the first one acts on the original point, the second on that image.
  \item A regular polygon with $n$ sides has $n$ lines of symmetry, and rotating it by $360^\circ / n$ about its centre maps it exactly onto itself.
  \item Reflecting twice in parallel lines gives a translation; reflecting twice in intersecting lines gives a rotation about the crossing point.
\end{itemize}
