\wstitleblock{Factoring Special Forms in Area and Volume Models}{Algebra 1}{Differences of squares, perfect-square trinomials, and pulling out the common factor first}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every expression below measures a real area or volume, so keep the units in mind as you work: they tell you whether your factors are lengths.
  \item Factor \textbf{completely}. Always check for a common factor before you reach for a special pattern.
  \item Two patterns do all the work here:
        $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$ \quad and \quad $a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2 = (a \pm b)^2$.
  \item Show your work in the space provided, then write your final answer on the answer line.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\problem[5cm]{A square sheet of copper has area $x^2$ square centimetres. A square hole of area $36$ square centimetres is punched out of its middle, leaving $x^2 - 36$ square centimetres of metal. Write the remaining area as a product of two binomials.}

\problem[5cm]{A square patio is tiled so that its area is $x^2 + 10x + 25$ square metres. Factor the area completely so that the side length of the patio, in metres, can be read from the answer.}

\problem[5cm]{A square banner measures $3x$ centimetres on each side. A square logo measuring $7$ centimetres on each side is cut out of it, leaving $9x^2 - 49$ square centimetres of fabric. Write the remaining area as a product of two binomials.}

\problem[5cm]{The floor of a square storage shed has area $4x^2 + 12x + 9$ square feet. Factor the area completely so that the side length of the shed, in feet, can be read from the answer.}

\problem[5cm]{A rectangular deck has area $2x^2 - 50$ square feet. Factor the expression completely. Take out the common factor first.}

\problem[5cm]{A rectangular window pane measures $(x+7)$ inches along one edge and $(x-7)$ inches along the other. Write its area, in square inches, in standard form with no parentheses.}

\problem[5.5cm]{Two square vegetable plots lie side by side. The larger plot has side $5x$ metres and the smaller plot has side $4y$ metres, so the difference of their areas is $25x^2 - 16y^2$ square metres. Write that difference as a product of two binomials.}

\problem[5.5cm]{A square rug and its carpet border together cover $3x^2 - 30x + 75$ square feet. Factor the expression completely: take out the greatest common factor first, then look at what is left.}

\problem[6cm]{The volume of a shipping crate, in cubic inches, is modelled by $x^3 - 16x$, where $x$ is a length in inches. Factor the expression completely so that the three dimensions of the crate can be read from the answer.}

\problem[7cm]{A square window pane has area $16x^2 - 24x + 9$ square inches.
\par\medskip
(a) Factor the area completely, and write the factored form on the answer line.
\par\smallskip
(b) In the space above, explain how the numbers $16$, $24$ and $9$ tell you this expression is a perfect-square trinomial rather than a difference of squares.}
