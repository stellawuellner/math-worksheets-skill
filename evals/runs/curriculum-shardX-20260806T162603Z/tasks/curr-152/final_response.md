Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 equivalent-ratio-tables fluency set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_ratio_tables_curr152.pdf`) — 12 problems, every one built
  around completing or using a ratio table. The ramp starts from the worked
  pattern in the directions (whole-number multipliers), moves through
  unit-rate columns and scaling down by division, then non-whole-number
  multipliers and missing-value proportions, and finishes with a two-machine
  rate comparison and a two-step punch-recipe challenge. No two problems share
  an identical skeleton.
- **Answer key** (`ak_ratio_tables_curr152.pdf`) — quick-answers table, a
  generated curriculum section (6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3, difficulty 1–4), and
  step-by-step solutions that show the multiplier reasoning for every table,
  plus the common wrong answer on the proportion problem 8 (scaling by the
  ratio upside down).
- **Study guide** (`ss_ratio_tables_curr152.pdf`) — three sections: scaling a
  table up or down, building a unit-rate column, and finding a missing value
  with a proportion; each has a rule box, a worked example, and a try-it with
  the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** every one of the 25 answer blanks on the worksheet is
machine-checked with SymPy — each table cell has its own labelled check, so the
quick-answers column tells you which value fills which blank. Nothing on this
sheet needs instructor judgment; there are no open-response items on a fluency
drill. All six study-guide example and try-it answers are machine-checked too.
