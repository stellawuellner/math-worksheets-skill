\begin{center}\small\textit{postulates and theorems, converses, and biconditionals}\end{center}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item A \textbf{postulate} is accepted without proof; a \textbf{theorem} is proved from postulates, definitions, and earlier theorems.
  \item The \textbf{converse} of ``if $P$ then $Q$'' is ``if $Q$ then $P$''. A converse may be true or false, and it has to be judged on its own.
  \item A \textbf{biconditional} (``$P$ if and only if $Q$'') asserts both directions at once, so it is only available when the statement \emph{and} its converse are true.
  \item Every diagram shows labelling only; all measurements are in the problem text.
\end{itemize}
