\problem{Jacket \$$40$, sales tax $6\%$ --- find the tax in dollars. \\
The base is the price, \$$40$, because the tax is charged \emph{on the price}.
``$6\%$ of'' means multiply by $\dfrac{6}{100}$:
$40 \cdot \dfrac{6}{100} = \dfrac{240}{100} = 2.4$. The tax is \$$2.40$.
\ans{2.40}
\par\noindent\emph{Grader's note:} the question asks for the tax only. A student
who wrote $42.40$ answered the total instead --- correct method, wrong quantity.}

\problem{Dinner bill \$$36$, tip $15\%$ --- find the tip in dollars. \\
The base is the bill before the tip, \$$36$.
$36 \cdot \dfrac{15}{100} = \dfrac{540}{100} = 5.4$, so the tip is \$$5.40$.
\ans{5.40}
\par\noindent\emph{Mental-math check:} $10\%$ of $36$ is $3.60$ and $5\%$ is half
of that, $1.80$; $3.60 + 1.80 = 5.40$ $\checkmark$}

\problem{Backpack \$$45$, $20\%$ off --- ratio table and sale price. \\
Completed table: $100\%$ of the price is \$$45$; the sale is
$100 - 20 = 80\%$ of the price. Because percents are proportional to dollars,
$1\%$ is $\dfrac{45}{100} = 0.45$, so $80\%$ is $80 \cdot 0.45 = 36$.
\par\noindent In one step, multiply by the \emph{remaining} fraction:
$45 \cdot \dfrac{100 - 20}{100} = 45 \cdot 0.8 = 36$. The sale price is \$$36.00$.
\ans{36.00}}

\problem{Bike cost \$$80$, markup $35\%$ --- find the selling price in dollars. \\
Markup is added to the shop's cost, so the base is \$$80$ and the multiplier is
$1 + \dfrac{35}{100} = 1.35$. \ $80 \cdot 1.35 = 108$, so the shop sells it for
\$$108.00$. \ans{108.00}
\par\noindent\emph{Structure to point out:} the markup alone is
$80 \cdot 0.35 = 28$ dollars, and $80 + 28 = 108$ --- the same answer in two
steps instead of one.}

\problem{Calculator \$$24.50$, sales tax $7.25\%$ --- find the total paid. \\
Tax is added to the price, so multiply by
$1 + \dfrac{7.25}{100} = 1.0725$: \ $24.50 \cdot 1.0725 = 26.27625$, which rounds
to \$$26.28$ at the nearest cent. \ans{26.28}
\par\noindent\emph{Grader's note:} the tax by itself is
$24.50 \cdot 0.0725 = 1.77625 \approx 1.78$ dollars. A student who wrote
$1.78$ found the tax and forgot to add the price back.}

\problem{Restaurant bill \$$58.40$, tip $18\%$ --- find the total paid. \\
The tip is added to the bill, so the multiplier is
$1 + \dfrac{18}{100} = 1.18$: \ $58.40 \cdot 1.18 = 68.912$, which rounds to
\$$68.91$. \ans{68.91}
\par\noindent\emph{Grader's note:} $10.51$ is the tip alone
($58.40 \cdot 0.18 = 10.512$), not the amount paid.}

\problem{Coat \$$72$, $25\%$ off, then $6\%$ sales tax on the sale price. \\
\textbf{Step 1 --- discount.} Base is the original price:
$72 \cdot \dfrac{100 - 25}{100} = 72 \cdot 0.75 = 54$ dollars.
\par\noindent\textbf{Step 2 --- tax.} The base changes: tax is charged on the
\emph{sale} price, so $54 \cdot 1.06 = 57.24$ dollars. \ans{57.24}
\par\noindent\emph{Grader's note:} $58.32$ is what you get by folding the two
percents into a single $19\%$ change ($72 \cdot 0.81$). That is wrong because
the two percents act on different bases --- the discount on \$$72$, the tax on
\$$54$.}

\problem{Board game cost \$$18.60$, markup $45\%$ --- find the shelf price. \\
Markup is added to cost, so multiply by $1 + \dfrac{45}{100} = 1.45$:
\ $18.60 \cdot 1.45 = 26.97$ dollars. \ans{26.97}
\par\noindent\emph{Grader's note:} $8.37$ is $45\%$ \emph{of} the cost
($18.60 \cdot 0.45$) --- the markup amount, not the shelf price. Add it to the
cost: $18.60 + 8.37 = 26.97$ $\checkmark$}

\problem{Sweater sells for \$$34$ after $15\%$ off --- find the original price. \\
The discount is taken off the \emph{original} price, so the original price is
the base and \$$34$ is $100 - 15 = 85\%$ of it. With $x$ the original price in
dollars:
\[ x \cdot \frac{100 - 15}{100} = 34 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 0.85x = 34
   \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x = \frac{34}{0.85} \]
So the original price was \$$40.00$. \ans{x = 40.00}
\par\noindent\emph{Check:} $15\%$ of $40$ is $6$ dollars, and $40 - 6 = 34$
$\checkmark$ \ Taking $15\%$ off the \$$34$ sale price instead is the classic
error --- it uses the wrong base and gives about $28.90$.}

\problem{Receipt total \$$77.04$ including $7\%$ tax --- find the price before tax. \\
Tax is charged on the pre-tax price, so that price is the base and the receipt
total is $107\%$ of it. With $x$ the price before tax in dollars:
\[ x \cdot \left(1 + \frac{7}{100}\right) = 77.04 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
   1.07x = 77.04 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x = \frac{77.04}{1.07} \]
So the price before tax was \$$72.00$. \ans{x = 72.00}
\par\noindent\emph{Check:} $72 \cdot 0.07 = 5.04$ dollars of tax, and
$72 + 5.04 = 77.04$ $\checkmark$ \ Subtracting $7\%$ of the total
($77.04 \cdot 0.07 = 5.3928$) uses the wrong base and misses by about $0.35$.}
