\begin{center}\small\textit{Reading a picture graph where one picture stands for four, and
a bar graph whose scale counts by twos}\end{center}

\noindent\small\textbf{For the grown-up helping:}
\begin{itemize}[label={$\bullet$}, leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every graph on this sheet has a \emph{key} or a \emph{scale}. That is the whole
        idea: one picture is worth more than one thing, and one grid line is worth more
        than one thing.
  \item Before each answer, ask ``what is one picture worth?'' or ``how much is one grid
        line worth?'' Let the child point at the key and say it out loud.
  \item Skip-counting helps more than multiplying at this age. For a key of four, count
        4, 8, 12, 16, 20 along the row with a finger.
  \item There is space to draw or write under each question. Jottings are welcome.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize
