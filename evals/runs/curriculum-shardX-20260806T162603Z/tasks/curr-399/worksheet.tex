\wstitleblock{Find the Mistake: Signs and Denominators in Conic Equations}{Algebra 2}{}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every problem quotes a value someone actually wrote. Produce the correct value, and where asked, name the move that produced the wrong one.
  \item Standard forms are written with subtractions: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$. A plus inside a bracket means a negative coordinate.
  \item The right-hand side of a circle equation is $r^2$, and the numbers under $x^2$ and $y^2$ in an ellipse or hyperbola are $a^2$ and $b^2$ --- squares, every one of them.
  \item Foci: \emph{subtract} for an ellipse, $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$; \emph{add} for a hyperbola, $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$.
\end{itemize}
