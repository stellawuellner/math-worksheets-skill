\begin{center}
\textit{Factoring and cancelling, telling a hole from a vertical asymptote, and finding the height of a hole}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Factor the numerator and the denominator completely before deciding anything about the graph.
  \item A factor that cancels leaves a \textbf{hole}: one missing point. A factor still in the denominator after cancelling gives a \textbf{vertical asymptote}.
  \item The height of a hole comes from the \emph{cancelled} form, never from substituting into the original quotient.
  \item Four problems show work that a student actually handed in. Find the correct answer first, then say what went wrong.
\end{itemize}
