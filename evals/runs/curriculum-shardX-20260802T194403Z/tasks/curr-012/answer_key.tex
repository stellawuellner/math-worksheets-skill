\problem{Compare $3$ and $7$.

\textbf{Solution:} Count up: $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ --- you say $3$ first, then
keep counting to $7$. The numeral said later is greater, so $7$ is greater
and $3$ is less. The open end of the symbol faces $7$:
$\;$ $\ans{3 < 7}$ \quad ``three is less than seven.''}

\problem{Compare $9$ and $4$.

\textbf{Solution:} Counting up you say $4$ first and $9$ later, so $9$ is the
greater numeral. It is written first here, so the open end of the symbol
faces left, toward $9$: $\;$ $\ans{9 > 4}$ \quad ``nine is greater than
four.''}

\problem{Compare $6$ and $6$.

\textbf{Solution:} Both numerals name the same amount --- six counters and six
counters. Neither is said later when counting, so neither is greater; the
correct symbol is the equal sign: $\;$ $\ans{6 = 6}$.}

\problem{Circle the numeral that is greater: $8$ or $5$.

\textbf{Solution:} Counting up you say $5$ first and $8$ later, so $8$ is the
greater numeral --- circle $8$. Written greater-first, the pair is
$\;$ $\ans{8 > 5}$.}

\problem{Circle the numeral that is less: $7$ or $2$.

\textbf{Solution:} Counting up you say $2$ first, so $2$ is the smaller
numeral --- circle $2$. Written smaller-first, the pair is
$\;$ $\ans{2 < 7}$.}

\problem{Compare $10$ and $8$.

\textbf{Solution:} $10$ has two digits and $8$ has one, but the safe check is
still counting: after $8$ you say $9$, then $10$, so $10$ comes later and is
greater. Do not be tricked by the small $1$ at the front of $10$:
$\;$ $\ans{10 > 8}$.}

\problem{Write $4$, $9$, $6$ from least to greatest.

\textbf{Solution:} Find the one you say first when counting up: $4$. Of the
two left, $6$ is said before $9$. So the order is
$\;$ $\ans{4,\ 6,\ 9}$.}

\problem{Mia starts at $2$ and counts on $3$ more. Write Mia's numeral, then
compare it with $9$.

\textbf{Solution:} Counting on three from $2$: $3, 4, 5$. Mia's numeral is
$\;$ $\ans{5}$. Now compare $5$ with $9$: counting up you say $5$ first and
$9$ later, so $5$ is less: $\;$ $\ans{<}$ \quad (``five is less than nine'').}

\problem{Write $10$, $3$, $7$ from greatest to least.

\textbf{Solution:} Find the one you say last when counting up: $10$. Of the
two left, $7$ is said after $3$. So greatest to least is
$\;$ $\ans{10,\ 7,\ 3}$.}

\problem{Sam's numeral is $1$ more than $6$. Ana's numeral is $1$ less than
$9$. Write both numerals, then compare them.

\textbf{Solution:} One more than $6$ means count on one: Sam's numeral is
$\;$ $\ans{7}$. One less than $9$ means count back one: Ana's numeral is
$\;$ $\ans{8}$. Finish both counts \emph{before} comparing. Counting up you
say $7$ first and $8$ later, so Sam's numeral is less than Ana's:
$\;$ $\ans{<}$.}
