Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 5 mixed decimal review:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_decmix_curr140.pdf`) — 12 problems in an interleaved
  synthesis format: a short blocked warm-up (two estimates, an add, a
  subtract), then the four subskills mixed so the student must choose the
  operation each time (multiply/divide drills, a change-from-a-bill problem, a
  plank-cutting division, a unit-price multiplication), an
  is-the-estimate-high-or-low reasoning item, and a final challenge that runs
  estimate → exact cost → change in one scenario.
- **Answer key** (`ak_decmix_curr140.pdf`) — worked reasoning for every step
  (why decimal points line up, why the product gets two decimal places, why
  both numbers shift in a division), a quick-answers bank with each two-part
  problem's slots labeled, and "common wrong answers" notes for the three
  declared misconception traps.
- **Study guide** (`ss_decmix_curr140.pdf`) — 2 pages, four skills matching
  the sheet's facets (round-and-estimate; line up the point; multiply then
  place the point; make the divisor whole), each with rule box, worked
  example, and try-it.

**What is verified:** 16 of the 17 checks are machine-verified with SymPy —
every numeric answer slot on the worksheet (including all estimate/exact/
change sub-parts and the boxed comparison in problem 11a) and all 8
study-guide examples/try-its. The one open item is problem 11(b), where the
student explains *why* the exact product must beat Ella's estimate; that is a
written justification, flagged for instructor review, with the key stating
what a correct explanation must contain.
