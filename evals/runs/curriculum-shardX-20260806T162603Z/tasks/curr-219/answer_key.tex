\problem{Find the slope through $A(2, 1)$ and $B(6, 9)$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Take both differences from $A$ to $B$: the change in $y$ is
$9 - 1 = 8$ and the change in $x$ is $6 - 2 = 4$. Slope is the $y$ change over
the $x$ change, so $m = 8/4$. \ans{m = 2}

\problem{Jonah: slope through $P(-3, 4)$ and $Q(1, -4)$; he wrote
$m = \dfrac{1 - (-3)}{-4 - 4} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. (a) Correct slope.
(b) Name his error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Change in $y$ from $P$ to $Q$ is $-4 - 4 = -8$; change
in $x$ is $1 - (-3) = 4$. So $m = -8/4$. \ans{m = -2}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} Jonah's two differences are the
right two numbers in the wrong places: he wrote the $x$ change on top and the
$y$ change underneath, so his fraction is the reciprocal of the slope. A
correct response names the inversion (``run over rise instead of rise over
run''). ``He subtracted wrong'' is not enough --- nothing was subtracted
wrongly.

\problem{Mara: slope through $R(5, 2)$ and $S(1, 10)$; she wrote
$m = \dfrac{10 - 2}{5 - 1} = 2$. (a) Correct slope. (b) Name her error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Going from $R$ to $S$, the change in $y$ is
$10 - 2 = 8$ and the change in $x$ is $1 - 5 = -4$. So $m = 8/(-4)$.
\ans{m = -2}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} Mara subtracted in opposite
orders: $S$ minus $R$ on top, $R$ minus $S$ on the bottom. Reversing one
difference and not the other flips the sign. A correct response says the
subtraction must start from the same point in both differences.

\problem{Draining tank; hours 0, 2, 4, 6 against gallons 40, 34, 28, 22. Ravi
says the rate is $-\dfrac{6}{18}$ gallons per hour. (a) Gallons lost from hour
0 to hour 6. (b) Rate of change. (c) Name the error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Read the two ends of the table: at hour 6 there are
22 gallons and at hour 0 there were 40, so the change is $22 - 40$.
\ans{-18}

\textbf{Solution (b):} The rate of change is gallons per hour, so the gallon
change goes on top: $m = \dfrac{-18}{6 - 0}$. \ans{m = -3}

\textbf{Solution (c) --- instructor-judged.} Ravi divided the hour change by
the gallon change, so his ratio is upside down; his units come out as hours
per gallon, not gallons per hour. A correct response names the inversion, and
a units check is full credit on its own.

\problem{A printer prints 300 pages in 12 minutes. Sam wrote
$m = \dfrac{12}{300} = 0.04$. (a) Correct slope in pages per minute.
(b) Name his error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Pages against minutes means pages on top:
$m = \dfrac{300 - 0}{12 - 0}$. \ans{m = 25}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} Sam divided minutes by pages,
which answers a different question --- minutes per page. A correct response
says pages belong on top because the slope asked for is pages per minute; a
units check earns full credit.

\problem{Ramp through $(1, 9)$ and $(7, 3)$. Aisha wrote
$m = \dfrac{9 - 3}{7 - 1} = 1$. (a) Correct slope. (b) Name her error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} From the first point to the second, $y$ goes
$3 - 9 = -6$ while $x$ goes $7 - 1 = 6$, so $m = -6/6$. \ans{m = -1}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} Aisha took the $y$ difference
starting at the second point but the $x$ difference starting at the first, so
the sign flipped. A correct response names the mismatched order; noting that
the ramp falls from left to right, so the slope must be negative, is a valid
supporting argument.

\problem{Ticket table: 2, 4, 6, 8 tickets against 15, 27, 39, 51 dollars. Leo
says the slope is $\dfrac{6}{36}$ dollars per ticket. (a) Extra cost of 8
tickets over 2. (b) Slope. (c) Name the error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} From the table, $51 - 15$ dollars. \ans{36}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Dollars per ticket puts the dollar change on top:
$m = \dfrac{36}{8 - 2}$. \ans{m = 6}

\textbf{Solution (c) --- instructor-judged.} Leo divided the ticket change by
the cost change, inverting the ratio; his answer is tickets per dollar. A
correct response names the inversion and says the dollar change belongs on
top.

\problem{Dana and Kofi computed the slope through $(-2, 5)$ and $(4, -7)$.
Dana got $-2$; Kofi got 2. (a) Compute it. (b) Explain who is right and what
the other did.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} $y$ changes by $-7 - 5 = -12$ while $x$ changes by
$4 - (-2) = 6$, so $m = -12/6$. \ans{m = -2}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} Dana is right. Kofi reversed the
order on the top only, computing $5 - (-7) = 12$ over the same positive $x$
change, which flips the sign. A correct response identifies Dana and names
Kofi's reversed subtraction; the observation that $y$ falls while $x$ rises,
so the slope must be negative, is valid supporting evidence.
