\problem{$2x + y = 7$. Isolate $y$ by subtracting $2x$ from both sides; nothing has to be
divided because $B = 1$.
\[ 2x + y = 7 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = 7 - 2x \;\Longrightarrow\; y = -2x + 7 \]
Written as $y = mx + b$: the slope is $m = -2$ and the $y$-intercept is $b = 7$, i.e. the
point $(0, 7)$. \ans{y = -2x + 7}}

\problem{$y = 3x - 5$. Standard form keeps $x$ and $y$ on the left, so subtract $3x$ from
both sides, then multiply by $-1$ so that $A > 0$.
\[ y = 3x - 5 \;\Longrightarrow\; -3x + y = -5 \;\Longrightarrow\; 3x - y = 5 \]
Check with $x = 4$: slope-intercept gives $y = 3(4) - 5 = 7$, and $3(4) - 7 = 5$. \ans{3x - y = 5}}

\problem{$3x + 4y = 12$. Subtract $3x$, then divide \emph{every} term by $4$.
\[ 4y = 12 - 3x \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \frac{12 - 3x}{4} = \frac{12}{4} - \frac{3x}{4} \]
so $y = -\tfrac{3}{4}x + 3$: slope $-\tfrac{3}{4}$, $y$-intercept $3$.
\ans{y = -\tfrac{3}{4}x + 3}

\noindent{\small The slope stays a fraction --- $-0.75$ is the same number, but the fraction
shows the "down 3, right 4" movement directly.}}

\problem{\textbf{(a)} The table pairs $x = 4$ with the level $18$, read straight off the
bottom row (centimetres). \ans{18}

\textbf{(b)} Rate of change between the first and last columns:
$m = \dfrac{12 - 30}{6 - 0} = \dfrac{-18}{6} = -3$ cm per hour, and the level at $x = 0$ is
$30$ cm, so $b = 30$. \ans{y = -3x + 30}

\textbf{(c)} Move the $x$ term across: $3x + y = 30$. Check with $x = 2$:
$3(2) + 24 = 30$. \ans{3x + y = 30}}

\problem{$y = -\tfrac{2}{5}x + 3$. Multiply every term by $5$ to clear the fraction, then
collect $x$ and $y$ on the left.
\[ 5y = -2x + 15 \;\Longrightarrow\; 2x + 5y = 15 \]
Check with $x = 10$: $y = -\tfrac{2}{5}(10) + 3 = -1$, and $2(10) + 5(-1) = 15$.
\ans{2x + 5y = 15}}

\problem{$-6x + 2y = 10$. Add $6x$ to both sides, then divide every term by $2$.
\[ 2y = 6x + 10 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = 3x + 5 \]
The slope is $+3$: subtracting a negative $x$ term flips its sign, which is where a lost
minus sign usually shows up. \ans{y = 3x + 5}}

\problem{\textbf{(a)} Slope between $(2, 9)$ and $(6, 17)$:
$m = \dfrac{17 - 9}{6 - 2} = \dfrac{8}{4}$. \ans{2}

\textbf{(b)} Substitute one point into $y = 2x + b$ to find $b$: using $(2, 9)$,
$9 = 2(2) + b$, so $b = 5$. \ans{y = 2x + 5}

\textbf{(c)} Subtract $2x$: $-2x + y = 5$, then multiply by $-1$ for $A > 0$:
$2x - y = -5$. Check with $x = 1$: $y = 7$ and $2(1) - 7 = -5$. \ans{2x - y = -5}}

\problem{$y = \tfrac{1}{2}x - 4$. Multiply every term by $2$: $2y = x - 8$. Subtract $2y$
and add $8$ to put $x$ and $y$ on the left with $A > 0$:
\[ 2y = x - 8 \;\Longrightarrow\; x - 2y = 8 \]
Check with $x = 6$: $y = -1$, and $6 - 2(-1) = 8$. \ans{x - 2y = 8}}

\problem{$4x + 6y = 120$. Subtract $4x$, then divide every term by $6$ and reduce:
\[ 6y = 120 - 4x \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \frac{120}{6} - \frac{4x}{6} = -\tfrac{2}{3}x + 20 \]
The $y$-intercept is $20$: if no child tickets are sold, $20$ adult tickets bring in the
whole \$120. \ans{y = -\tfrac{2}{3}x + 20}}

\problem{\textbf{(a)} $15x + 25y = 300$. Subtract $15x$ and divide every term by $25$:
\[ 25y = 300 - 15x \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \frac{300}{25} - \frac{15x}{25} = -\tfrac{3}{5}x + 12 \]
\ans{y = -\tfrac{3}{5}x + 12}

\textbf{(b)} The slope $-\tfrac{3}{5}$ means $3$ fewer driveways for every $5$ extra lawns.
For $5$ more lawns: $\tfrac{3}{5} \times 5$. \ans{3}

\noindent{\small Sense check: $5$ lawns pay $5 \times \$15 = \$75$, and $3$ driveways pay
$3 \times \$25 = \$75$ --- the same money either way, which is exactly what the slope says.}}
