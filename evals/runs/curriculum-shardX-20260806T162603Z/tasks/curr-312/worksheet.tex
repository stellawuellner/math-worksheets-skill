\begin{center}\small\textit{filling in reasons, filling in statements, and finishing with CPCTC}\end{center}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Every line needs a reason: a \emph{given}, a definition, the reflexive property, a theorem (vertical angles, alternate interior angles), or a congruence criterion.
  \item A shared side or angle enters by the \textbf{reflexive property}; it is never marked on the figure.
  \item \textbf{CPCTC} --- corresponding parts of congruent triangles are congruent --- is what you use \emph{after} the criterion, never before it.
  \item Diagrams show the marks and the labelling; all measurements are in the problem text.
\end{itemize}
