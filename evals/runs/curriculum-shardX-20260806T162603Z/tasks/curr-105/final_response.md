# Place-Value Synthesis Review — Grades 4–5

Here are the three PDFs for this interleaved review:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_pvmix_curr105.pdf`) — 12 problems combining expanded form,
  comparison, and rounding decisions. A four-problem warm-up touches each
  skill once; after that the three subskills are interleaved so your child must
  choose the tool for each problem (including a below-the-midpoint rounding of
  52,499, a carrying case at 63,970, an expanded-form-then-compare two-parter,
  and a "Ben" comparison error to diagnose). Problem 12 is the synthesis
  challenge: order three attendances, round the greatest, and reason about
  which two round to the same thousand.
- **Answer key** (`ak_pvmix_curr105.pdf`) — quick-answers bank up front, then
  step-by-step reasoning for every problem, with full-credit rubrics for the
  written explanations. Generated Curriculum section: 4.NBT.A.2, 4.NBT.A.3,
  with the difficulty range (1 up to a level-5 finisher).
- **Study guide** (`ss_pvmix_curr105.pdf`) — three sections matching the three
  skills (expanded form, comparing numbers, rounding decisions), each with
  rule, worked example, and try-it, plus a watch-out box on picking the right
  tool in a mixed review.

**What is verified:** 14 of the 17 responses are machine-checked — every
standard-form value, missing addend, comparison symbol, ordering, and rounded
value, including parts (a) and (b) of the synthesis problem. Three responses
are instructor-judged and flagged for manual review: which rounded duck count
is closer (problem 10), the explanation of Ben's mistake (problem 11), and
part (c) of problem 12 — each with an explicit rubric in the key. The key's
"What is verified" note reflects the same split.
