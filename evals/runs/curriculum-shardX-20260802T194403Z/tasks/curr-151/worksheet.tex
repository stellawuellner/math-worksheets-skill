\wstitleblock{Ratios and Equivalent Ratio Tables}{Grade 6--7 Mathematics}{}

\begin{center}
Writing ratios from models, part-to-part and part-to-whole forms, equivalent
ratio tables, and the value of a ratio
\end{center}

\noindent\small\textbf{Directions.} Each problem gives you a model --- a tile
strip, a tape diagram, or a ratio table. Connect the model to notation: a ratio
of $a$ to $b$ may be written \textbf{$a$ to $b$}, \textbf{$a:b$}, or
\textbf{$\frac{a}{b}$}. Show the model work you used, then write the answer on
the line.\normalsize

\problem[5cm]{\textbf{Tile strip.} The strip below models one handful of tiles.
Each R is a red tile and each B is a blue tile.
\begin{center}\large
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.8cm}R & R & R & R & B & B & B & B & B & B \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Write the ratio of \textbf{red tiles to blue tiles} in all three forms, then give
it in simplest form as a fraction.}

\problem[5cm]{\textbf{Same tile strip.} Now compare a part with the whole handful.
Write the ratio of \textbf{red tiles to all the tiles} as a fraction in simplest
form, and say in words what that fraction tells you about the handful.}

\problem[6cm]{\textbf{Ratio table.} A pancake recipe mixes flour and water in the
ratio 3 to 2 (3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of water).
\begin{center}\large
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|}\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.75cm}Water (cups) & 2 & 4 & 6 & 8 \\\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.75cm}Flour (cups) & 3 & & & \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Complete the table. How many cups of flour go with 8 cups of water?}

\problem[5cm]{\textbf{Value of a ratio.} A smoothie stand uses 12 strawberries
for every 3 smoothies. Write the ratio of strawberries to smoothies, then find
its \textbf{value}: the number of strawberries per single smoothie.}

\problem[6cm]{\textbf{Tape diagram.} At a shelter the ratio of cats to dogs is
3 to 5. The tape diagram shows one box for each part, and every box stands for
the same number of animals.
\begin{center}\large
\begin{tabular}{@{}l@{\hspace{4pt}}c@{}}
Cats: & \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}\hline \rule{0pt}{0.6cm}~~~ & ~~~ & ~~~ \\\hline\end{tabular}\\[6pt]
Dogs: & \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline \rule{0pt}{0.6cm}~~~ & ~~~ & ~~~ & ~~~ & ~~~ \\\hline\end{tabular}
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Each box stands for 4 animals. How many animals are at the shelter in all?}

\problem[6cm]{\textbf{Ratio table.} Pencils are sold at a steady price: 5 pencils
cost 2 dollars.
\begin{center}\large
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|}\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.75cm}Pencils & 5 & 10 & 20 \\\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.75cm}Cost (dollars) & 2 & & \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Complete the table. What does a bundle of 20 pencils cost, in dollars?}

\problem[5cm]{\textbf{Part to whole.} In one class, the ratio of students who walk
to school to students who ride to school is $2:7$, and every student does exactly
one of the two. What \textbf{fraction of the whole class} walks? Explain how the
7 in the ratio can turn into a 9 in your answer.}

\problem[5cm]{\textbf{Comparing by value.} Two drink mixes are made with scoops of
powder and cups of water.\\[3pt]
Mix A: 4 scoops in 10 cups. \qquad Mix B: 6 scoops in 14 cups.\\[3pt]
Write each mix as a ratio in fraction form, find the value of each ratio, then
write $<$ or $>$ between them in this order: value of Mix A \underline{\hspace{1.2cm}}
value of Mix B.}

\problem[6cm]{\textbf{Ratio table, harder step.} A lemonade recipe uses 9 lemons
for every 6 cups of lemonade.
\begin{center}\large
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|}\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.75cm}Cups of lemonade & 6 & 2 & 14 \\\hline
\rule{0pt}{0.75cm}Lemons & 9 & & \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Fill in the middle column first --- it is the easy step --- and use it to find how
many lemons are needed for 14 cups.}

\problem[6cm]{\textbf{Synthesis.} A mural paint is mixed from blue and yellow in
the ratio 3 to 2. Nia needs 45 liters of mixed paint in total.\\[3pt]
Draw a tape diagram with one box per part, work out what one box is worth, then
find how many liters of \textbf{blue} paint Nia needs. State the ratio of blue
paint to total paint as a fraction as part of your reasoning.
