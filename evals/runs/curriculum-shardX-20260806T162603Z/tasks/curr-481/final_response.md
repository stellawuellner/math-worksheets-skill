# Reading and Sketching Slope Fields — three PDFs

**Course:** AP Calculus AB/BC · **Topic:** differential equations and numerical
solution, focused on reading and sketching slope fields · **8 problems**

## What you get

- **`ws_slopefields_curr481.pdf` — the student worksheet (4 pages).**
  Every problem carries its **own printed slope field**, drawn to scale from
  its own differential equation, with the field on the left and the questions
  and writing space beside it. A red dot marks the point each sketch must pass
  through, so the student draws directly on the picture rather than copying a
  grid first. The eight fields cover the shapes that matter: slope depending on
  both variables ($x - y$), on $y$ alone ($y/2$, $y$), on $x$ alone ($2x$), a
  circular family ($-x/y$), a separable exponential ($xy$), a logistic field
  with two equilibrium rows ($y(4-y)$), and one where the solution is an
  implicit square root.
- **`ak_slopefields_curr481.pdf` — the full answer key.**
  Every problem restated and worked, and each sketch part carries an explicit
  marking rubric — what the drawn curve must do, and what earns nothing. It
  opens with a generated Quick Answers bank, a "What is verified" note, and a
  Curriculum block.
- **`ss_slopefields_curr481.pdf` — the study guide (2 pages).**
  Three sections: reading a slope off a field (with the row-versus-column tell
  that identifies which variable the slope depends on), sketching a solution
  curve (with a small worked field showing a solution drawn tangent to it), and
  going from the field to a formula. Each has a rule box, a worked example, and
  a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

## Verification, stated honestly

The eight problems ask for **19 responses**, and on a sketching topic the split
is necessarily wide:

- **12 of 19 are machine-checked** with SymPy: every slope value is recomputed
  from the differential equation at the stated point, every equilibrium and
  horizontal-segment condition is re-solved, both antiderivatives are checked
  by differentiating them back, and both particular-solution values are
  recomputed exactly.
- **7 of 19 are instructor-judged** — the sketches and the three "explain or
  describe" parts. No gate can look at a drawn curve, and this sheet does not
  pretend otherwise: those print as `---` in the Quick Answers bank, and each
  has a matching rubric in the key saying what the curve must do (tangent to
  the field throughout, through the marked point, flat where the field is flat)
  and what it must not (cut across a segment, however smooth it looks).

The study guide's six results are all machine-checked.

## Suggested order

Study guide first, then the worksheet, then the answer key.
