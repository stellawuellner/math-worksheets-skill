Unit conversion, prism volume, angle measure, and multistep synthesis

\noindent\small\textbf{Directions:} Read each problem for the units it gives you
and the unit it asks for. Show the conversion, the formula, or the angle
relationship you used, then write the answer with its unit on the answer line.
Useful facts: $1$ ft $=12$ in, $1$ m $=100$ cm, $1$ kg $=1000$ g,
$1$ L $=1000$ mL, $1$ h $=60$ min, and $1$ L of water fills $1000$ cm$^3$.

\problem{A jump rope is $4$ ft long. How long is the rope in
\textbf{inches}?\par\vspace*{3.5cm}\answerline{in}}

\problem{A water bottle holds $3$ L. How many \textbf{millilitres} does it
hold?\par\vspace*{3.5cm}\answerline{mL}}

\problem{A gift box is a rectangular prism $8$ cm long, $5$ cm wide, and
$3$ cm high. Find its \textbf{volume}.\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{cm$^3$}}

\problem{Ray $BD$ lies between ray $BA$ and ray $BC$, so angles $ABD$ and
$DBC$ together make angle $ABC$. If $\angle ABD = 35^\circ$ and
$\angle DBC = 47^\circ$, find $\angle ABC$.\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{degrees}}

\problem{A baker weighs out $2$ kg $250$ g of flour. What is the total mass in
\textbf{grams}?\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{g}}

\problem{A storage bin is a rectangular prism with a volume of $240$ cm$^3$.
Its base is $8$ cm by $6$ cm. How \textbf{tall} is the
bin?\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{cm}}

\problem{Angle $PQR$ is a right angle. Ray $QS$ splits it into $\angle PQS$ and
$\angle SQR$. If $\angle PQS = 34^\circ$, find
$\angle SQR$.\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{degrees}}

\problem{A film runs for $2$ h $25$ min. How long is the film in
\textbf{minutes}?\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{min}}

\problem[5.5cm]{A shipping carton measures $12$ cm by $10$ cm by $4$ cm. Wooden
cubes with an edge of $2$ cm are packed inside with no gaps. How many cubes fill
the carton? (Find the carton's volume and one cube's volume first.)}

\problem{Rosa measured an angle that is clearly \emph{acute} --- both of its
rays open less than a square corner --- but she wrote down $130^\circ$. Her
protractor shows two scales. Explain what Rosa most likely did wrong and
describe one check she can make before writing an angle measure down. Then give
the angle's true measure.\par\vspace*{5.5cm}\answerline{degrees}}

\problem{A fish tank is $40$ cm long, $25$ cm wide, and $30$ cm high. When it is
filled to the top, how many \textbf{litres} of water does it hold?
$(1$ L $=1000$ cm$^3)$\par\vspace*{5.5cm}\answerline{L}}

\problem{A packing crate is $2$ ft long, $18$ in wide, and $1$ ft high. Find its
volume in \textbf{cubic feet}. (Convert every edge to feet
first.)\par\vspace*{6cm}\answerline{ft$^3$}}
