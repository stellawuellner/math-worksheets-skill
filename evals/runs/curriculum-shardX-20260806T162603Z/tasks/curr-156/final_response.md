Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 unit-rates concept set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_unit_rates_curr156.pdf`) — 10 problems on finding unit
  rates with whole-number quantities, built around concrete models: an
  equal-groups picture to sort eggs into batches, rate tables with a
  "one unit" column, unit price, speed, and rate-per-serving problems, then
  three comparison problems (better pen deal, miles per gallon, and a juice
  pack comparison where the unit prices come out equal and the student
  interprets what that means). Each model is tied to the division or
  multiplication the student writes.
- **Answer key** (`ak_unit_rates_curr156.pdf`) — quick-answers table with
  labelled sub-parts, generated curriculum section (6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3,
  difficulty 1–4), worked solutions connecting each model to the notation,
  and the two declared common wrong answers (dividing the rate upside down on
  the speed and mileage problems).
- **Study guide** (`ss_unit_rates_curr156.pdf`) — three sections: divide down
  to one unit, multiply back up with the rate, and compare two rates fairly;
  each with rule, worked example, and try-it, plus a watch-out on rate order.

**What is verified:** 18 of the 19 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every unit rate, table cell, and comparison relation, plus both misconception
traps. One response is instructor-judged: the interpretation sentence on
problem 10(d) about what equal unit prices mean for a shopper; the key states
the full-credit answer. All eight study-guide checks are machine-verified.
