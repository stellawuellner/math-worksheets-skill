\wstitleblock{Writing Equations from Word Situations}{Grades 6--7 \quad Expressions and Equations}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Writing and solving one-step equations from a story, writing and
solving two-step equations, and writing an expression from a story and
evaluating it for a given value}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{How to turn a situation into an equation.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
\item Say in one sentence what the letter stands for, including its unit ---
``let $n$ be the number of months''. An unnamed letter is where most errors
start.
\item Find the amount that happens once (a fee, a starting amount) and the
amount that happens per unit (a rate). A rate is always multiplied by the
variable; a one-time amount is added or subtracted.
\item Write the equation before you compute anything, and make sure both sides
measure the same kind of thing.
\item Solve by undoing the operations in reverse order, then check the solution
in the original sentence, not in your rearranged version.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem{Maya bought $6$ packs of pencils. Each pack holds the same number of
pencils, and she ended up with $72$ pencils in all. Write an equation for the
number of pencils in one pack, then solve it.
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{pencils}}

\problem{A club's fund held some money, in dollars. The club spent $45$ on
supplies, and $130$ was left. Write an equation for the amount the fund held
before the purchase, then solve it.
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{dollars}}

\problem{A taxi charges a flat $3$, in dollars, plus $2$ for each mile driven.
Write an expression for the fare of a ride of $m$ miles, then use it to find
the fare for a ride of $7$ miles.
\par\vspace*{4cm}\answerline{dollars}}

\problem{A gym charges a one-time joining fee of $20$, in dollars, plus $15$
each month. Ren has paid $110$ in all. Write an equation for the number of
months Ren has been a member, then solve it.
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{months}}

\problem{A rectangular garden bed has a perimeter of $46$ cm in a scale
drawing, and its width is $9$ cm. Write an equation for the length of the bed,
then solve it.
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{cm}}

\problem{A muffin recipe uses $\tfrac{3}{4}$ cup of oats for one batch. A baker
used $6$ cups of oats in all, with none left over. Write an equation for the
number of batches, then solve it.
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{batches}}

\problem{A pool holds $300$ gallons of water and is draining at $12$ gallons
per minute. Write an expression for the number of gallons still in the pool
after $t$ minutes, then use it to find how much water is left after $18$
minutes.
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\answerline{gallons}}

\problem{A restaurant takes $6$, in dollars, off a bill with a coupon. Three
friends then split what is left evenly, and each of them pays $14$. Write an
equation for the bill before the coupon, then solve it.
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{dollars}}

\problem{A rink rents skates for $5$, in dollars, plus $3$ for each hour. Ana
paid $23$ in all. Ben modelled this with the equation $5h + 3 = 23$, where $h$
is the number of hours. Explain in one sentence why Ben's equation does not
match the situation, then write the correct equation and solve it.
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{hours}}

\problem{Two thirds of the members of a coding club signed up for a contest.
Then $4$ of those who signed up dropped out, leaving $18$ contestants. Write an
equation for the number of members in the club, then solve it.
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{students}}
