# Take-Away and Comparing Stories (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_takestories_curr028.pdf`) — 8 story problems within 10
  covering take-from stories (balloons popping, cookies eaten, kites
  landing, a two-step grape story) and comparison stories (how many more /
  fewer, including a one-to-one matching problem with two printed counter
  rows to connect). Every story states its givens and unit plainly with no
  distracting detail, and answers go on labeled answer lines. The challenge
  combines both: a take-from step, then a comparison.
- **Answer key** (`ak_takestories_curr028.pdf`) — reasoning for every
  problem (cross-out models, matching logic, the two-step chain), a
  quick-answers bank, and a curriculum section (K.OA.A and 1.OA.C.6,
  difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_takestories_curr028.pdf`) — three sections: model a
  take-from story, write the take-away equation, and compare by matching,
  each with a rule box, worked example, and fresh try-it, plus a watch-out
  showing that "more" and "fewer" questions use the same subtraction.

**What is verified:** 9 of the 11 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every difference, the two-step result, and both numeric answers of the
challenge; the units on the answer lines are bound to the verification data
too. The 2 open responses (the matching lines drawn in problem 5 and the
"who has more" name in problem 8) are flagged for instructor review with
the key stating what a correct response shows. The equation frames' first
two blanks transcribe story numbers and are printed as working space, which
the key discloses. The key's "What is verified" note reflects the same
split.
