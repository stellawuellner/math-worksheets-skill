\wstitleblock{Adding Three-Digit Numbers with Regrouping}{Grades 2--3 \quad Number and Operations in Base Ten}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Adding three-digit numbers with regrouping, estimating a sum by
rounding to the nearest hundred, and adding to answer story questions}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{How to work these problems.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
\item Stack the two numbers so the ones sit under the ones, the tens under the
tens, and the hundreds under the hundreds.
\item Add the ones column first, then the tens, then the hundreds.
\item If a column adds up to ten or more, write the ones digit under that
column and carry the ten into the next column to the left. That carry is the
\emph{regrouping}, and it has to be added in with the next column.
\item Write your little carry digits above the columns and leave them there.
They show your thinking, and they are the first place to look if an answer
comes out wrong.
\end{itemize}
