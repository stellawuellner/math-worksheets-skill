\wstitleblock{Subtracting on a Number Path}{Kindergarten / Grade 1}{}

\noindent\small
Counting back, counting up to find the difference, and finding a missing number.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Each problem has its own number path. Put your finger on the start
        square, then hop.
  \item \textbf{Count back} to take away: hop one square to the \emph{left} for
        each one you take away. The square you land on is the answer.
  \item \textbf{Count up} when the two numbers are close: start at the smaller
        number and hop \emph{right} until you reach the bigger one. Count your
        hops.
  \item Draw your hops on the path, then write the answer on the line.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize
\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem[3.2cm]{\Large $6 - 2 =$ \ansblank \normalsize
\par\smallskip
Start on $6$ and hop back $2$ squares.
\numpath}

\problem[3.2cm]{\Large $8 - 3 =$ \ansblank \normalsize
\par\smallskip
Start on $8$ and hop back $3$ squares.
\numpath}

\problem[3.2cm]{\Large $10 - 4 =$ \ansblank \normalsize
\par\smallskip
Start on $10$ and hop back $4$ squares.
\numpath}

\problem[3.2cm]{\Large $9 - 7 =$ \ansblank \normalsize
\par\smallskip
These numbers are close together. Start on $7$ and hop up to $9$. How many hops?
\numpath}

\problem[3.2cm]{\Large $7 - 5 =$ \ansblank \normalsize
\par\smallskip
Hop back on the path. Circle the square you land on.
\numpath}

\problem[3.5cm]{\Large $9 - \underline{\hspace{1.2cm}} = 4$ \normalsize
\par\smallskip
Start on $9$. Hop back until you land on $4$. How many hops did you take? Write
that number in the box above.
\numpath}

\problem[3.2cm]{\Large $10 - 6 =$ \ansblank \normalsize
\par\smallskip
Start on $6$ and hop up to $10$. Count your hops.
\numpath}

\problem[3.5cm]{Eight frogs sat on a log. Five frogs hopped away.
How many frogs are still on the log?
\par\smallskip
Show it on the path: start on $8$ and hop back $5$.
\numpath}

\problem[3.5cm]{\Large $\underline{\hspace{1.2cm}} - 3 = 5$ \normalsize
\par\smallskip
You do not know where to start! Begin on $5$ and hop \emph{up} $3$ squares. The
square you land on is the missing start number.
\numpath}

\problem[3.5cm]{Mia had ten stickers. She gave eight stickers away.
How many stickers does Mia have now?
\par\smallskip
Counting back $8$ times is a lot of hops. Start on $8$ and hop up to $10$
instead. Write how many hops you took.
\numpath}
