\problem{Use the distributive property to expand: \quad $4(x + 3)$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiply the 4 by \emph{each} term inside:
$4 \cdot x + 4 \cdot 3$. \ans{4x + 12}

\problem{A rectangular garden bed is 3 m deep, with width split into $x$ m of tomatoes
and 5 m of peppers. (a) area as a product; (b) area as a sum; (c) explain why they
are equal.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) The whole width is $(x + 5)$ m and the depth is 3 m, so the
area is depth times width: \ans{3(x + 5)}

(b) Distribute the 3 over both width pieces: $3 \cdot x + 3 \cdot 5$. \ans{3x + 15}

(c) Model answer: the tomato section has area $3x$ and the pepper section has area
$15$ square meters, and together those two pieces tile exactly the same region the
product $3(x + 5)$ measures, so the two expressions must be equal. (Any one-sentence
answer that identifies both section areas with the whole area earns credit ---
instructor judges the sentence.)

\problem{Combine like terms: \quad $7x + 4 + 2x - 9$}

\textbf{Solution:} Group the like terms: $(7x + 2x) + (4 - 9)$. The $x$-terms give
$9x$; the constants give $-5$. \ans{9x - 5}

\problem{Factor out the greatest common factor: \quad $6x + 15$}

\textbf{Solution:} The GCF of 6 and 15 is 3. Write each term as 3 times something:
$6x = 3 \cdot 2x$ and $15 = 3 \cdot 5$. \ans{3(2x + 5)}

\problem{Expand: \quad $-2(4x - 7)$}

\textbf{Solution:} Distribute $-2$ to \emph{both} terms, watching the signs:
$-2 \cdot 4x = -8x$ and $-2 \cdot (-7) = +14$. \ans{-8x + 14}

\problem{The sides of a triangle measure $x$ cm, $(x + 4)$ cm, and $(2x - 1)$ cm.
Write and simplify an expression for the perimeter.}

\textbf{Solution:} Perimeter is the sum of the three sides:
$x + (x + 4) + (2x - 1)$. Combine the $x$-terms ($x + x + 2x = 4x$) and the constants
($4 - 1 = 3$). \ans{4x + 3\ \text{cm}}

\problem{4 friends each buy a sandwich costing $s$ dollars and a juice costing 2
dollars. (a) total cost as a product; (b) as a sum.}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Each friend spends $(s + 2)$ dollars and there are 4 friends:
\ans{4(s + 2)}

(b) Distribute the 4: $4 \cdot s + 4 \cdot 2$. \ans{4s + 8}

\problem{Factor out the greatest common factor: \quad $9x + 24$}

\textbf{Solution:} The GCF of 9 and 24 is 3: $9x = 3 \cdot 3x$ and $24 = 3 \cdot 8$.
\ans{3(3x + 8)}

\problem{Simplify completely: \quad $3(2x + 5) - 4x + 1$}

\textbf{Solution:} Distribute first: $3(2x + 5) = 6x + 15$. Then combine like terms:
$6x + 15 - 4x + 1 = (6x - 4x) + (15 + 1)$. \ans{2x + 16}

\problem{\textbf{Challenge.} Simplify completely: \quad $5(2x + 3) - 2(x - 4)$}

\textbf{Solution:} Distribute both products, keeping the signs:
$5(2x + 3) = 10x + 15$ and $-2(x - 4) = -2x + 8$. Combine:
$(10x - 2x) + (15 + 8)$. \ans{8x + 23}
