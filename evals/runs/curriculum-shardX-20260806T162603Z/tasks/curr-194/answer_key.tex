\problem{Clock face, radius $6$ cm: circumference.

\textbf{Solution:} Circumference uses the radius doubled:
$C = 2\pi r = 2\pi \cdot 6 = 37.699\ldots$, so \ans{37.70\ \text{cm}}}

\problem{Pizza, diameter $10$ in: area.

\textbf{Solution:} The area formula needs the \emph{radius}: $r = 10 \div 2
= 5$. Then $A = \pi r^2 = \pi \cdot 5^2 = 78.539\ldots$, so
\ans{78.54\ \text{in}^2}}

\problem{Garden $9$ m by $4$ m: fencing.

\textbf{Solution:} Fence runs \emph{around} the garden, so we need perimeter,
not area: $P = 2(9 + 4) = 2 \cdot 13$, giving \ans{26\ \text{m}}}

\problem{Rug $7$ ft by $5$ ft: floor covered.

\textbf{Solution:} "How much floor does it cover" asks for \emph{area}:
$A = 7 \cdot 5$, giving \ans{35\ \text{ft}^2} (as coordinates: the rug's
corners $(0,0)$, $(7,0)$, $(7,5)$, $(0,5)$ enclose $35$ square feet).}

\problem{Pond, diameter $14$ m: distance around.

\textbf{Solution:} With the \emph{diameter} given, use $C = \pi d$ directly:
$C = \pi \cdot 14 = 43.982\ldots$, so \ans{43.98\ \text{m}} A common error is
treating $14$ as the radius and doubling again to get $87.96$.}

\problem{Mara's circle, diameter $16$ cm.

\textbf{Solution (a):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} Mara substituted the
\emph{diameter} $16$ into $A = \pi r^2$. The radius is half the diameter:
$r = 16 \div 2 = 8$ cm. Her $804.25$ is four times too large.

\textbf{Solution (b):} $A = \pi \cdot 8^2 = \pi \cdot 64 = 201.061\ldots$, so
\ans{201.06\ \text{cm}^2}}

\problem{Jon's paint for a $12$ ft by $3$ ft stripe.

\textbf{Solution (a):} \emph{(instructor-judged)} $2(12+3) = 30$ is the
\emph{perimeter} --- the distance around the stripe. Paint covers surface, so
Jon needed the \emph{area}. His $30$ square feet is a perimeter wearing an
area's unit.

\textbf{Solution (b):} $A = 12 \cdot 3$, giving \ans{36\ \text{ft}^2}}

\problem{Challenge: table top, radius $0.6$ m --- trim (around) and glass
(covering).

\textbf{Solution (a):} Trim is circumference: $C = 2\pi r = 2\pi \cdot 0.6 =
3.769\ldots$, so \ans{3.77\ \text{m}}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Glass is area: $A = \pi r^2 = \pi \cdot 0.6^2 =
\pi \cdot 0.36 = 1.130\ldots$, so \ans{1.13\ \text{m}^2} Note the two answers
use different units --- meters for a length, square meters for a surface.}
