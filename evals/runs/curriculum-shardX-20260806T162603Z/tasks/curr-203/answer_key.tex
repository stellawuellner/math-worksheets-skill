\problem{$\sqrt{30}$ lies between which two whole numbers? Name the perfect squares used.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The perfect squares on either side of the radicand are $25$ and
$36$, so $25 < 30 < 36$. Taking square roots across the inequality keeps the order, giving
$5 < \sqrt{30} < 6$: between \ans{5} and \ans{6}.

\problem{Give $\sqrt{8}$ to the nearest tenth.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad $4 < 8 < 9$, so the value is between $2$ and $3$. Squeeze with
tenths: $2.8^2 = 7.84$ and $2.9^2 = 8.41$, so the root lies between them and is nearer the
lower one. \ans{2.8}

\problem{A square rug covers $45$ square feet. Side to the nearest tenth of a foot?}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad For a square, area is side times side, so the side is the square
root of the area. $36 < 45 < 49$ puts it between $6$ and $7$ feet; then $6.7^2 = 44.89$ and
$6.8^2 = 46.24$ trap the area, and $44.89$ is the closer of the two. \ans{6.7\ \text{ft}}

\problem{$\sqrt{115}$ lies between which two whole numbers? Name the perfect squares used.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The perfect squares either side are $100$ and $121$, and
$100 < 115 < 121$, so the root lies between \ans{10} and \ans{11}.

\problem{Give $\sqrt{72}$ to the nearest hundredth.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad $64 < 72 < 81$ gives the bracket $8$ to $9$. Tenths:
$8.4^2 = 70.56$ and $8.5^2 = 72.25$. Hundredths: $8.48^2 = 71.9104$ and
$8.49^2 = 72.0801$, which trap the radicand. \ans{8.49}

\problem{A square vegetable bed covers $150$ square feet. Side to the nearest tenth of a
foot?}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The side is the square root of the area. $144 < 150 < 169$ puts it
between $12$ and $13$ feet; $12.2^2 = 148.84$ and $12.3^2 = 151.29$ trap the area, and the
lower one is closer. \ans{12.2\ \text{ft}}

\problem{Locate $\sqrt{40}$: (a) to the nearest tenth, (b) marked on the number line.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} \quad $36 < 40 < 49$ brackets the root between $6$ and $7$.
Tenths: $6.3^2 = 39.69$ and $6.4^2 = 40.96$, so the root is just above the lower one.
\ans{6.3}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} \quad The dot belongs between the ticks at
three tenths and four tenths past the sixth whole number, close to the left one, and
labelled with the radical or the rounded decimal. A dot outside that tenth-wide interval is
not correct.

\problem{Give $\sqrt{95}$ to the nearest hundredth.}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad $81 < 95 < 100$ brackets it between $9$ and $10$. Tenths:
$9.7^2 = 94.09$ and $9.8^2 = 96.04$. Hundredths: $9.74^2 = 94.8676$ and
$9.75^2 = 95.0625$, which trap the radicand, and the upper one is nearer. \ans{9.75}

\problem{A square patch of ground has area $80$ square feet, and tarps come only in whole
numbers of feet on a side. Smallest tarp that covers it?}

\textbf{Solution:} \quad The patch's side is $\sqrt{80}$, and $64 < 80 < 100$ places that
between $8$ and $10$; more tightly, $8^2 = 64$ is too small and $9^2 = 81$ is the first
whole square at or above the area. An eight-foot tarp would leave part of the patch bare,
so round \emph{up}, not to the nearer whole number. \ans{9\ \text{ft}}

\problem{\textbf{Challenge.} Ravi estimates $\sqrt{50} \approx 25$. (a) Give $\sqrt{50}$ to
the nearest hundredth. (b) Explain his error.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} \quad $49 < 50 < 64$ brackets the root just above $7$. Hundredths:
$7.07^2 = 49.9849$ and $7.08^2 = 50.1264$ trap the radicand. \ans{7.07}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} \quad Ravi halved the radicand instead of
looking for the number whose square is the radicand. The squaring check settles it: his
value squared is $625$, which is more than twelve times the number under the radical, while
the correct estimate squared lands within a hundredth of it. A full answer names the
halving error and shows that squaring check; the bracketing whole numbers are a fair
sanity check to cite as well.
