\problem{$3x + 2y = 36$ (notebooks at \$3, pens at \$2, \$36 total). Rewrite
solved for $y$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract the $x$-term from both sides: $2y = 36 - 3x$. Now
divide \emph{every} term by 2: $y = 18 - \frac{3}{2}x$. Writing the $x$-term
first gives the usual $y = mx + b$ order. \ans{y = -\tfrac{3}{2}x + 18}

\problem{$4x + 5y = 40$ (child tickets \$4, adult tickets \$5, \$40 total).
Rewrite solved for $y$.}

\textbf{Solution:} $5y = 40 - 4x$, then divide every term by 5:
$y = 8 - \frac{4}{5}x$. \ans{y = -\tfrac{4}{5}x + 8}

\problem{$y = -\frac{2}{3}x + 8$. Rewrite as $2x + 3y = C$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Substitute the expression for $y$ into $2x + 3y$:
\[
  2x + 3\left(-\tfrac{2}{3}x + 8\right) = 2x - 2x + 24 = 24 .
\]
The $x$-terms cancel, which is exactly why the standard form has a constant on
the right. \ans{2x + 3y = 24}

\problem{$5x + 4y = 60$ (posters \$5, flyers \$4, budget \$60).
(a) $x$-intercept. (b) $y$-intercept.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} At the $x$-intercept, $y = 0$, so $5x = 60$.
\ans{x = 12}

\textbf{Solution (b):} At the $y$-intercept, $x = 0$, so $4y = 60$.
\ans{y = 15}

\problem{$2x - y = 7$. Rewrite solved for $y$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $2x$: $-y = 7 - 2x$. Multiply both sides by $-1$
--- every term changes sign: $y = 2x - 7$. \ans{y = 2x - 7}

\problem{$y = \frac{3}{4}x - 5$. Rewrite as $3x - 4y = C$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Substitute into $3x - 4y$:
\[
  3x - 4\left(\tfrac{3}{4}x - 5\right) = 3x - 3x + 20 = 20 .
\]
\ans{3x - 4y = 20}

\problem{$6x + 3y = 18$ (seeds \$6, soil \$3, fund \$18). (a) $x$-intercept.
(b) $y$-intercept. (c) Slope from the two intercept points.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Set $y = 0$: $6x = 18$. \ans{x = 3}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Set $x = 0$: $3y = 18$. \ans{y = 6}

\textbf{Solution (c):} The two intercept points are $(3, 0)$ and $(0, 6)$.
Going from the first to the second, $y$ rises by 6 while $x$ falls by 3, so
the slope is $6/(-3)$. \ans{m = -2}

\problem{$3x + 4y = 48$ with the table $x = 0, 4, 8, 12$ against
$y = 12, 9, 6, 3$. (a) Change in $y$ from $x = 0$ to $x = 12$.
(b) Slope-intercept form.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} The last table entry minus the first: $3 - 12$.
\ans{-9}

\textbf{Solution (b):} $4y = 48 - 3x$, then divide every term by 4:
$y = 12 - \frac{3}{4}x$. Notice the table agrees: 12 small drinks cost
9 large ones, which is a drop of $\frac{3}{4}$ per small drink.
\ans{y = -\tfrac{3}{4}x + 12}

\problem{$y = -\frac{5}{2}x + 7$. Rewrite as $5x + 2y = C$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Substitute into $5x + 2y$:
\[
  5x + 2\left(-\tfrac{5}{2}x + 7\right) = 5x - 5x + 14 = 14 .
\]
\ans{5x + 2y = 14}

\problem{$8x + 6y = 120$ (tomatoes \$8, peppers \$6, budget \$120).
(a) Slope-intercept form. (b) What the slope means for the gardener.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} $6y = 120 - 8x$, then divide every term by 6:
$y = 20 - \frac{8}{6}x$, and $\frac{8}{6}$ reduces to $\frac{4}{3}$.
\ans{y = -\tfrac{4}{3}x + 20}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged.} A correct explanation reads the
slope as a trade-off in the garden: each extra tomato plant costs four thirds
of a pepper plant, or equivalently every 3 extra tomato plants cost 4 pepper
plants, and the negative sign says one number falls as the other rises. The
units may be stated either way round. ``The slope is negative'' with no
reference to plants does not earn credit.
