# Compare Sets by Counting On (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **ws_comparecount_curr005.pdf** — a 10-problem interleaved review on
  counting on to compare two represented sets. A three-problem warm-up drills
  "which has more/fewer/the same"; after that the three subskills — which has
  more, how many more, and making sets equal — rotate so the child must choose
  a method each time. Problem 4 scaffolds counting on with hop lines (marked
  as working space, not answers), and the sheet ends with a synthesis
  challenge combining counting, comparing, and explaining the count-on.
- **ak_comparecount_curr005.pdf** — the step-by-step answer key: every
  solution models the count-on reasoning ("from 4: 5, 6, 7 — three hops"),
  with a quick-answers bank and generated Curriculum section (K.CC.C.6).
- **ss_comparecount_curr005.pdf** — a one-page study guide teaching the three
  subskills with a rule, worked example, and try-it each, plus a watch-out
  about spread-out rows looking bigger.

**What is verified:** 21 of the 22 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every count has its own entry checked against the dot data that draws the row,
every more/fewer/same answer is verified as the underlying comparison
(4 < 6, 6 = 6, ...), and every how-many-more answer is verified from the two
counts. The five hop lines in problem 4 are declared as working space, and the
answer key reports that claim to you. **One item is instructor-judged:** the
challenge asks the child to say how they counted on, with an accept rubric in
the key. The study guide's examples and try-its are machine-verified the same
way.
