\begin{center}
\textit{Counting on, doubles and near doubles, and partners that make ten}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions:} Before you add, pick the strategy that fits
the numbers best!
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Count on} when one number is small: say the big number, hop
        the little one.
  \item \textbf{Doubles} when the numbers match --- and \textbf{near
        doubles} when they almost match.
  \item \textbf{Tens partners} when the two numbers fit together to make 10.
\end{itemize}
