Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 5 measurement review:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_mvca_curr145.pdf`) — 12 problems in an interleaved
  synthesis format: a short warm-up (a length conversion, a volume, a
  straight-line angle, a mass conversion), then volume, conversion, and angle
  problems mixed so the student must pick the method each time (a two-box step
  stool, a km-race with meters remaining, the minute hand's sweep, a
  missing-height box, a jug-and-cups capacity problem), ending with a
  fish-tank challenge that chains volume → liters → an explain-why part.
  Every unit answer has a labeled answer line (cm, cm³, degrees, g, m, min, L).
- **Answer key** (`ak_mvca_curr145.pdf`) — worked reasoning for every problem
  (layers of cubes, convert-first-then-subtract, degrees per minute), a
  quick-answers bank with the challenge's sub-parts labeled, and common-wrong-
  answer notes for the three declared traps (×100 for kg→g, dividing by one
  base dimension, and the trap-checked conversions).
- **Study guide** (`ss_mvca_curr145.pdf`) — three skills matching the sheet
  (volume of a box, converting units, finding a missing angle), each with a
  rule box, worked example, and try-it, plus a same-units-first watch-out.

**What is verified:** 13 of the 14 worksheet checks and all 6 study-guide
examples/try-its are machine-verified with SymPy — every numeric answer,
each with its unit bound to the printed answer line and the key's boxed
answer. The one open item is 12(c), the written explanation of why dividing
by 1000 converts cm³ to liters; it is flagged for instructor review with a
rubric in the key stating what a correct explanation must contain.
