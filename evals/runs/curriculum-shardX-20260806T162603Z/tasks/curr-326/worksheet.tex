\wstitleblock{Distance and Midpoint on the Coordinate Plane}{High School Geometry}{}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item The \textbf{distance} between two points is the hypotenuse of the right
        triangle whose legs are the horizontal and vertical gaps:
        $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$.
  \item The \textbf{midpoint} is the pair of averages:
        $M = \left( \dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2} \right)$.
  \item Give every midpoint as an ordered pair. Give distances exactly where
        they come out whole, and otherwise to two decimal places.
  \item Two problems include a blank grid. Plotting the points is optional --- it
        is there so you can check that your answer looks right.
\end{itemize}
