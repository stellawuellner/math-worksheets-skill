# One-Step Equations with Rational Coefficients (Grades 6-7)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_onestep_curr176.pdf`) — 10 guided-practice problems on
  one-step equations whose coefficients are fractions and decimals. The early
  problems come with concrete models tied directly to the notation: a balance
  scale for x + 7 = 12 (remove 7 from both pans), a tape diagram of five 4s for
  x/5 = 4, and a three-part bar for (2/3)x = 8. The four undo moves — subtract/
  add, divide both sides, multiply by the reciprocal, undo a division — are
  interleaved after the warm-up, and the sheet ends with a challenge that asks
  your child to write the equation for "two-fifths of a number is 18" and then
  solve it.
- **Answer key** (`ak_onestep_curr176.pdf`) — every solution shows the undo
  step, the computation, and a substitution check back into the original
  equation, plus a quick-answers column and the generated curriculum section
  (7.EE.B.4). 
- **Study guide** (`ss_onestep_curr176.pdf`) — a two-page card with one section
  per undo move, each with the rule (stated in balance/tape language), a worked
  example that names its strategy, and a try-it with the answer upside down.

**What is verified:** 10 of the 11 responses the worksheet asks for are
machine-checked with a computer algebra system (every solution, including the
fraction and negative-decimal answers, and all 8 study-guide examples/try-its).
One response is instructor-judged: 10(a), where your child writes the equation
from the sentence — the answer key states which equivalent forms earn credit.
The key's "What is verified" note reflects the same split.
