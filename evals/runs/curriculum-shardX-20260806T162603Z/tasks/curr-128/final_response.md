Here are the three PDFs for the mixed-numbers-in-context worksheet (Grades 4–5):

- **Worksheet** (`ws_mixednum_curr128.pdf`) — 10 word problems on adding and subtracting mixed numbers, all set in realistic situations (trails, boards, recipes, pumpkins, fabric, a punch bowl) with the givens and units stated explicitly and no distracting story detail. The set ramps from same-denominator addition through subtraction with regrouping, one unlike-denominator addition, a missing-part problem, and a two-step challenge. Every answer line carries its unit.
- **Answer key** (`ak_mixednum_curr128.pdf`) — quick-answers bank plus full step-by-step solutions showing the wholes-and-parts strategy and each regrouping trade (e.g., 5 1/4 = 4 5/4), with every boxed answer including its unit.
- **Study guide** (`ss_mixednum_curr128.pdf`) — a reference card teaching the three skills tested: adding mixed numbers, subtracting mixed numbers, and regrouping to subtract, each with a rule box, worked example, and a try-it with the answer upside down, plus a watch-out about writing units.

**What is verified:** all 10 printed answers were machine-checked with SymPy (units included, both in the verification data and in the printed answer boxes), and all 6 study-guide worked examples and try-its were verified the same way. Nothing on this sheet needs instructor judgment. The full gate chain passed green.
