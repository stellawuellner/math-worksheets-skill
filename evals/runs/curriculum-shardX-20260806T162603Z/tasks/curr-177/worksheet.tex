\begin{center}
\textit{solving two-step equations, including negative coefficients, and checking solutions by substitution}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{The pattern (worked for you):} to solve $3x + 4 = 10$, undo the
$+\,4$ first (subtract 4 from both sides: $3x = 6$), then undo the $\times\,3$
(divide both sides by 3: $x = 2$). \emph{Check} by substituting back:
$3(2) + 4 = 10$. \checkmark

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=1pt]
  \item Undo addition or subtraction first, then multiplication or division.
  \item Check every solution by substituting it into the original equation.
\end{itemize}
