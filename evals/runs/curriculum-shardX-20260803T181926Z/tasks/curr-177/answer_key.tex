\problem{Solve for $x$: \quad $3x + 7 = 22$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $7$ from both sides: $3x = 15$. Divide both sides by $3$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = 5}

\problem{Solve for $x$: \quad $2x + 15 = 9$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $15$ from both sides: $2x = 9 - 15 = -6$. Divide both sides by $2$. A negative result is fine --- the balance rules do not change.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = -3}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve for $x$: \quad $\dfrac{x}{4} + 6 = 10$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $6$ from both sides: $\dfrac{x}{4} = 4$. The variable is divided by $4$, so undo it by multiplying both sides by $4$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = 16}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve for $x$, then check your solution by substituting it back into the original equation. \quad $9x + 14 = 77$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $14$ from both sides: $9x = 63$. Divide both sides by $9$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = 7}

\textbf{Check:} substitute $x = 7$ into the left side: $9(7) + 14 = 63 + 14$, which equals the right side.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{77}

\problem{Maya solved the equation $6x - 5 = 31$ and wrote $x = 4$. Substitute $x = 4$ into the left side of her equation to find the value it really produces, then solve the equation correctly.}

\textbf{Solution --- the check:} substitute $x = 4$ into $6x - 5$: \ $6(4) - 5 = 24 - 5$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{19}

Since $19 \neq 31$, Maya's value is not a solution.

\textbf{Solving correctly:} add $5$ to both sides: $6x = 36$. Divide both sides by $6$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = 6}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve for $x$, then check your solution. \quad $-3x + 11 = 2$}

\textbf{Solution:} Subtract $11$ from both sides: $-3x = -9$. Divide both sides by $-3$; a negative divided by a negative is positive.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = 3}

\textbf{Check:} $-3(3) + 11 = -9 + 11$, which equals the right side.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{2}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve for $m$: \quad $\dfrac{m}{3} - 7 = -2$}

\textbf{Solution:} Add $7$ to both sides: $\dfrac{m}{3} = 5$. Multiply both sides by $3$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{m = 15}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve for $x$, then check your solution by substituting it back into the original equation. \quad $-4x - 9 = 15$}

\textbf{Solution:} Add $9$ to both sides: $-4x = 24$. Divide both sides by $-4$.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = -6}

\textbf{Check:} $-4(-6) - 9 = 24 - 9$, which equals the right side.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{15}

\vspace{0.15cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Solve for $x$, then check your solution. \quad $5(x - 3) = 45$}

\textbf{Solution:} The whole bracket is multiplied by $5$, so divide both sides by $5$ first: $x - 3 = 9$. Then add $3$ to both sides.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{x = 12}

\textbf{Check:} $5(12 - 3) = 5(9)$, which equals the right side.

$\Rightarrow$ \ans{45}
