\problem{Orange table: 2 kg $\to$ 8, 5 kg $\to$ 20, 8 kg $\to$ 32, 12 kg $\to$ 48
dollars. (a) Cost of 8 kg. (b) Constant of proportionality. (c) Equation. (d) Plot.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Read the column headed 8 straight from the table.
\ans{32}

\textbf{Solution (b):} The constant of proportionality is $k = \dfrac{c}{w}$, the cost
of one kilogram. Using the pair $(8, 32)$: $k = \dfrac{32}{8}$. Every other column
gives the same value ($\tfrac{8}{2} = \tfrac{20}{5} = \tfrac{48}{12}$), which is what
makes the relationship proportional.
\ans{k = 4}

\textbf{Solution (c) and (d):} The equation is $c = 4w$. Plotted, the four points
lie on one straight line through the origin --- the visual signature of a
proportional relationship.

\problem{Cyclist table: 1 h $\to$ 12 km, 3 h $\to$ 36, 4 h $\to$ 48, 6 h $\to$ 72.
(a) Distance in 4 hours. (b) Constant of proportionality. (c) Distance in 10 hours.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Read the table at 4 hours.
\ans{48}

\textbf{Solution (b):} $k = \dfrac{d}{t} = \dfrac{48}{4}$, so the cyclist covers
12 km each hour.
\ans{k = 12}

\textbf{Solution (c):} The equation is $d = 12t$, so at $t = 10$,
$d = 12 \times 10$. Reading it off the table is impossible --- 10 hours is not in the
table --- which is exactly why the equation is worth having.
\ans{120}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Graph of pages read against minutes. Find the unit rate and the equation.}

\textbf{Solution:} Pick a lattice point the line clearly passes through: the marked
point is $(4, 10)$, and the line goes through the origin $(0,0)$. The unit rate is
the slope,
\[
  k = \frac{\text{change in pages}}{\text{change in minutes}} = \frac{10 - 0}{4 - 0}
\]
so Amir reads two and a half pages a minute and the equation is $y = 2.5x$.
\ans{k = 2.5}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{$c = 3.5n$ for $n$ bags of soil. (a) Cost of 12 bags. (b) Bags for 70 dollars.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Substitute $n = 12$: \ $c = 3.5 \times 12$.
\ans{42}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Now the cost is known and the count is not, so solve rather
than substitute: $3.5n = 70$, so $n = 70 \div 3.5$.
Check: $3.5 \times 20 = 70$ $\checkmark$
\ans{n = 20}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Graph of litres in a tank against minutes. (a) Unit rate. (b) Litres after
10 minutes.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} The line passes through the origin and the marked point
$(8, 12)$, so the slope is $\dfrac{12 - 0}{8 - 0} = 1.5$ litres a minute.
\ans{k = 1.5}

\textbf{Solution (b):} The equation is $y = 1.5t$; at $t = 10$ minutes,
$y = 1.5 \times 10$. Note that 10 minutes lies beyond the right-hand edge of the
graph --- the equation reaches where the drawing stops.
\ans{15\ \text{L}}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of milk. (a) Constant of proportionality.
(b) Equation. (c) Flour for 9 cups of milk.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Flour per cup of milk is $\dfrac{f}{m} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
\ans{k = 1.5}

\textbf{Solution (b) and (c):} The equation is $f = \tfrac{3}{2}m$. With $m = 9$,
\[ f = \frac{3}{2}\times 9 = \frac{27}{2} \]
so 13 and a half cups of flour.
\ans{f = 13.5}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Table with $x$: 3, 5, ?, 12 and $y$: 21, 35, 56, 84. (a) Constant of
proportionality. (b) The missing $x$.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} $k = \dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{35}{5}$, and the other complete
columns agree: $\tfrac{21}{3} = 7$ and $\tfrac{84}{12} = 7$.
\ans{k = 7}

\textbf{Solution (b):} The missing entry must satisfy $7x = 56$, so
$x = 56 \div 7$.
\ans{x = 8}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Complete the table for $y = 6x$ at $x = 2$, $5$ and $9$, then plot.}

\textbf{Solution:} Substitute each $x$ into $y = 6x$:
$6 \times 2$, then $6 \times 5$, then $6 \times 9$.
\ans{y = 12} \quad at $x = 2$;

then \ans{y = 30} \quad at $x = 5$;

and \ans{y = 54} \quad at $x = 9$.

Plotted, $(2,12)$, $(5,30)$ and $(9,54)$ line up with the origin, so a ruler through
the origin and any one of them passes through all three.

\problem{Plan A: 0.25 dollars a minute. Plan B: 5 dollars plus 0.15 dollars a minute.
(a) Plan A for 40 minutes. (b) Plan B cost per minute at 40 and at 20 minutes.
(c) Is Plan B proportional?}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Plan A is proportional, so multiply:
$0.25 \times 40$.
\ans{10}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Plan B's total for 40 minutes is
$5 + 0.15 \times 40 = 11$ dollars, so the cost per minute is
$\dfrac{11}{40} = 0.275$ dollars. For 20 minutes the total is
$5 + 0.15 \times 20 = 8$ dollars, so the cost per minute is
$\dfrac{8}{20} = 0.4$ dollars.
\ans{0.275}

For the shorter month the same division gives \ans{0.4}

\textbf{Solution (c) --- model answer (open response, review by hand):}
The two figures are different, and in a proportional relationship the cost per
minute would have to be the same for every number of minutes --- that constant is
the whole point of $y = kx$. Plan B's monthly 5 dollars is charged whether you call
for 20 minutes or 40, so it gets spread more thinly the more you talk, which is why
the per-minute figure falls from 0.4 to 0.275. Plan B is therefore \emph{not}
proportional; its graph is a straight line, but one that crosses the vertical axis
at 5 rather than passing through the origin.

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Graph of two runners. (a) Each unit rate. (b) Compare them.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Both lines start at the origin, so each unit rate is just
$\dfrac{\text{metres}}{\text{seconds}}$ at any marked point. Runner $A$ passes
through $(5, 20)$: $\dfrac{20}{5} = 4$. Runner $B$ passes through $(4, 12)$:
$\dfrac{12}{4} = 3$.
\ans{4}

and for runner $B$, \ans{3}

\textbf{Solution (b):} The steeper line is the faster runner, and 4 metres a second
beats 3 metres a second.
\ans{4 > 3}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{$d = 45t$ kilometres after $t$ hours. (a) Distance in 2.5 hours.
(b) Hours to travel 315 km.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Substitute $t = 2.5$: \ $d = 45 \times 2.5$.
\ans{112.5\ \text{km}}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Here the distance is known, so solve $45t = 315$, giving
$t = 315 \div 45$, that is 7 hours.
Check: $45 \times 7 = 315$ $\checkmark$
\ans{t = 7}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Poster orders: 8 posters for 24 dollars, 20 posters for 60 dollars.
(a) Constant of proportionality. (b) Equation. (c) Cost of 35. (d) Posters for 102
dollars. (e) Plot.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} From the second order, $k = \dfrac{c}{n} = \dfrac{60}{20}$.
The first order agrees: $\tfrac{24}{8} = 3$, which is the check that the shop really
does charge proportionally.
\ans{k = 3}

\textbf{Solution (b) and (c):} The equation is $c = 3n$, so 35 posters cost
$3 \times 35$.
\ans{105}

\textbf{Solution (d):} Now the cost is given, so solve: $3n = 102$ gives
$n = 102 \div 3$.
Check: $3 \times 34 = 102$ $\checkmark$
\ans{n = 34}

\textbf{Solution (e):} Plot $(8, 24)$ and $(20, 60)$; the line through them passes
through the origin, and every question above can also be read off that line.
