\begin{center}
\textit{Adding, subtracting, and finding the missing number, all mixed together
so you decide what each one is asking}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{What to do.} These problems are mixed up on purpose. Look at
each one before you start and ask yourself: \emph{am I putting groups together,
taking some away, or hunting for a number that is missing?}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item A plus sign means put the groups together.
\item A minus sign means take some away.
\item A blank line means a number is missing. It might be at the start, in the
middle, or at the end. Fill the blank so both sides of the $=$ sign are the
same.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{You may use the number line.} Hop forward to add and hop
backward to subtract. Counters, fingers, or drawings are all fine too --- show
how you worked it out in the space under each problem.
