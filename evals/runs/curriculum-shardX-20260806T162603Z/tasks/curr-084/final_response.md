# Fractions: Equal Parts Fix-It (Grade 3) — what you received

Three PDFs:

- **Student worksheet** (`ws_fracmodels_curr084.pdf`) — 8 misconception and
  error-analysis problems on 3.NF.A, all about one idea: a fraction can only be
  named when the parts are equal. Four find-and-fix items (the grid rectangle,
  the number line, the folded strip, and the garden) show a student's wrong
  claim to correct; every model is drawn on the page.
- **Step-by-step answer key** (`ak_fracmodels_curr084.pdf`) — worked reasoning
  for every part, a quick-answers bank for fast grading, a "Common wrong
  answers" note for the two planted traps, and a model answer for each
  explanation so you know what to accept.
- **Study guide** (`ss_fracmodels_curr084.pdf`) — three short skills (check
  whether parts are equal, write the fraction from an equal-parts model, fix an
  unequal model by counting equal squares), each with a rule box, a worked
  example, and a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** the worksheet's 16 responses split 9 machine-checked and
7 instructor-judged. Every numeric answer (the fractions, the square counts,
the 2 cm tick spacing, the 10 cm half-strip) was recomputed by SymPy, and the
two planted wrong answers (the false 1/3 and the false 1/4) were machine-checked
to be distinguishably wrong. The written explanations in problems 2-8 and the
drawing in problem 8 are genuinely open responses: the key flags them with a
dash and gives the rubric an instructor grades against, matching the key's own
"What is verified" note. All six study-guide examples and try-its are
machine-verified as well.
