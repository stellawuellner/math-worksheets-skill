# Sort Shapes Like a Mathematician (Grade 1, defining attributes)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_shapesort_curr047.pdf`) — 10 sorting problems, every
  one about defining attributes (sides and corners) rather than color, size,
  or tilt. It opens with Ada's worked sorting pattern, then ramps through
  count-the-group, two-group sorts, group comparisons, an odd-one-out, two
  "is she right?" attribute arguments (big vs small triangles, the tilted
  "diamond" square), and a seven-shape challenge sort with a built-in
  self-check sum. No two problems share an identical skeleton, and the
  drawn shape sets include distractors (circles, pentagons, gray fills,
  tilted shapes).
- **Answer key** (`ak_shapesort_curr047.pdf`) — the counting reasoning for
  every group, the accepted letters for each sort, model answers for the
  "say why" parts, and a quick-answers column.
- **Study guide** (`ss_shapesort_curr047.pdf`) — three skills matching the
  sheet's facets: sort by sides, sort by corners (and compare group sizes),
  and defining attributes vs looks, each with rule + worked example +
  try-it, plus a watch-out ("if you cannot COUNT it, do not sort by it").

**What is verified:** 17 of the 22 responses are machine-checked — every
group count, difference, sum, and the group-size comparison. The 5 open
responses (writing group letters and the two "say why" explanations) are
flagged for instructor judgement, with the accepted letters and model
explanations stated in the key.
