\problem{Oven: $T(t) = 20t + a$ for $0 \le t < 5$ and $T(t) = 150 - 2t$ for
$t \ge 5$, in degrees Celsius. Find $a$ so $T$ is continuous at $t = 5$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Continuity at $t = 5$ needs the two one-sided limits to agree.
\begin{align*}
  \lim_{t \to 5^-} T(t) &= 20(5) + a = 100 + a \\
  \lim_{t \to 5^+} T(t) &= 150 - 2(5) = 140
\end{align*}
Set them equal: $100 + a = 140$, so $a = 40$. (Check: the second piece already
gives $T(5) = 140$, so all three conditions hold.) \ans{a = 40\ \text{degrees}}

\problem{Tank: $V(x) = 4x + 6$ for $0 \le x < 6$ and $V(x) = kx$ for $x \ge 6$,
in litres. Find $k$ so $V$ is continuous at $x = 6$.}

\textbf{Solution:} The left-hand limit at $x = 6$ is $4(6) + 6 = 30$, and the
right-hand limit is $k(6) = 6k$. Continuity forces
\[
  30 = 6k \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad k = 5 .
\]
So the tank holds 30 litres at the six-minute mark and the second stage must
start from that same 30 litres. \ans{k = 5}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem{Sensor: $r(x) = \dfrac{x^2-25}{x-5}$ for $x \neq 5$, with $r(5) = c$.
Choose $c$ so $r$ is continuous at $x = 5$.}

\textbf{Solution:} A removable discontinuity is filled by the limit, so compute
it. Direct substitution gives $\frac{0}{0}$; factor and cancel:
\[
  \frac{x^2 - 25}{x - 5} = \frac{(x-5)(x+5)}{x-5} = x + 5 \qquad (x \neq 5).
\]
Hence $\lim_{x \to 5} r(x) = 5 + 5 = 10$, and continuity requires $c$ to equal
that limit. \ans{c = 10}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem{Drone: $h(t) = t^2 + a$ for $0 \le t < 3$ and $h(t) = 4t + 3$ for
$t \ge 3$, in metres. Find $a$ so $h$ is continuous at $t = 3$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Match the one-sided limits at $t = 3$:
\[
  3^2 + a = 4(3) + 3 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 9 + a = 15 .
\]
So $a = 6$. The drone is 15 metres up when the flight law changes, and $a$ is the
launch-pad offset that makes the first piece reach exactly that height.
\ans{a = 6\ \text{m}}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.2cm}

\problem{Calibration: $E(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x+7} - 3}{x - 2}$ for $x \neq 2$, with
$E(2) = c$. Choose $c$ so $E$ is continuous at $x = 2$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Again $c$ must be the limit. Substitution gives
$\frac{3-3}{0} = \frac{0}{0}$ and nothing factors, so multiply by the conjugate:
\begin{align*}
  \frac{\sqrt{x+7}-3}{x-2}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{x+7}+3}{\sqrt{x+7}+3}
    &= \frac{(x+7) - 9}{(x-2)\left(\sqrt{x+7}+3\right)} \\[2pt]
    &= \frac{x-2}{(x-2)\left(\sqrt{x+7}+3\right)}
     = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x+7}+3} \qquad (x \neq 2).
\end{align*}
At $x = 2$ this is $\frac{1}{3+3}$, so the stored value must be
\ans{c = \tfrac{1}{6}}

\problem{Conveyor: $f(x) = x^2$ for $x < 2$, $f(x) = ax + b$ for $2 \le x < 5$,
$f(x) = 3x + 1$ for $x \ge 5$. Find $a$ and $b$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Each junction gives one equation.
\begin{align*}
  \text{at } x = 2: \quad 2^2 &= a(2) + b &&\Longrightarrow&& 2a + b = 4 \\
  \text{at } x = 5: \quad a(5) + b &= 3(5) + 1 &&\Longrightarrow&& 5a + b = 16
\end{align*}
From the first equation $b = 4 - 2a$. Substituting into the second gives
$5a + (4 - 2a) = 16$, so $3a = 12$ and \ans{a = 4}

Back-substituting, $2(4) + b = 4$, so \ans{b = -4}

Check: the middle piece is $4x - 4$, which equals $4$ at $x = 2$ and $16$ at
$x = 5$ --- both junctions close.

\problem{Phone plan: $C(g) = 30 + 12g$ for $0 \le g < 4$ and $C(g) = a + 7g$ for
$g \ge 4$, in dollars. (a) Find $a$. (b) What would $a = 45$ do to a bill near
4 gigabytes?}

\textbf{Solution (a):} The left-hand limit at $g = 4$ is $30 + 12(4) = 78$ and
the right-hand limit is $a + 7(4) = a + 28$. Setting them equal,
\[
  78 = a + 28 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad a = 50 .
\]
So the second tier is $C(g) = 50 + 7g$, which is also 78 dollars at 4 gigabytes.
\ans{a = 50}

\textbf{Solution (b) --- instructor-judged, no machine check.} With $a = 45$ the
second piece gives $45 + 7(4) = 73$ dollars at 4 gigabytes while the first piece
climbs to 78 dollars just below it: the bill \emph{drops} by 5 dollars as usage
crosses 4 gigabytes. Full credit identifies that 5-dollar downward jump and says
what it means --- a customer using slightly more than 4 gigabytes would be
charged less than one using slightly less, which is why the plan is written to be
continuous. Accept any wording that names the jump and its consequence; do not
accept a bare ``it would not be continuous'' with no consequence stated.

\problem{Challenge. $g(x) = \dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ for $x < 4$, $g(4) = a$, and
$g(x) = bx$ for $x > 4$. Find $a$ and $b$.}

\textbf{Solution:} All three continuity conditions must hold at $x = 4$: the
left-hand limit, the right-hand limit, and the value $g(4)$ must be one number.

\textbf{Left-hand limit.} Factor and cancel:
$\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4} = \dfrac{(x-4)(x+4)}{x-4} = x+4$ for $x \neq 4$, so
$\lim_{x \to 4^-} g(x) = 4 + 4 = 8$. Since $g(4) = a$ must equal that,
\ans{a = 8}

\textbf{Right-hand limit.} $\lim_{x \to 4^+} g(x) = b(4) = 4b$, and this must
equal the same 8, so $4b = 8$ and \ans{b = 2}

With $a = 8$ and $b = 2$ every piece meets at the single value 8, so $g$ is
continuous at $x = 4$.
