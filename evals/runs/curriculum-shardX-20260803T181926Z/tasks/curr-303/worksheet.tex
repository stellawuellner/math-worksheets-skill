\wstitleblock{Completing Short Algebraic and Segment Proofs}{High School Geometry}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Justifying each step of an algebraic solution, proving segment
relationships with the Segment Addition Postulate, and using the definitions of
midpoint and congruent segments}
\end{center}

\noindent\small
\textbf{Every line of a proof needs a reason.} A statement is what you know; a
reason is the definition, postulate, property or theorem that lets you write
it. The reasons you will need on this sheet:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Properties of equality} --- Addition, Subtraction,
        Multiplication, Division, Substitution, and the Distributive Property.
  \item \textbf{Segment Addition Postulate} --- if $B$ is between $A$ and $C$,
        then $AB + BC = AC$.
  \item \textbf{Definition of midpoint} --- if $M$ is the midpoint of
        $\overline{AB}$, then $\overline{AM} \cong \overline{MB}$.
  \item \textbf{Definition of congruent segments} --- $\overline{AB} \cong
        \overline{CD}$ exactly when $AB = CD$.
\end{itemize}
All lengths on this sheet are in centimetres. Write the requested value on the
answer line, and fill every blank cell of a proof table.
\normalsize
