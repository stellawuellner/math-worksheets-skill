# Reading and Comparing Multi-Digit Numbers — Grades 4–5

Here are the three PDFs for this practice set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_placevalue_curr101.pdf`) — 10 problems of guided concept
  practice on reading and comparing multi-digit whole numbers. It opens with
  place-value chart models (the value of a digit), moves through standard,
  expanded, and word forms, then compares and orders numbers. Problem 9 asks
  your child to correct a classmate's comparison mistake and explain it, and
  problem 10 is a two-part stadium-attendance challenge. Difficulty ramps from
  warm-up (level 1–2) to a level-4 finisher.
- **Answer key** (`ak_placevalue_curr101.pdf`) — a quick-answers bank for fast
  grading up front, then a full step-by-step solution for every problem,
  including the model explanation for the "what did Maya forget?" item. A
  Curriculum section lists the standards covered (4.NBT.A.1, 4.NBT.A.2) and the
  difficulty range.
- **Study guide** (`ss_placevalue_curr101.pdf`) — a two-page reference card with
  one section per skill tested: value of a digit, number forms, and comparing
  numbers. Each section has the rule, a short worked example, and a "try it"
  with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** 11 of the 12 answers on this sheet are machine-checked
with a computer algebra system — every numeric value, every comparison symbol,
and the ordering in problem 7. One response is instructor-judged: problem 9's
written explanation of Maya's mistake, which is flagged for manual review, and
the answer key states exactly what a full-credit explanation must contain. The
key's own "What is verified" note reflects this same split.
