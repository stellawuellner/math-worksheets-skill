% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 1
\problem{A rectangle is 6 cm long and 4 cm wide. What is its area?}

\textbf{Solution:} Area is length times width: $6 \times 4 = 24$. The area is
\ans{24\ \text{cm}^2}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 2
\problem{A pen is 9 m long and 3 m wide. What is its perimeter?}

\textbf{Solution:} Perimeter adds all four sides: $2 \times (9 + 3) = 24$.
The perimeter is \ans{24\ \text{m}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 3
\problem{Each grid square is one square unit. Find the area of the L-shape.}

\textbf{Solution:} Split along the grid: the bottom bar is 4 squares long and
1 square tall (4 squares), and the left column above it is 2 squares wide and
2 squares tall (4 squares). Together: $4 + 4 = 8$, so the area is
\ans{8\ \text{square units}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 4
\problem{Walk around the L-shaped patio with sides 8, 3, 4, 3, 4, and 6 cm.
What is the perimeter?}

\textbf{Solution:} Add every side as you walk around the edge:
$8 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 4 + 6 = 28$. The perimeter is \ans{28\ \text{cm}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 5
\problem{Find the area of the L-shaped playground (sides 10, 2, 5, 2, 5, 4 m).}

\textbf{Solution:} Split along the dashed line: the bottom rectangle is
10 m by 2 m ($10 \times 2 = 20$) and the top-left rectangle is 5 m by 2 m
($5 \times 2 = 10$). Total area: $20 + 10 = 30$, so the playground covers
\ans{30\ \text{m}^2}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 6
\problem{An L-shaped rug joins a 7 m by 2 m piece and a 3 m by 2 m piece.
What is the total area?}

\textbf{Solution:} Find each rectangle's area, then add:
$7 \times 2 = 14$ and $3 \times 2 = 6$, so $14 + 6 = 20$. The rug covers
\ans{20\ \text{m}^2}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 7
\problem{Find the area of the L-shaped sticker (sides 10, 3, 5, 3, 5, 6 cm).}

\textbf{Solution:} Split along the dashed line: the bottom rectangle is
10 cm by 3 cm ($10 \times 3 = 30$) and the top-left rectangle is 5 cm by 3 cm
($5 \times 3 = 15$). Total: $30 + 15 = 45$, so the area is
\ans{45\ \text{cm}^2}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 8
\problem{A photo frame is 8 cm by 6 cm. How many centimeters of wooden edge go
around it?}

\textbf{Solution:} ``Around'' means perimeter: $2 \times (8 + 6) = 28$.
The edge is \ans{28\ \text{cm}} long.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 9
\problem{Missing sides! The shape shows sides 9, 3, 4, ?, ?, 5 cm.
(a) Find the two missing sides. (b) Find the perimeter.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} The two top pieces together must match the 9 cm bottom,
so the missing top side is $9 - 4 = 5$ cm. \ans{5} \par
The two right-hand pieces together must match the 5 cm left side, so the
missing short side is $5 - 3 = 2$ cm. \ans{2}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Now add every side around the shape:
$9 + 3 + 4 + 2 + 5 + 5 = 28$. The perimeter is \ans{28\ \text{cm}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 10
\problem{A deck joins a 6 m by 3 m rectangle and a 4 m by 2 m rectangle.
What is the total area?}

\textbf{Solution:} Add the two rectangle areas: $6 \times 3 = 18$ and
$4 \times 2 = 8$, so $18 + 8 = 26$. The deck covers \ans{26\ \text{m}^2}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 11
\problem{The staircase shape has a 6 m bottom, a 6 m left side, and every step
piece is 2 m. What is the perimeter?}

\textbf{Solution:} Walk around: the bottom is 6, then six step pieces of 2
each ($2+2+2+2+2+2 = 12$), then the left side of 6. Add them:
$6 + 12 + 6 = 24$. The perimeter is \ans{24\ \text{m}}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Problem 12
\problem{Challenge. The garden has sides 12, 2, 6, 2, 6, 4 m. (a) Area?
(b) Perimeter? (c) Compare the two numbers.}

\textbf{Solution (a):} Split along the dashed line: bottom rectangle
12 m by 2 m ($12 \times 2 = 24$) plus top-left rectangle 6 m by 2 m
($6 \times 2 = 12$): $24 + 12 = 36$. The area is \ans{36\ \text{m}^2}

\textbf{Solution (b):} Add every side: $12 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 6 + 4 = 32$.
The perimeter is \ans{32\ \text{m}}

\textbf{Solution (c):} 36 is more than 32, so \ans{36 > 32} --- here the area
number is bigger, but that is not true for every shape!
