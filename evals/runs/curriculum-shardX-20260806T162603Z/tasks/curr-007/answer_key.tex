\problem{Full top row, one dot below: bottom row and total.}

\textbf{Solution:} The bottom row holds \ans{1}

The full top row is 5, so count on one: $5 + 1 = 6$. In all \ans{6}

\problem{Full top row, two dots below: bottom row and total.}

\textbf{Solution:} The bottom row holds \ans{2}

Five and two more: $5 + 2 = 7$. In all \ans{7}

\problem{Full top row, three dots below: bottom row and total.}

\textbf{Solution:} The bottom row holds \ans{3}

Five and three more: $5 + 3 = 8$. In all \ans{8}

\problem{Almost-full frame: empty boxes and total.}

\textbf{Solution:} Just one box has no dot: \ans{1}

One empty box means one less than ten: $10 - 1 = 9$. Dots in all \ans{9}

\problem{Build 8 on an empty frame: dots per row.}

\textbf{Solution:} Fill the top row first --- it takes \ans{5}

Count on to 8: ``6, 7, 8'' puts three dots below, $8 - 5 = 3$. Bottom row
\ans{3} \quad Check the drawing: the top row full and 3 below.

\problem{Completely full frame: total and empty boxes.}

\textbf{Solution:} A full frame is the picture of ten --- no counting needed:
\ans{10}

Every box is used, so the empty boxes number \ans{0}

\problem{Full top row, four dots below: bottom row and total.}

\textbf{Solution:} The bottom row holds \ans{4}

Five and four more: $5 + 4 = 9$. In all \ans{9}

\problem{Build 6 on an empty frame: dots per row.}

\textbf{Solution:} The top row fills first with \ans{5}

Count on to 6: one more dot goes below, $6 - 5 = 1$. Bottom row \ans{1}
\quad Check the drawing: full top row and a single dot below.

\problem{Frame with two empty boxes: empty boxes and total.}

\textbf{Solution:} Two boxes have no dot: \ans{2}

Two less than ten: $10 - 2 = 8$. Dots in all \ans{8}

\problem{Challenge: full top row and 4 below --- total, then tell how the
frame helps.}

\textbf{Solution:} Five and four more: $5 + 4 = 9$. In all \ans{9}

\textbf{Telling how (instructor-judged):} accept either frame shortcut said in
the child's own words --- ``the top row is 5, so I counted on 6, 7, 8, 9,'' or
``one box is empty, so it is one less than 10.'' Counting all nine dots one by
one gets the answer but is not the frame shortcut this asks for.
