# Place Value with Base-Ten Models — Grade 2

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_placevalue_curr051.pdf`) — 8 guided concept problems on reading
  hundreds, tens, and ones from base-ten models. Drawn flat/rod/cube models on
  problems 1, 3, and 6 connect directly to the notation (e.g. 200 + 40 + 6), with a
  zero-placeholder model (308), a model comparison, and a regrouping challenge
  (12 tens and 5 ones) to finish. Difficulty ramps from 1 to 4.
- **Answer key** (`ak_placevalue_curr051.pdf`) — full reasoning for every problem, a
  quick-answers bank for fast grading, a "common wrong answers" note for the two
  declared misconception traps (reading 308 as 38; giving the digit instead of its
  value), and a curriculum section (standard 2.NBT.A, difficulty range).
- **Study guide** (`ss_placevalue_curr051.pdf`) — three skills (reading a model,
  places inside a number, comparing), each with a rule box, a worked example, and a
  fresh try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**Verification split:** 12 of 13 checks are machine-verified with SymPy (every
number answer and the comparison), and the two misconception traps were proven to
give visibly wrong answers. **1 response is instructor-judged**: problem 8's written
explanation of regrouping ten tens into one hundred — the key states what a correct
explanation must contain. The key's "What is verified" note marks it the same way.
All study-guide examples and try-its are machine-verified.
