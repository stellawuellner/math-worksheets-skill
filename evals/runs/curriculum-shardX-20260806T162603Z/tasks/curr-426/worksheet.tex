\begin{center}\small\textit{Finding the third angle, solving AAS and ASA triangles, and using the law of sines in surveying problems}\end{center}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Every triangle is drawn to scale from its own given values. Side $a$ lies opposite angle $A$, side $b$ opposite $B$, side $c$ opposite $C$; the unknown you are asked for is marked with a question mark.
  \item Law of sines: $\dfrac{a}{\sin A} = \dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{c}{\sin C}$. Pair each side with the angle \emph{across} from it.
  \item If the side you know and the side you want are not both paired with a known angle, find the third angle first.
  \item Round side lengths to two decimal places; angles are exact here.
\end{itemize}
