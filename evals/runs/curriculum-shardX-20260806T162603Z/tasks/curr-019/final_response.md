# Number Bond Fix-It Practice (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_bonds_curr019.pdf`) — 6 problems on correcting incomplete
  and duplicated number-bond families for numbers to 10. Every problem shows
  its own number-bond picture. It ramps from finding a missing part (1–2),
  through finishing an incomplete fact family (3), to three find-and-fix
  items: a bond with a wrong part (4), a family with a duplicated fact (5),
  and a challenge that asks why every two-part bond makes four facts (6).
- **Answer key** (`ak_bonds_curr019.pdf`) — step-by-step reasoning for every
  problem (count-on strategies written out), a quick-answers bank for fast
  grading, a "Common wrong answers" note for the two planted mistakes, and a
  curriculum section (K.OA.A, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_bonds_curr019.pdf`) — three skills, each with a rule
  box, a worked example, and a fresh try-it: finding a missing part,
  finishing a fact family, and checking/fixing a broken bond.

**What is verified:** 8 of the 11 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every missing part, every family fact, and both corrections in the
find-and-fix items. The two planted wrong results are declared as
misconception traps and machine-proven distinguishable (keeping Ana's wrong
part 5; copying 10−3=7 instead of writing 10−7). The 3 "tell how/why you
know" explanations on problems 4, 5, and 6 are genuinely open responses:
they are flagged for instructor review, and the answer key states what a
correct explanation must contain. The key's own "What is verified" note
reflects the same split.
