\wstitleblock{Rounding Multi-Digit Numbers to Named Places}{Grade 4--5 Mathematics}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Rounding on a number line, rounding to a named place, estimating a sum
or difference by rounding first, and rounding real counts and distances}
\end{center}

\noindent\small
\textbf{Use the number line every time.} Rounding to a named place means
choosing between the two multiples of that place the number sits between.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Find the two benchmarks --- the multiple of the place just below the
        number and the one just above it.
  \item Mark the halfway point between them. It is always the lower benchmark
        plus half of the place.
  \item If the number lands past halfway, round up; before halfway, round down;
        exactly on halfway, round up.
  \item Every digit to the right of the named place becomes a zero. Nothing to
        the left of it changes, unless rounding up carries into it.
\end{itemize}
Numbers on this sheet are written without commas.
\normalsize
