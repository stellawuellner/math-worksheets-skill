\problem{Corresponding angles $(2x + 14)^\circ$ and $(3x - 6)^\circ$, $j \parallel k$. Find $x$.}

\textbf{Solution:} corresponding angles on parallel lines are congruent, so set the measures equal.
\begin{align*}
  2x + 14 &= 3x - 6 \\
  20 &= x
\end{align*}
Check: both angles measure $2(20) + 14 = 54$ and $3(20) - 6 = 54$. \ans{x = 20}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Same-side interior angles $(3x)^\circ$ and $(2x + 30)^\circ$, $j \parallel k$. Find $x$.}

\textbf{Solution:} same-side interior angles are supplementary --- add, do not equate.
\begin{align*}
  3x + (2x + 30) &= 180 \\
  5x + 30 &= 180 \\
  5x &= 150
\end{align*}
Check: $3(30) = 90$ and $2(30) + 30 = 90$, and $90 + 90 = 180$. \ans{x = 30}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Alternate interior: $m\angle 3 = (5x - 12)^\circ$, $m\angle 6 = (3x + 18)^\circ$. (a) $x$. (b) $m\angle 3$.}

\textbf{(a)} Alternate interior angles on parallel lines are congruent:
\begin{align*}
  5x - 12 &= 3x + 18 \\
  2x &= 30
\end{align*}
So $x = 15$. \ans{x = 15}

\textbf{(b)} $m\angle 3 = 5(15) - 12 = 75 - 12$. Check: $m\angle 6 = 3(15) + 18 = 63$ too. \ans{63^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{$m\angle 1 = 47^\circ$, $j \parallel k$. Find $m\angle 7$.}

\textbf{Solution, two links:} $\angle 1$ and $\angle 5$ are corresponding angles, so $m\angle 5 = 47$. Then $\angle 5$ and $\angle 7$ are a linear pair along the transversal at the lower vertex, so their measures add to $180$: $m\angle 7 = 180 - 47$. \ans{133^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Same-side interior: $m\angle 4 = (4x - 5)^\circ$, $m\angle 6 = (6x + 15)^\circ$. (a) $x$. (b) $m\angle 4$.}

\textbf{(a)}
\begin{align*}
  (4x - 5) + (6x + 15) &= 180 \\
  10x + 10 &= 180 \\
  10x &= 170
\end{align*}
So $x = 17$. \ans{x = 17}

\textbf{(b)} $m\angle 4 = 4(17) - 5 = 68 - 5$. Check: $m\angle 6 = 6(17) + 15 = 117$, and $63 + 117 = 180$. \ans{63^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{$m\angle 4 = (3x + 5)^\circ$, $m\angle 5 = (x + 45)^\circ$, alternate interior. (a) $x$. (b) $m\angle 6$.}

\textbf{(a)}
\begin{align*}
  3x + 5 &= x + 45 \\
  2x &= 40
\end{align*}
So $x = 20$. \ans{x = 20}

\textbf{(b)} $m\angle 4 = 3(20) + 5 = 65$. Now $\angle 4$ and $\angle 6$ are same-side interior angles, so they are supplementary: $m\angle 6 = 180 - 65$. Check: $m\angle 5 = 20 + 45 = 65$, which agrees with $\angle 4$. \ans{115^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Alternate exterior: $m\angle 7 = (8x - 20)^\circ$, $m\angle 2 = (5x + 25)^\circ$. (a) $x$. (b) $m\angle 2$.}

\textbf{(a)} Alternate exterior angles on parallel lines are congruent:
\begin{align*}
  8x - 20 &= 5x + 25 \\
  3x &= 45
\end{align*}
So $x = 15$. \ans{x = 15}

\textbf{(b)} $m\angle 2 = 5(15) + 25 = 75 + 25$. Check: $m\angle 7 = 8(15) - 20 = 100$ too. \ans{100^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Same-side interior: $m\angle 3 = (2x + 8)^\circ$, $m\angle 5 = (4x + 4)^\circ$. (a) $x$. (b) $m\angle 5$.}

\textbf{(a)}
\begin{align*}
  (2x + 8) + (4x + 4) &= 180 \\
  6x + 12 &= 180 \\
  6x &= 168
\end{align*}
So $x = 28$. \ans{x = 28}

\textbf{(b)} $m\angle 5 = 4(28) + 4 = 112 + 4$. Check: $m\angle 3 = 2(28) + 8 = 64$, and $64 + 116 = 180$. \ans{116^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{Three parallel lines $p$, $q$, $r$ cut by $t$; the marked angle at $p$ is $118^\circ$. Find $m\angle W$ at $r$.}

\textbf{Solution:} corresponding angles carry the $118^\circ$ down from $p$ to $q$, and from $q$ down to $r$ --- with three parallel lines the same transversal makes the same angle at each. The marked angle $W$ at $r$ sits between $q$ and $r$ on the other side of $t$ from the carried angle, so the two are same-side interior and therefore supplementary: $m\angle W = 180 - 118$. \ans{62^\circ}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\problem{$m\angle 3 = (5x + 3)^\circ$, $m\angle 8 = (7x - 27)^\circ$. (a) Name the chain. (b) $x$. (c) $m\angle 8$.}

\textbf{(a)} Two links. First, $\angle 3$ and $\angle 7$ are \textbf{corresponding angles} --- both lie below their own line and on the same side of $t$ --- so with $j \parallel k$ they are congruent. Second, $\angle 7$ and $\angle 8$ are a \textbf{linear pair} along line $k$, so their measures add to $180$. Putting the links together, $m\angle 3 = m\angle 7$ and $m\angle 7 + m\angle 8 = 180$, so $m\angle 3 + m\angle 8 = 180$: the two are \emph{supplementary}, which is why the expressions must be added and not set equal.

\textbf{(b)}
\begin{align*}
  (5x + 3) + (7x - 27) &= 180 \\
  12x - 24 &= 180 \\
  12x &= 204
\end{align*}
So $x = 17$. \ans{x = 17}

\textbf{(c)} $m\angle 8 = 7(17) - 27 = 119 - 27$. Check: $m\angle 3 = 5(17) + 3 = 88$, and $88 + 92 = 180$. \ans{92^\circ}

\textbf{Grading (a):} both link names, and the conclusion that a congruent link followed by a supplementary link leaves the endpoints supplementary.
