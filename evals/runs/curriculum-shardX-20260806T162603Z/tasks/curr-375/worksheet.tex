\wstitleblock{Radical Equations, Domains, and Decimals}{Algebra 2}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Finding the domain of a radical, solving radical equations, rejecting extraneous candidates, and reporting rounded decimal answers}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Problems 1--3 warm up one skill each. From problem 4 on the skills are mixed, so read each problem and decide what it is asking for.
  \item A square root is defined only where its radicand is at least $0$; a radical in a \emph{denominator} needs its radicand strictly greater than $0$.
  \item Squaring can create candidates the original equation never had. Test every candidate in the original equation before reporting it.
  \item Where a problem asks for a decimal, round to two places and give the unit stated.
\end{itemize}
