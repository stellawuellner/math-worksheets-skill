\wstitleblock{Seeing How Many Without Counting}{Kindergarten--Grade 1}{}

\noindent\textbf{Directions --- read these with your grown-up or teacher:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Look at the picture for a moment, then \emph{say} how many. Try not to
        point and count each dot one at a time.
  \item Write the number in the answer blank.
  \item If you are not sure, you may check by counting --- but say the number first.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent\textbf{How the pictures are drawn.} A \emph{dot card} shows loose dots.
A \emph{five-frame} is a strip of five boxes: when every box is filled it shows five,
so an empty box tells you how far you are from five. No numbers are printed on the
pictures --- the picture is the number.
