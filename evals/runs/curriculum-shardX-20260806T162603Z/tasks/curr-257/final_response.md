# Multistep equations with rational coefficients — Algebra 1

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Study guide** (`ss_rational_curr257.pdf`, 2 pages) — three sections: clearing the fractions by
  multiplying both sides by a common denominator, handling a fraction in front of a bracket (two
  legal routes, and when each is quicker), and gathering the variable when it appears on both
  sides. The third rule box also covers the two special endings — variable terms cancelling to a
  true statement (every number works) or to a false one (no solution). Each section has a worked
  example that opens with the strategy sentence, and a try-it with the answer upside down inside
  the box.
- **Worksheet** (`ws_rational_curr257.pdf`, 5 pages) — 12 problems, difficulty 1 → 5, no two built
  on the same skeleton: single fraction, fraction times a bracket, variable on both sides, decimal
  coefficients, a fraction bar over a whole binomial, two fractions subtracted (where the second
  numerator changes sign), and finally an equation whose variable terms cancel and which therefore
  has no solution at all.
- **Answer key** (`ak_rational_curr257.pdf`, 4 pages) — every problem solved step by step with a
  substitution check back into the original equation, plus notes on the two steps most often
  missed (multiplying the fraction-free terms by the common denominator, and changing the sign of
  a whole subtracted numerator). Includes a Quick Answers bank and a Curriculum block
  (HSA-REI.B.3, difficulty 1–5).

## What is verified, honestly

- **11 of the 12 problems are fully machine-checked** by SymPy — every solution recomputed
  independently of the key before anything compiled. Problem 12's *solution set* is machine-checked
  too: SymPy confirms it is empty, which is why the Quick Answers bank prints the empty-set symbol
  rather than a number.
- **Problem 12(b) is instructor-judged.** It asks the student to say what the line $-2 = 4$ means,
  and that is prose. The key prints the rubric — full credit says the false numerical statement
  means no value of $x$ works; writing $x = 0$ or trying to "solve" $-2 = 4$ does not. The bank
  marks it `—` rather than claiming it was checked.
- No blanks are marked as working space; every printed blank is an answer.

The grade level prints on the answer key only.
