\wstitleblock{Find and Fix: Does the Data Really Show a Cause?}{Grade 8 / Pre-Algebra}{}

\vspace{0.05cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.9cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Two quantities are \textbf{associated} when they rise or fall together. That is a pattern in the data, and nothing more.
  \item A \textbf{cause} is a much stronger claim. Data collected by watching (a survey) can show association; only a fair experiment, where the researcher decides who gets what, can support a cause.
  \item Watch for a \textbf{third factor} that pushes both quantities at once, and for a claim that has the direction backwards.
  \item Where a student's work is shown, correct it \textit{and} say what went wrong.
\end{itemize}
