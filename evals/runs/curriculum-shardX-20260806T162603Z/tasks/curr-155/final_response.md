Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 interleaved ratio-synthesis review:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_ratio_synthesis_curr155.pdf`) — 12 problems on choosing a
  ratio representation to solve multistep comparisons. Three warm-ups (one per
  tool: unit rate, scaling a table, tape diagram), then the tools are
  deliberately interleaved: better-buy and price-per-pound comparisons,
  scaling two readers/hikers to a common amount, comparing the larger shares
  of two different prize splits, a trap-style train problem where the raw
  distances mislead (both speeds are 70 km/h), one open-choice representation
  problem, and a final synthesis challenge that needs one tool for part (a)
  and another for part (b).
- **Answer key** (`ak_ratio_synthesis_curr155.pdf`) — quick-answers table with
  each sub-part labelled, generated curriculum section (6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3,
  difficulty 1–5), and worked solutions that name the representation used at
  every step.
- **Study guide** (`ss_ratio_synthesis_curr155.pdf`) — three sections matching
  the three tools, each with a "when to choose this" rule, a worked example,
  and a try-it, plus a watch-out about comparing raw numbers from
  different-sized batches.

**What is verified:** 27 of the 29 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every unit rate, every scaled amount, every share, and every comparison
(checked as a relation between the two computed rates). Two responses are
instructor-judged: the representation-choice explanations on problems 10 and
12; the key states what earns full credit on each. All ten study-guide checks
(examples and try-its) are machine-verified.
