\problem{\textbf{Area model for $4(x+7)$.}
The height $4$ multiplies \emph{each} piece of the base, because the two little
rectangles together make the whole rectangle:
\[ 4 \cdot x = 4x \qquad\text{and}\qquad 4 \cdot 7 = 28 \]
Total area $= 4x + 28$ square metres. \ans{4x + 28}
The model is the reason the rule works: splitting the base splits the area.}

\problem{\textbf{Five rooms of $(3x-2)$ minutes.}
Five equal boxes means $5$ copies added:
\[ 5(3x-2) = 5 \cdot 3x - 5 \cdot 2 = 15x - 10 \]
The minus sign belongs to the $2$, so the second product is subtracted.
Total time $= \ans{15x - 10}$ minutes.}

\problem{\textbf{Combine $7x + 3 + 2x + 8$.}
Sort first: the $x$-terms are $7x$ and $2x$; the number terms are $3$ and $8$.
\[ (7x + 2x) + (3 + 8) = 9x + 11 \]
Only like terms combine --- $9x$ and $11$ count different things (rows of tiles
versus single tiles), so the answer keeps both. \ans{9x + 11}}

\problem{\textbf{Combine $8m + 5 - 3m + 2$.}
Each term keeps the sign in front of it, so the $m$-terms are $+8m$ and $-3m$:
\[ (8m - 3m) + (5 + 2) = 5m + 7 \]
Tia has $\ans{5m + 7}$ markers. Check with $m = 10$: the original gives
$80 + 5 - 30 + 2 = 57$, and $5(10) + 7 = 57$ $\checkmark$.}

\problem{\textbf{Simplify $3(x+4) + 2x$.}
Distribute over the three curved sections first, then combine:
\begin{align*}
3(x+4) + 2x &= 3x + 12 + 2x \\
            &= (3x + 2x) + 12 \\
            &= 5x + 12
\end{align*}
Total path length $= \ans{5x + 12}$ metres. Note $2x$ is not inside the
parentheses, so it is never multiplied by $3$.}

\problem{\textbf{Factor $6x + 15$.}
List the factors: $6x = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot x$ and $15 = 3 \cdot 5$. The greatest
common factor is $3$, so the height of the model is $3$ and the two base pieces
are $6x \div 3 = 2x$ and $15 \div 3 = 5$:
\[ 6x + 15 = 3(2x + 5) \]
Area as a product: \ans{3(2x + 5)}. Distributing back gives $6x + 15$
$\checkmark$ --- factoring is the area model run backwards.}

\problem{\textbf{Simplify $4(2x+3) - 3(x-2)$.}
Distribute both products, and give the second one the subtraction sign it owns:
\begin{align*}
4(2x+3) &= 8x + 12 \\
-3(x-2) &= -3x + 6 \\
8x + 12 - 3x + 6 &= (8x - 3x) + (12 + 6) = 5x + 18
\end{align*}
Badges kept $= \ans{5x + 18}$. The trap is $-3 \cdot (-2)$: a negative times a
negative is $+6$, so the constants grow rather than shrink.}

\problem{\textbf{Factor $12n - 18$.}
$12n = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot n$ and $18 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 3$, so the greatest
common factor is $6$. Dividing each piece by $6$ gives $2n$ and $3$:
\[ 12n - 18 = 6(2n - 3) \]
Remaining area as a product: \ans{6(2n - 3)}.
Pulling out only $2$ or only $3$ is not wrong arithmetic, but it is not the
\emph{greatest} common factor, so the model would still have a shared factor
left inside.}

\problem{\textbf{Total tutoring cost $5(x+3) + 4x$ dollars.}
(a) Distribute, then combine like terms:
\[ 5(x+3) + 4x = 5x + 15 + 4x = 9x + 15 \]
(b) Substitute $x = 7$ into the simplified expression:
\[ 9(7) + 15 = 63 + 15 = 78 \]
The cost is \ans{78} dollars. Substituting into the original form is a good
check: $5(7+3) + 4 \cdot 7 = 50 + 28 = 78$ $\checkmark$ --- equivalent
expressions must agree at every value of $x$.}

\problem{\textbf{Challenge --- Jamal's error.}
Jamal multiplied the $2x$ by $3$ but left the $-5$ untouched. The distributive
property says the factor outside multiplies \emph{every} term inside, because
$3(2x-5)$ means three copies of the whole quantity $2x - 5$:
\[ 3(2x-5) = 3 \cdot 2x - 3 \cdot 5 = 6x - 15 \]
Correct expanded expression: \ans{6x - 15}.
Test the two at $x = 1$: the correct form gives $6 - 15 = -9$, which matches
$3(2 - 5) = -9$; Jamal's $6x - 5$ gives $1$, so his version is a different
expression.
\emph{Note for the grader: the written explanation is judged by you --- only the
corrected expression is machine-verified.}}
