# Signed Rational Numbers: Mixed Operations Review (Grades 6-7)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_signedops_curr170.pdf`) — 12 problems. A short warm-up
  (one addition, one subtraction, one multiplication with integers) is followed
  by an interleaved mix of fraction, decimal, and integer problems, so your
  child must choose the right sign rule each time rather than repeat one
  procedure. The sheet ends with a synthesis challenge combining addition and
  division of fractions in one expression. No two problems share an identical
  skeleton, and the four subskills named in the subtitle each appear at least
  three times.
- **Answer key** (`ak_signedops_curr170.pdf`) — step-by-step reasoning for
  every problem (which sign rule was applied and why), a quick-answers column,
  and "common wrong answers" notes for the two problems designed around classic
  errors (ignoring a double negative; dropping the sign of a product). The
  grade band and standards (7.NS.A.1, 7.NS.A.2) print here only.
- **Study guide** (`ss_signedops_curr170.pdf`) — a two-page reference card with
  one section per subskill: adding signed numbers, subtracting (adding the
  opposite), multiplying/dividing (sign rules), and mixed operations with
  method choice. Each section has the rule, a worked example that names its
  strategy, and a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** all 12 worksheet answers, all 8 study-guide worked
example/try-it answers, and both declared misconception traps were
machine-checked with a computer algebra system; the build passed every gate
with nothing flagged for manual review. There are no open-response items on
this sheet, so the answer key's verification note shows a fully machine-checked
column.
