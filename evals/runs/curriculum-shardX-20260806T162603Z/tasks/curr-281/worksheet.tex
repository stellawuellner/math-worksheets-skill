\begin{center}
\small Opening direction, axis of symmetry, vertex, and intercepts of a parabola
\end{center}
\vspace{0.15cm}

\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item For $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$: the parabola opens \textbf{upward} when $a > 0$ and \textbf{downward} when $a < 0$.
  \item The axis of symmetry is the vertical line $x = -\dfrac{b}{2a}$; the vertex sits on it, so its second coordinate is the value of $f$ at that input.
  \item The $y$-intercept is $f(0)$. The $x$-intercepts are the inputs that make $f(x) = 0$ --- factor and use the zero-product property.
  \item Write coordinates as ordered pairs and lines as equations, e.g.\ $x = 3$.
\end{itemize}
