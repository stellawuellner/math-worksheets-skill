# Number Path Subtraction (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_numberpath_curr027.pdf`) — 10 subtraction problems
  within 10, every one with its own 1–10 number path printed right in the
  problem. The ramp runs from a fully worked hop-back pattern (problem 1),
  through independent counting back, to counting up from the smaller number,
  missing-hop problems (8 − ▢ = 3), and a challenge that asks the child to
  explain how the path shows the answer. No two problems share an identical
  skeleton, and the three path methods are interleaved after the warm-up.
- **Answer key** (`ak_numberpath_curr027.pdf`) — every solution writes out
  the hop sequence, a quick-answers bank, and a curriculum section
  (1.OA.C.6, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_numberpath_curr027.pdf`) — three sections: count back
  on the path, count up on the path, and find the missing hop, each with a
  rule box, worked example, and fresh try-it, plus a watch-out about not
  counting the starting box.

**What is verified:** 10 of the 13 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every difference and both missing-hop numbers. The 3 open responses (hops
drawn on the path in problems 3 and 9, and "tell how the path shows it" on
the challenge) are flagged for instructor review, with the key stating what
correct hops look like. The key's "What is verified" note reflects the same
split.
