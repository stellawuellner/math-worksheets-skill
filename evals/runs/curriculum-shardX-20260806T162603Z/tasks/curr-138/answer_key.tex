\problem{Multiply: $3.6 \times 100$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiplying by $100$ makes every digit worth $100$ times
more, so the decimal point moves \emph{two} places to the right:
$3.6 \to 36 \to 360$. \ans{360}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Divide: $45.2 \div 10$}

\textbf{Solution:} Dividing by $10$ makes every digit worth ten times less, so
the decimal point moves \emph{one} place to the left: $45.2 \to 4.52$.
\ans{4.52}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Multiply: $0.4 \times 0.6$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiply as whole numbers: $4 \times 6 = 24$. The factors
have one decimal place each, so the product needs \emph{two} decimal places:
tenths $\times$ tenths $=$ hundredths, giving $24$ hundredths. \ans{0.24}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Notebooks: $4$ at $2.35$ dollars each}

\textbf{Solution:}
(a) Round the price to the nearest whole dollar: $2.35 \to 2$. Then estimate
$4 \times 2$, giving about \ans{8} dollars.

(b) Exact: $4 \times 2.35 = 4 \times 2 + 4 \times 0.35 = 8 + 1.40$, so the
total is \ans{9.40} dollars. The estimate of $8$ tells us $9.40$ is
reasonable.

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Divide: $7.2 \div 0.9$}

\textbf{Solution:} Multiply \emph{both} numbers by $10$ so the divisor becomes
a whole number: $7.2 \div 0.9 = 72 \div 9$. Both numbers shifted together, so
the quotient is unchanged. $72 \div 9 = 8$. \ans{8}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Watering can: $2.5$ L filled $6$ times}

\textbf{Solution:} Total $= 2.5 \times 6$. Think money: $2.5$ is $2$ and $5$
tenths, so $6$ groups make $12 + 3.0 = 15.0$. \ans{15 \text{ L}}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Ribbon: $4.5$ m cut into $0.5$-m pieces}

\textbf{Solution:} ``How many $0.5$s fit in $4.5$?'' Multiply both by $10$:
$45 \div 5 = 9$. So there are \ans{9} pieces.

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Copy paper: $2.4$ kg per box, $100$ boxes}

\textbf{Solution:} $2.4 \times 100$ moves the decimal point two places to the
right: $2.4 \to 24 \to 240$. \ans{240 \text{ kg}}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Trading cards: $7.35$ dollars shared by three friends}

\textbf{Solution:}
(a) $7.35 \div 3$: share the wholes ($7 \div 3 = 2$ with $1$ left), trade the
leftover dollar for tenths: $13$ tenths $\div\, 3 = 4$ tenths with $1$ tenth
left, trade for hundredths: $15 \div 3 = 5$. Each friend pays \ans{2.45}
dollars.

(b) Compare place by place: $2.45$ has $4$ tenths, $2.50$ has $5$ tenths, so
\ans{2.45 < 2.50}

\vspace{0.2cm}\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\vspace{0.1cm}

\problem{Challenge: milk at $1.2$ dollars per liter, $2.5$ liters, $5$-dollar
bill}

\textbf{Solution:}
(a) Cost $= 1.2 \times 2.5$. As whole numbers $12 \times 25 = 300$; the
factors have two decimal places between them, so the cost is $3.00$ dollars.
\ans{3}

(b) Change $= 5 - 3.00$, so Priya gets \ans{2} dollars back.
