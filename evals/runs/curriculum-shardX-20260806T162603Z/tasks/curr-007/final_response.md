# Ten-Frames: See Numbers to Ten (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **ws_tenframes_curr007.pdf** — a 10-problem fluency worksheet on recognizing
  structured quantities to 10 on ten-frames. The ramp opens with a worked
  pattern ("full top row is 5, count on"), then rotates three frame skills so
  no two problems share an identical skeleton: five-and-more frames,
  almost-ten frames read from their empty boxes, and empty frames the child
  builds a number on by drawing dots.
- **ak_tenframes_curr007.pdf** — the step-by-step answer key: every solution
  writes out the frame shortcut (5 + 3 = 8, or 10 − 1 = 9) rather than a bare
  answer, with a quick-answers bank and a generated Curriculum section
  (K.CC.B.5, difficulty 1–4).
- **ss_tenframes_curr007.pdf** — a one-page study guide teaching the three
  frame skills with a rule, worked example, and try-it each, plus a watch-out
  on choosing whether to count up from five or down from ten.

**What is verified:** 19 of the 20 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every bottom-row count, empty-box count, row split, and total has its own
labeled entry, checked as the five-and-more sum or ten-minus-empties
difference the frame teaches. **One item is instructor-judged:** the challenge
asks the child to tell how the frame helps them see 9 fast, and the key's
rubric accepts either frame shortcut in the child's own words. The study
guide's examples and try-its are machine-verified the same way.
