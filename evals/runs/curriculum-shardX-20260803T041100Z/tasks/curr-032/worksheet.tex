\wstitleblock{Teen Numbers: Numerals and Number Words}{Kindergarten--Grade 1}{}

\noindent\small
Teen numbers as one ten and some ones, number words to numerals, numerals to
number words, and comparing teen numbers.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Say each number out loud before you write it.
  \item A filled row of ten dots is \textbf{one ten}. Count the extra dots as \textbf{ones}.
  \item Number words you may need: eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen,
        sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize
