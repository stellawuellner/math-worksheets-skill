Comparing numerals with symbols, choosing greater or less, ordering three
numerals, and counting on before comparing
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{How to compare two numerals.} Count up out loud: $1, 2, 3,
\ldots$ The numeral you say \emph{later} is the \textbf{greater} one. The open
end of the symbol always faces the greater numeral.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Worked pattern.} Compare $2$ and $5$. Counting up you say $2$
first and $5$ later, so $5$ is greater and $2$ is less:
$\;\boldsymbol{2 < 5}$. \quad Read it aloud: ``two is less than five.''
