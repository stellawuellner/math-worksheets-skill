\wstitleblock{Exponential Growth and Decay Practice}{Algebra 1}{}

\noindent\small
Evaluating exponential functions, graphing them from a table of values, and
using growth and decay models.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item An exponential function has the form $f(x) = a \cdot b^{x}$: the
        \emph{starting value} $a$ is multiplied by the \emph{base} $b$ once for
        every step of $x$. Raise the base to the power \emph{first}, then
        multiply by $a$.
  \item A negative exponent means divide: $b^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{b^{n}}$.
  \item Show the substitution and the power before you write the final value.
  \item Where a grid is given, plot your points and join them with a smooth
        curve --- exponential graphs are never straight lines.
  \item Round money answers to the nearest cent and people or animals to a
        whole number, as each problem says.
\end{itemize}
\normalsize

\problem[7cm]{Two accounts are opened on the same day. Account A starts with
$1200$ dollars and grows by $5$ percent each year, so it is worth
$1200 \cdot (1.05)^{t}$ after $t$ years. Account B starts with $900$ dollars
and grows by $9$ percent each year, so it is worth $900 \cdot (1.09)^{t}$.
Find the value of each account after $10$ years, in dollars, rounded to the
nearest cent, and state which account is worth more.}
