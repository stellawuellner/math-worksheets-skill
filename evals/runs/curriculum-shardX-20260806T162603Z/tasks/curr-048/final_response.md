# Where Is It? Shapes on the Toy Shelf (K/Grade 1, position words)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_position_curr048.pdf`) — 8 problems built around one
  realistic toy-shelf scene (drawn once, used by every problem, with the
  shelf order stated in words so the givens are explicit). The child counts
  shapes in named places (top, bottom, between, next to), names the shape at
  a position (below the triangle, right of the small circle), compares shelf
  counts, reasons about a shape moving shelves, produces a position word,
  and finally gives directions to a friend using two position words.
- **Answer key** (`ak_position_curr048.pdf`) — the reasoning for each count,
  the accepted shape name or position word for every open part, and a
  quick-answers column.
- **Study guide** (`ss_position_curr048.pdf`) — three skills: count the
  shapes in one place (the "between" subtraction), name the shape at a
  position by its sides and corners, and use position words yourself, plus
  a watch-out on left/right.

**What is verified:** 7 of the 14 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every count, the shelf comparison, and the move-a-shape arithmetic. The 7
open responses (shape names at a position, the fill-in position word, and
the two-word direction sentence) are word answers, flagged for instructor
judgement with accepted answers listed in the key. In the study guide, the
position-word example and try-it are likewise marked instructor-judged; the
other four are machine-checked.
