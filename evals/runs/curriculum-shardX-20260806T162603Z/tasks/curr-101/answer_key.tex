\problem{The place-value chart shows $5{,}872$. What is the value of the digit 8?}

\textbf{Solution:} The chart puts the 8 in the \emph{hundreds} column. The value
of a digit is the digit times its place: $8 \times 100 = 800$. \ans{800}

\problem{What is the value of the digit 6 in $46{,}205$?}

\textbf{Solution:} Reading places from the right: 5 ones, 0 tens, 2 hundreds,
6 thousands, 4 ten-thousands. The 6 sits in the thousands place, so its value is
$6 \times 1{,}000 = 6{,}000$. \ans{6000}

\problem{Write the number in standard form: $30{,}000 + 7{,}000 + 400 + 9$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Each addend fills one place: 3 ten-thousands, 7 thousands,
4 hundreds, 0 tens (no tens are listed, so a zero holds that place), 9 ones.
$30{,}000 + 7{,}000 + 400 + 9 = 37{,}409$. \ans{37409}

\problem{Write in standard form: sixty-two thousand, four hundred eighteen.}

\textbf{Solution:} ``Sixty-two thousand'' is $62{,}000$; ``four hundred'' is
$400$; ``eighteen'' is $18$. Adding the parts: $62{,}000 + 400 + 18 = 62{,}418$.
Note the zero holding the empty hundreds--tens boundary is built in when we add.
\ans{62418}

\problem{Write $<$, $>$, or $=$: \ $46{,}205 \;\square\; 46{,}502$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Both numbers have five digits, and the ten-thousands (4) and
thousands (6) match. Compare hundreds: $2 < 5$, so the left number is smaller.
\ans{46{,}205 < 46{,}502}

\problem{Write $<$, $>$, or $=$: \ $72{,}341 \;\square\; 72{,}314$.}

\textbf{Solution:} Ten-thousands, thousands, and hundreds all match (7, 2, 3).
Compare tens: $4 > 1$, so the left number is greater. \ans{72{,}341 > 72{,}314}

\problem{Order from least to greatest: $23{,}450$; $23{,}045$; $23{,}504$.}

\textbf{Solution:} All three share $23$ thousands--ten-thousands... compare the
hundreds place: $0 < 4 < 5$. So $23{,}045$ is least, then $23{,}450$, then
$23{,}504$. \ans{23{,}045,\ 23{,}450,\ 23{,}504}

\problem{In $44{,}175$, the ten-thousands 4 is how many times the thousands 4?}

\textbf{Solution:} The first 4 is worth $40{,}000$ and the second is worth
$4{,}000$. Each place is worth 10 times the place to its right, and these are
neighboring places: $40{,}000 \div 4{,}000 = 10$. \ans{10}

\problem{Maya says $9{,}876 > 12{,}345$. Correct symbol, and what did she forget?}

\textbf{Solution:} Count digits first: $9{,}876$ has four digits (thousands at
most) while $12{,}345$ has five (it reaches the ten-thousands), so $12{,}345$ is
automatically greater. Maya compared the leading digits 9 and 1 without first
lining up the place values. \ans{9{,}876 < 12{,}345}

A full-credit explanation says that you compare the \emph{number of digits} (or
the greatest place) before comparing any digits, and that a five-digit number
always beats a four-digit number.

\problem{Saturday: $68{,}742$ fans; Sunday: $68{,}472$ fans. (a) Greater day?
(b) How many more?}

\textbf{Solution:} (a) Same ten-thousands and thousands (6, 8); compare
hundreds: $7 > 4$, so Saturday's $68{,}742$ is greater:
\ans{68{,}742 > 68{,}472\ \text{(Saturday)}}

(b) Subtract: $68{,}742 - 68{,}472 = 270$. (The thousands and ten-thousands
cancel; $742 - 472 = 270$.) \ans{270}
