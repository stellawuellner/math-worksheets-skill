\wstitleblock{Counting On to Compare Two Sets}{Kindergarten--Grade 1 \quad Counting and cardinality}{}

\begin{center}
\textit{Counting on to find how many, comparing two sets, and finding how many
more}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{How to count on.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
\item A full row of a frame is $5$. A full frame is $10$. You do not have to
count a full frame again --- start from its number.
\item Then touch the extra things one at a time and keep saying the next
number. The last number you say tells how many.
\item To compare, finish \emph{both} counts first. Then the bigger number wins.
$>$ and $<$ always open toward the bigger number.
\item ``How many more?'' means: start at the smaller number and count on until
you reach the bigger one. Count how many number words you said.
\end{itemize}
