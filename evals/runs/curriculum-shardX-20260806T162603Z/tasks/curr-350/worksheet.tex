\wstitleblock{Planning Constructions and Justifying Them}{High-school geometry}{}

\begin{center}
{\small\textit{Planning classical compass-straightedge constructions and justifications}}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item A \emph{plan} is a numbered list of compass and straightedge moves precise
    enough that someone else could carry it out without guessing. Say where each
    compass point goes and whether the span changes.
  \item A \emph{justification} says why the finished figure has the property
    claimed. Equal radii are the raw material: they give congruent segments, and
    congruent segments give congruent triangles.
  \item Measuring with a ruler or protractor is not a construction move. Neither
    is "draw a perpendicular" --- that is the thing to be constructed.
  \item The problems mix planning, justifying and coordinate checking on purpose.
    Decide which the question is asking for before you start writing.
\end{itemize}

\problem[8cm]{In the copy-an-angle construction you strike one arc across both
