\wstitleblock{Standard, Word, and Expanded Form to 1,000}{Grade 2--3 Mathematics}{}

\noindent\small
Standard form, expanded form, number names, and comparing numbers written in
different forms. Use these three ways of writing the same number:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Standard form} --- the digits: $546$
  \item \textbf{Word form} --- the number name: five hundred forty-six
  \item \textbf{Expanded form} --- the value of each digit added up: $500 + 40 + 6$
\end{itemize}
Say the number out loud first. A digit's value depends on its place, so the
$5$ in $546$ is worth $500$, not $5$. When a place has a zero, that place adds
nothing --- leave it out of the expanded form.
\normalsize

\vspace{0.2cm}

ninety-three? Write the two numbers in standard form, then write them with the

form.}
