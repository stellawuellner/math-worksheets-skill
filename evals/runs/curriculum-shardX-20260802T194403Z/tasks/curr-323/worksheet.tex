\noindent\textbf{How the two angles are measured.} Both are measured from the
\emph{horizontal}, never from the vertical. The reference diagram below shows
the labelling every problem on this sheet uses. \emph{No values are shown --- use
the numbers given in each problem.}

\noindent Draw and label the right triangle before you compute. State the ratio
you are using, and round each answer as the problem asks. Keep your calculator in
degree mode.

\problem{A surveyor stands on level ground $40$ ft from the base of a tree. The
angle of elevation from the ground at her feet to the top of the tree is
$32^\circ$. How tall is the tree, in feet, to the nearest hundredth of a foot?
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{ft}}

\problem{From the top of a lighthouse $85$ m above sea level, the angle of
depression to a boat is $12^\circ$. How far is the boat from the foot of the
lighthouse, measured along the water, to the nearest tenth of a metre?
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{m}}

\problem{A wheelchair ramp rises $3$ ft over a horizontal run of $20$ ft. Find
the angle of elevation of the ramp, in degrees, to the nearest tenth of a
degree.
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{degrees}}

\problem{A surveyor's eye is $45$ m horizontally from the base of a tower, and
the top of the tower is $24$ m above her eye level. Let $\theta$ be the angle of
elevation from her eye to the top of the tower. Write $\tan\theta$ as a fraction
in lowest terms. (Do not find $\theta$.)
\par\vspace*{4.5cm}\ansline}

\problem{A kite is flying at the end of a taut string $150$ ft long. The string
makes an angle of elevation of $28^\circ$ with the ground. How high above the
ground is the kite, in feet, to the nearest hundredth of a foot? (Assume the
string is held at ground level.)
\par\vspace*{5cm}\answerline{ft}}

\problem{A plane is cruising at an altitude of $3200$ ft. The pilot measures the
angle of depression to an airport ahead as $15^\circ$. How far is the airport
from the point directly beneath the plane, measured along the ground, to the
nearest foot?
\par\vspace*{5.5cm}\answerline{ft}}

\problem{A student whose eye is $1.6$ m above the ground stands $12$ m from the
foot of a flagpole on level ground. The angle of elevation from her eye to the
top of the flagpole is $38^\circ$. Find the height of the flagpole above the
ground, in metres, to the nearest hundredth of a metre. (The extra measurement
is not decoration --- decide where it belongs.)
\par\vspace*{6cm}\answerline{m}}

\problem{A ranger stands at the edge of a cliff $50$ m above the sea and looks
out at two boats directly in line with the cliff. The angle of depression to the
nearer boat is $35^\circ$ and to the farther boat is $20^\circ$. How far apart
are the two boats, in metres, to the nearest hundredth of a metre?
\par\vspace*{7cm}\answerline{m}}
