# Count-On Detectives (Kindergarten–Grade 1)

Three PDFs are ready:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_counton_curr024.pdf`) — 6 problems on finding and fixing
  the classic count-on error (saying the start number as the first hop, which
  lands one short). After a warm-up that models the correct count, there are
  four find-and-fix items — Jen, Kim, Raj and Ava each show a planted wrong
  count with its one-short answer — and a challenge asking why saying the
  start number first prevents the mistake.
- **Answer key** (`ak_counton_curr024.pdf`) — every solution shows the correct
  count-on sequence, a "Common wrong answers" note derived from the planted
  traps (so a grader instantly recognizes the one-short answers 7, 8, 9, 8),
  a quick-answers bank, and a curriculum section (K.OA.A, difficulty 1–4).
- **Study guide** (`ss_counton_curr024.pdf`) — three sections: counting on the
  right way, spotting the one-short mistake, and fix-it-and-check, each with
  a rule box, worked example, and fresh try-it, plus a fingers-per-hop
  watch-out.

**What is verified:** 6 of the 11 responses are machine-checked with SymPy —
every corrected sum on every answer line. All four planted wrong results are
declared as misconception traps and machine-proven to be rejected by their
problem's own check (each is exactly one short). The 5 explanation responses
("tell why the count went wrong", "is she right", etc.) are genuinely open:
they are flagged for instructor review, and the answer key states what a
correct explanation must contain. The key's "What is verified" note reflects
the same split.
