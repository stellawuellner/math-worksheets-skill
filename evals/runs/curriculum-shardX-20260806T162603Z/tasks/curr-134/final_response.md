Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 5 fraction worksheet:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_fracmd_curr134.pdf`) — 8 problems on multiplying and
  dividing fractions, built as an "error detective" sheet: routine multiply and
  divide practice ramping into two find-and-fix problems (Jordan flips the
  wrong fraction; Ava cross-multiplies like a proportion), a whole-number ÷
  fraction word problem, a measurement division word problem, and a
  mixed-number challenge.
- **Answer key** (`ak_fracmd_curr134.pdf`) — full worked reasoning for every
  problem, a quick-answers bank for fast grading, and a "common wrong answers"
  note for each of the 8 declared misconception traps (e.g. "If they got 0.8:
  flipped the first fraction instead of the second"), so a wrong answer tells
  you *which* mistake was made.
- **Study guide** (`ss_fracmd_curr134.pdf`) — 2 pages, three skills (multiply
  straight across; keep–change–flip; spot-the-mistake checklist), each with a
  rule box, a worked example, and a try-it with the answer printed upside down.

**What is verified:** 8 of the 10 checks are machine-verified with SymPy —
every computational answer on the sheet, all 6 study-guide examples/try-its,
and all 8 misconception traps (each planted wrong method is proven to give a
visibly different number from the correct answer). The 2 remaining items are
the written "name the error" explanations in problems 4(a) and 6(a); those are
genuinely open responses, so they are flagged for instructor judgment, and the
answer key states what a correct explanation must contain. The key's own "What
is verified" note reflects the same split.
