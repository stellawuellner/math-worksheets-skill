Here are the three PDFs for the Grade 6 set on tape diagrams and double number
lines:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_tape_dnl_curr153.pdf`) — 10 problems, every one built
  around a drawn representation: reading a tape diagram, completing double
  number lines (hose, recipe, price, cyclist), sharing a total in a ratio,
  difference problems ("Ana has 12 more"), one problem where the student
  finishes drawing the tape diagram themselves, and a final problem that asks
  for the answer plus one sentence on how the diagram helped. Units and givens
  are stated explicitly in every stem.
- **Answer key** (`ak_tape_dnl_curr153.pdf`) — quick-answers table, generated
  curriculum section (6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3, difficulty 1–4), and worked
  solutions that follow the diagram reasoning (find one box, count boxes),
  plus the three declared common wrong answers (dividing by the number of
  people, treating the difference as one box, reporting the long piece instead
  of the difference).
- **Study guide** (`ss_tape_dnl_curr153.pdf`) — three sections: sharing a
  total with a tape diagram, difference problems, and reading a double number
  line; each has a rule box, worked example, and a try-it with the answer
  upside down.

**What is verified:** 15 of the 16 responses on the worksheet are
machine-checked with SymPy, including every double-number-line blank (each has
its own labelled check) and the three misconception traps. One response is
instructor-judged: the one-sentence explanation on problem 10, and the key
states what a full-credit sentence must contain. All six study-guide answers
are machine-checked.
