\wstitleblock{Evaluating Piecewise and Absolute-Value Functions}{Algebra 1}{Absolute-value values, choosing the right piecewise rule, and working backwards to the input}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item $|a|$ is the \textbf{distance} of $a$ from zero, so it is never negative: $|{-5}| = 5$. Work out what is inside the bars first, then take the distance, then do everything outside.
  \item A \textbf{piecewise} function is several rules with a condition on each. Before you compute anything, check which condition your input satisfies --- that is the whole skill.
  \item Watch the difference between $<$ and $\le$. Exactly one rule may claim a boundary value.
  \item Show the substitution, not just the answer: $f(7) = |7-3| + 2 = 4 + 2 = 6$.
\end{itemize}
