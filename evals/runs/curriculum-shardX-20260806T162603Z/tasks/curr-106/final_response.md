# Area Models for Two-Digit Multiplication — Grades 4–5

Here are the three PDFs for this concept-models set:

- **Worksheet** (`ws_areamodel_curr106.pdf`) — 10 problems using area models
  for two-digit multiplication. Every problem carries a model: the first two
  are fully labeled (14 × 23 and 26 × 31, with a blank for each of the four
  cells and the total), and the rest give an empty model your child labels and
  fills. The set covers splitting factors into tens and ones, computing
  partial products, adding cells to the total (including a 34 × 26 tiles
  application), estimating by rounding before computing exactly, a
  missing-side model to reason backward through (240 and 72 cells), an
  "Elena" error item where the classic dropped-cell mistake (900 instead of
  990) must be explained, and a 63 × 47 challenge.
- **Answer key** (`ak_areamodel_curr106.pdf`) — quick-answers bank, then
  cell-by-cell worked solutions for every model. Elena's planted 900 is
  declared as a machine-checked misconception trap, so the key prints a
  "common wrong answers" note for it. Curriculum section: 4.NBT.B.5.
- **Study guide** (`ss_areamodel_curr106.pdf`) — four sections matching the
  four subskills: build the model, find each partial product, add the cells,
  and estimate to check — each with a rule, worked example, and try-it.

**What is verified:** 28 of the 30 responses are machine-checked — every
partial product, every total, both splits, the estimate, and the backward
missing-side computation; the planted wrong answer in the Elena problem is
machine-verified to be distinguishably wrong. Two responses are
instructor-judged and flagged for manual review: the student-drawn model in
problem 4 (rubric: the four correct cells are listed in the key) and the
written explanation in problem 9. The key's "What is verified" note reflects
the same split.
