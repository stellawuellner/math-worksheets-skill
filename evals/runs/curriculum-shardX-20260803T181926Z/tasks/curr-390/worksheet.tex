{\large Systems from context, matrix equations, and nonlinear systems}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{Directions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.7cm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Define your variables before you write any equation --- say what each letter counts or measures.
  \item Show the system (or the matrix equation $AX = B$) you set up, then solve it.
  \item Check every answer in \emph{both} original equations before you write it on the answer line.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.15cm}

\problem[6cm]{A school play sells adult tickets at \$8 and student tickets at \$5.
On opening night 120 tickets were sold and \$825 was collected.
Let $a$ be the number of adult tickets and $s$ the number of student tickets sold.
Write a system of equations and find how many of each kind were sold.}

\problem{A lab mixes a 20\% acid solution with a 50\% acid solution to make 12 L
of a 30\% acid solution. Let $x$ be the number of litres of the 20\% solution and
$y$ the number of litres of the 50\% solution. Write a system and find how much
of each solution is needed.
\par\vspace*{6cm}\answerline{L}}

\problem[7cm]{A print shop bills two orders. Order one: 3 boxes of pens and 2 boxes
of markers cost \$16. Order two: 4 boxes of pens and 5 boxes of markers cost \$33.
Let $x$ be the price of a box of pens and $y$ the price of a box of markers, in dollars.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(a) Write the two orders as one matrix equation $AX = B$ and compute $\det A$.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(b) Solve the matrix equation for $x$ and $y$.}

\problem[7cm]{A tour company seats groups in vans and buses. 2 vans and 3 buses
seat 96 passengers; 5 vans and 2 buses seat 108 passengers. Let $x$ be the number
of seats in a van and $y$ the number of seats in a bus.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(a) Write the coefficient matrix $A$ and compute $\det A$.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(b) Solve for $x$ and $y$ using Cramer's rule.}

\problem[7cm]{A drone climbs along the curve $y = x^2 - 4x + 7$ while a support
cable runs along the line $y = 2x - 1$, where $x$ and $y$ are both measured in metres
from the base of the tower. Find every point where the drone's path meets the cable.}

\problem[6cm]{A kayaker paddles 12 km downstream in 2 hours, then paddles the same
12 km back upstream in 3 hours. Let $k$ be the kayak's speed in still water and $c$
the speed of the current, both in km/h. Write a system and find both speeds.}

\problem[7cm]{A workshop builds chairs and tables. Each chair takes 4 hours of
cutting and 6 hours of finishing; each table takes 3 hours of cutting and 2 hours
of finishing. This week exactly 27 cutting hours and 28 finishing hours are used.
Let $x$ be the number of chairs and $y$ the number of tables.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(a) Write the plan as a matrix equation and compute $\det A$.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(b) Solve for $x$ and $y$.}

\problem[7cm]{A survey drone flies along the line $y = x + 1$. A circular restricted
zone has boundary $x^2 + y^2 = 25$, with both coordinates in kilometres. Find every
point where the flight path crosses the boundary of the zone.}

\problem[6cm]{A family invests \$4000 in two accounts. One account pays 5\% simple
interest per year and the other pays 8\% per year. After one year the two accounts
together have earned \$275 in interest. Let $x$ be the amount invested at the lower
rate and $y$ the amount invested at the higher rate, in dollars. How much went into
each account?}

\problem[8cm]{A caterer files two order sheets for the same event. Sheet one: 2 trays
of sandwiches and 3 trays of fruit, for 12 servings. Sheet two: 4 trays of sandwiches
and 6 trays of fruit, for 30 servings. Let $x$ be the servings in a sandwich tray and
$y$ the servings in a fruit tray.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(a) Compute $\det A$ for the coefficient matrix of this system.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(b) Solve the system, or show that it has no solution.
\par\vspace{2pt}
(c) Explain what your determinant tells you about whether both order sheets can be correct.}

\problem{A rectangular community garden is enclosed by 34 m of edging and covers
70 m$^2$ of ground. Find the two side lengths of the garden.
\par\vspace*{7cm}\answerline{m}}

\problem[9cm]{A concert sells adult tickets at \$12, student tickets at \$8 and
child tickets at \$5. In all, 200 tickets were sold for a total of \$1800, and the
number of student tickets sold was exactly 2 times the number of child tickets.
Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ be the numbers of adult, student and child tickets. Write a
three-variable system and find how many of each were sold.}
